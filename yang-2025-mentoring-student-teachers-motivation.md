# Mentoring for Motivation: Supporting Student Teachers' Basic Psychological Needs in Chinese Public Kindergarten Internships

Yang Y, Chen Y. Frontiers in Psychology, 2025.

## Summary

A qualitative case study of nine senior early childhood student teachers on internships, using semi-structured interviews and thematic analysis framed by self-determination theory. The authors describe mentors meeting student teachers' needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness through graduated responsibility, scaffolded tasks with feedback, and both professional collaboration and emotional care. They propose a model of mentoring as a cycle of assessing readiness, setting suitably challenging tasks, analysing performance and adjusting support.

## Why It Matters

Ewan read it during the 2025-26 autumn term when the trainee was doing well and he was wondering how fast to hand over classes. The "assess readiness, then calibrate the challenge" cycle is close to what [[co-planning]] does week by week, and it fed the staged handover in [[yusuf-first-half-term-plan]].

## Caveats

Nine participants, kindergarten internships, one country. A framework for thinking about mentoring, not evidence about secondary placements.
