# Trip Places Equity Analysis

My analysis of 1 December 2025 of what a 95% attendance threshold would do to Tarnholm places. Written for the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]] after [[adeel-chaudhry]] proposed the threshold at the [[slt-meeting-2025-11-19]].

## Question

If only applicants above 95% attendance could go on the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]], who would be left out?

## Data

- 61 applications received by the 14 November deadline for 52 places.
- Attendance year to date at 28 November 2025 from [[ledgerwise]], extracted by [[marek-nowicki]].
- Bursary eligibility as flagged for the trip by [[olumide-bankole]] (eligible for free school meals or meeting the school's hardship criteria). 17 of the 61 applicants were eligible.
- Worked on aggregates only. The pupil-level sheet was deleted after the meeting; this note keeps counts.

## Findings

> [!info]
> A 95% threshold would exclude 23 of 61 applicants. 14 of the 23 are bursary-eligible: 14 of the 17 bursary-eligible applicants would lose their chance of a place, against 9 of the 44 others.

| Threshold | Applicants excluded | Of whom bursary-eligible | Bursary applicants still eligible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| None | 0 | 0 | 17 of 17 |
| 90% | 9 | 6 | 11 of 17 |
| 92% | 15 | 10 | 7 of 17 |
| 95% | 23 | 14 | 3 of 17 |

- Of the 23 below 95%, authorised illness made up most of the gap for 15. A threshold cannot tell a fortnight of flu from truancy.
- 5 of the 23 were in the huddle's caseload of [[persistent-absence|persistently absent]] pupils. The threshold would have hit them, but they were a minority of those it excluded.
- With the threshold, 38 applicants would remain for 52 places: the trip would run 14 places under capacity at a higher per-pupil cost, or open places to non-applicants.

## Options Considered

1. **95% threshold** (Adeel's proposal). Excludes mostly bursary-eligible pupils and pupils with authorised illness. Undersubscribes the trip.
2. **Lower threshold, 90%.** Still excludes 6 of 17 bursary applicants.
3. **First come, first served.** Rewards families who check email at 07:00. Rejected.
4. **Lottery with a [[behaviour-agreement]].** Same chance for every applicant; the condition is about conduct on the trip, which is what staff actually need. Recommended.

## Outcome

Option 4 adopted on 3 December. 52 places drawn on 12 December; 9 applicants on a waiting list. Of the 23 who would have been excluded, 20 got places. See [[lottery-allocation]] and [[trip-places-should-not-be-earned-by-attendance]].

## Framing

The two tests come from [[educational-equity]]: who a rule shuts out, and whether the trip stays open to the whole year group. A random draw is the answer public life uses for the same problem ([[sortition]]).

## Caveats

- Year-to-date attendance at the end of November covers only 12 weeks; a single illness moves a pupil several points.
- Bursary eligibility for the trip is a school flag, not a full measure of family income.
- Marek checked the figures against Ledgerwise on 2 December. Two late code corrections moved one applicant above 95% and another below it, so the totals stood.
