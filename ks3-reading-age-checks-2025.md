# KS3 Reading Age Checks 2025

[[bethan-lloyd-hughes]]'s reading checks on the five Year 7 booklets before first printing, August 2025.

## Summary

- Booklets 1, 3 and 5 within range for most of Year 7.
- Booklets 2 and 4 pitched about two years above the average Year 7 reading age in their narrative texts.
- Common issues: long sentences in source introductions, unexplained words such as 'feudal' and 'tithe'.

## Why It Matters

We printed anyway because of time and rewrote 2 and 4 a year later ([[history-department-meeting-2026-07-08]]). Lesson: do the reading check three weeks before printing, not one ([[plan-an-enquiry-unit]]).
