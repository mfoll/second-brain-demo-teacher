# An Immersive Field Trip Focused on Beef Production Increases the Sense of Belonging in Ethnoracial Minority College Students

Drewery ML, Cooper JV, Waliczek TM, Wickersham TA. *Translational Animal Science*, 2023.

## Summary

21 university students took a five-day industry tour and completed the same questionnaire before and after. Students from ethnoracial minority groups started with a lower sense of belonging in the discipline than White students; after the tour their belonging rose to about the same level, while White students' scores did not change. Imposter tendencies did not change for anyone.

## Why It Matters

I found it while writing the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] in late November 2025. It is a long way from a Year 10 residential, but it points the same way as my own instinct: the students who start furthest from feeling they belong may gain most from an immersive trip. That is an argument against filtering out pupils with weaker attendance ([[should-trip-places-depend-on-attendance]]). I mentioned it in one sentence at the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]] and no more, given the sample.

## Caveats

- 21 university students, no control group, a single trip.
- Belonging in an academic discipline is not belonging at school.
