# Merrow Exemplar Scripts

The board's annotated exemplar answers that we standardise on, downloaded from the Merrow centre portal and kept by series.

## Summary

- Medicine Through Time: 24 exemplars across all question types, including six 16-mark answers covering Levels 1 to 4.
- Migration, Empire and People: only the specimen mark scheme; no Level 4 exemplars yet.
- Weimar, Elizabethan and Conflict and Tension: full sets.

## Why It Matters

Step 2 of [[run-a-blind-double-marking-sample]]. The one-sided but detailed Level 2 answer in the Medicine set is the single most useful script we have; it shows what we were over-marking in 2025.

## Caveats

Missing Migration exemplars weaken standardisation for the pilot classes ([[migration-resources-tracker]]).
