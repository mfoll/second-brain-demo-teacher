# AI In Education

Exploration cluster on using AI tools in my teaching, mainly for drafting feedback on essays. Nothing here is approved practice; the trust has not finished its data protection impact assessment.

## Current position

> [!info]
> On 30 September 2026 the trust DPO, Paul Ingram, said no pupil work may go into an unapproved AI tool until the trust DPIA is finished, expected December 2026. I mark the November mocks by hand and trial comparative judgement instead. Open in [[can-we-use-ai-to-draft-feedback-on-mock-essays]].

## What I wanted to try

Drafting whole-class feedback from a set of 28 mock essays: common misconceptions, missing knowledge, typical structure errors. Not marks. My stance is in [[ai-feedback-is-a-workload-tool-not-a-marking-tool]].
