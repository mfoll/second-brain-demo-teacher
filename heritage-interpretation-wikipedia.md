# Heritage interpretation

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Explains heritage interpretation as the way museums, parks and heritage sites help visitors understand the meaning of places, objects and stories, through education, communication and storytelling that connect people, environment and the past.

## Why It Matters

Read while planning the March 2027 ropewalk site walk with [[luis-ortega]] ([[local-history-enquiry]]). The idea that a site's signage tells visitors what to think gives Year 9 a question to ask of the canal-side interpretation boards.

## Caveats

Written from the point of view of practitioners; little on critique of interpretation, which is what I want pupils to do.
