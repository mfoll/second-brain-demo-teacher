# Mentorship

English Wikipedia article on mentorship.

## Summary

Defines mentorship as guidance given by someone with relevant experience to a less experienced person, influencing their personal and professional growth. Notes that mentors are usually but not necessarily more senior; what counts is experience others can learn from.

## Why It Matters

Read before taking on the first trainee in 2025. The point that seniority is not the qualification stuck: Yusuf Demir knows more about handling objects with groups than Ewan does, and the 2026-27 placement treats that as something to learn from rather than correct.

## Caveats

General and organisational. Says little specific about mentoring teachers; [[teacher-education-wikipedia]] and the Esterbrook handbooks cover that.
