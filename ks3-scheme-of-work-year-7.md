# KS3 Scheme Of Work Year 7

The Year 7 scheme of work for the new curriculum: two timeline lessons, then five enquiries at one lesson a week.

## Summary

Lesson-by-lesson sequence, the assessed task and descriptor for each enquiry, quiz schedule, and homework (one Quillyard quiz a fortnight). 34 lessons including two assessment lessons.

## Why It Matters

What a cover teacher or a trainee opens first. Matches [[ks3-curriculum-map-2026-27]].
