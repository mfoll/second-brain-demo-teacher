# Attendance Letter Template 2024

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 20 October 2025 by the [[attendance-letter-template-2025]]. Kept for comparison in the Middle Leadership Programme write-up.

The school-wide stage 1 attendance letter used up to October 2025. It opened with the legal duty of parents and the possibility of penalties, gave the attendance figure in the third paragraph, and named no member of staff to contact. Families I spoke to in my first weeks as head of year described it as "a fine notice". The rewrite kept the legal content but moved it to the end ([[year-10-attendance-drive]]).
