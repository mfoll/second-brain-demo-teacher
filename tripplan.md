# TripPlan

The Wyvernmoor Learning Trust's system for educational visit approval and risk assessment. Every visit leaves an audit trail in it: purpose, staffing, risk assessment, approvals, and the participant list with medical and consent forms.

## Summary

The trip leader creates the visit, attaches the risk assessment and budget, and submits it. The educational visits coordinator ([[rebecca-tull]]) reviews and approves; residentials then go to the governors' visits committee. Families complete medical, dietary and consent forms through a linked form, visible to the leader and the trip first aider only.

## Why It Matters

The [[tarnholm-residential-2026]] went through TripPlan between November 2025 and January 2026, and [[tarnholm-residential-2027]] goes in by 25 November 2026. Everything about individual pupils on a trip (medical needs, dietary needs, emergency contacts) lives here and in Ledgerwise, not in the vault ([[plan-a-residential-visit]]).

## Key Details

- Approval levels: day visit (EVC), residential (EVC plus governors), overseas (EVC, governors, trust).
- The risk assessment template follows the [[trust-educational-visits-policy]]. Centre risk assessments are attached, not re-typed.
- The participant list exports to a printed emergency sheet; the leader and deputy each carry one on the trip, returned and shredded afterwards.
