# Inquiry-based learning

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes inquiry-based learning as active learning that starts from questions, problems or scenarios rather than the teacher presenting facts, with the teacher as facilitator. Learners identify and research issues to build knowledge or solutions. The article places problem-based learning within it and links it to thinking and problem-solving skills.

## Why It Matters

Read when the department agreed to rebuild Key Stage 3 around enquiries ([[history-department-meeting-2025-03-05]]). Useful mainly to explain to [[kofi-asamoah]] and governors that a history enquiry is not the open-ended discovery learning the general term suggests: our [[enquiry-question]] is set by the teacher and the knowledge is taught directly.

## Caveats

General education article, not specific to history. Its open-inquiry end is the version Desmond worries about; ours is closer to structured inquiry.
