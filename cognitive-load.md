# Cognitive load

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on cognitive load, the effort being used in working memory, and the instructional-design distinction between intrinsic load (the topic itself), extraneous load (how it is presented) and germane load (building lasting knowledge).

## Why It Matters

Re-read when planning the first migration unit for 10H1. Migration c790-present covers twelve centuries; the intrinsic load is high and the specimen papers came late. I cut the booklet's first draft from 14 pages to 9, removed side boxes and put the timeline on one page ([[10h1-migration-scheme-of-work-autumn-2026]]).

## Caveats

Overview article; the terms are contested in the research. I use them as a planning checklist, not as theory.
