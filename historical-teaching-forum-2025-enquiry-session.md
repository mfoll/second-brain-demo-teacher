# Historical Teaching Forum 2025 Enquiry Session

My notes from the session on designing enquiry questions at the Historical Teaching Forum annual conference on 21 June 2025, chaired by [[ben-akintola]]. Three presenters, all practising heads of history.

## Summary

- An enquiry question should be arguable, answerable with the unit's knowledge and understood by pupils on day one.
- Write the final task first.
- Watch for questions that are topic headings in disguise.
- One presenter's school had abandoned enquiries in Year 7 for a chronological overview year; their reasons were Desmond's.

## Why It Matters

Most of the criteria in [[enquiry-question]] came from here, tested against our own units. The Year 7 overview-year example went into the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25]] discussion.
