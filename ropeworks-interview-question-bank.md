# Ropeworks Voices Question Bank

Ewan's question bank for the interviews, first drafted on 28 April 2025 and revised after RV06. About 60 open questions in six sections: before the works, starting there, the work itself, people and the town, change and closure, after.

## Summary

Questions are prompts, not a script. Each section opens with a broad "tell me about" question and lists follow-ups. The closure section is deliberately last and gentle: "When did you first hear the works might close?" rather than "Why did it close?".

## Why It Matters

Keeps interviews comparable without making them stiff, which matters for the Year 9 enquiry: pupils compare answers to the same prompts across several workers.

## Key Details

- Revision after RV06: removed questions about wages that made two interviewees uneasy; added "What did you make, and where did it go?", which produced some of the best material on ships and farms.
- A pupil version of 12 questions is used in the Forum workshop and the enquiry.
