# Effects of inquiry-based learning on hierarchical cognitive outcomes in tea cultural heritage education: a quasi-experimental study.

Long Y, Tan WH, Weng R, Li X, Frontiers in Psychology, 2026.

## Summary

A quasi-experimental comparison of inquiry-based learning in heritage education, assessing cognitive outcomes at several levels rather than one aggregate score. The authors note that earlier work on inquiry in heritage education was mostly qualitative or non-comparative.

## Why It Matters

Read this week while finishing the ropeworks booklet ([[local-history-enquiry]]). The idea of checking different levels of outcome (recall, interpretation, application) separately is how I want to look at the first teaching: do pupils remember the facts, and can they weigh the testimony?

## Caveats

Different subject, setting and age group; read from the abstract. A quasi-experiment, so groups were not randomised.
