# KS3 Assessment Model Draft

Priyanka's draft Key Stage 3 assessment model, version of 27 February 2026, presented to the faculty on 4 March.

## Summary

- One assessed piece per enquiry, marked on a four-point descriptor for the enquiry's second-order concept: working towards, secure, strong, exceptional.
- Three reporting points a year, descriptor only, no grades before Year 10.
- Moderation: one department meeting per enquiry, five scripts compared.
- Adapted from the Kittow model ([[kittow-ks3-descriptor-model]]).

## Why It Matters

Paused at the [[faculty-meeting-2026-03-04]] until the trust calendar exists.
