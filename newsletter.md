# Newsletter

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines a newsletter as a printed or electronic report on the activities of an organisation, sent to its members, customers, employees or other subscribers. The article covers formats, distribution and the move from print to email.

## Why It Matters

Looked up in September 2025 while setting up the [[year-10-newsletter]], mostly to check terms for the email side (open rates, subscriber lists) before talking to the data lead about sending through Ledgerwise. The newspaper habits I brought matter more than anything here; my working rules are in [[newsletters-should-be-short]].

## Caveats

Generic; nothing on schools or on consent for images of children.
