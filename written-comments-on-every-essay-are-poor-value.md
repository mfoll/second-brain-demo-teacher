# Written Comments On Every Essay Are Poor Value

## Stance

In Key Stage 3, writing an individual comment on every piece of work costs too much time for too little change in what pupils do next. Whole-class feedback with a redraft does more for a third of the time.

## Why I Think This

- The trial numbers: 2 h 40 min down to 55 min a set, and redrafts in 26 of 30 books against 9 of 30 ([[whole-class-feedback-trial-findings]]).
- Most of my old comments repeated the same three points across the set.

## What Would Change My Mind

Evidence that pupils who need most help fall behind under the sheet. So far the book scrutiny has not shown it, but we have not looked at it directly.

## Scope

Not Year 11 essays, where I still add a line per essay ([[does-whole-class-feedback-work-for-year-11-essays]]).
