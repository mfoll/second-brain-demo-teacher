# Effects of distributed leadership on teachers' job satisfaction: The mediating role of teacher collaboration.

Ma L, Gu Y, PLoS One, 2026.

## Summary

Uses the OECD TALIS 2018 teacher survey and regression models to examine how distributed leadership relates to teacher job satisfaction, with teacher collaboration as a mediating route.

## Why It Matters

Read for the Middle Leadership Programme after the May reversal. The department's best decisions this year came from shared work (the moderation protocol, the pilot co-planning with Ruth), the worst from a vote taken before the work existed. Fed my reflection in [[pilot-before-a-whole-department-switch]].

## Caveats

Cross-sectional survey data; associations, not effects of any intervention. International sample, not our trust.
