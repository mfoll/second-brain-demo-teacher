# MLP Project Proposal: Year 10 Attendance

Ewan's one-page project proposal, approved at the programme session of 21 January 2026.

## Summary

Problem: Year 10 attendance of 91.4% in October 2025, with 31 persistently absent pupils. Aim: 94% by the end of the year. Actions already running: tutor first-day calls and a weekly huddle with the pastoral manager and attendance officer. Evidence: weekly year-group figures, the persistently absent count, a tutor survey in June. Leadership challenge: leading eight tutors without line-managing them.

## Why It Matters

The baseline the write-up is judged against. Writing "94%" in a proposal was a mistake in one sense: it became the only number people asked about. See [[leadership-projects-should-report-misses]].
