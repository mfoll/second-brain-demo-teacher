# Attendance Drive Lessons 2025-26

What I would keep, change and stop after the [[year-10-attendance-drive]]. Written on 21 July 2026, first week of the summer holiday, while it was fresh. Feeds the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]].

## Keep

- The weekly huddle, same time, same three people ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]).
- First-day calls by tutors, with Donna as cover ([[first-day-response]]).
- Reporting two numbers: average and persistent absence count ([[persistent-absence]]).
- Attendance shown by tutor group on the Monday slide; never a pupil list on screen.

## Change

- Start in week one, not week six. Done for 2026-27.
- Bring in the Year 9 list at handover ([[year-9-to-10-handover-notes-2026]]).
- Re-launch first-day calls after every holiday, not only after Christmas.
- Split the caseload earlier between drifting pupils (huddle action) and pupils with long-term difficulties (pastoral plan, outside help).

## Stop

- Stage 2 letters for Year 10.
- Tutor group league tables in assembly. Tried in November; two tutors said it shamed groups with a few long-term absent pupils.

## What I Do Not Know

Whether the huddle or the calls made the difference ([[does-first-day-calling-change-attendance]]), and why the spring stalled ([[why-did-year-10-attendance-stall-in-the-spring-term]]).
