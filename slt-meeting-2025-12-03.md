# SLT Meeting 2025-12-03

Senior leadership meeting, Wednesday 3 December 2025, 15:45 to 17:15, head's office. I attended for item 3 only (Tarnholm places), about 25 minutes. Present for the item: [[gillian-mortimer]] (chair), [[adeel-chaudhry]], [[fiona-mcrae]], [[olumide-bankole]] and me.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> The 95% attendance threshold for Tarnholm places was dropped. Places go by lottery among all 61 applicants, each place conditional on a behaviour agreement signed by pupil and parent. The bursary fund plus the Friends' 1,200 GBP grant pays for 11 places.

## Context

At the [[slt-meeting-2025-11-19]], Adeel proposed that only pupils above 95% attendance could have a place on the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]], to make the trip a reward within the attendance push. I asked for a week to model it. The result was the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] of 1 December, circulated with the papers.

## Discussion

- I presented the analysis in five minutes: the threshold would exclude 23 of the 61 applicants; 14 of those 23 were bursary-eligible, out of 17 bursary-eligible applicants in all. For 15 of the 23, authorised illness made up most of the gap, which the threshold cannot tell apart from truancy.
- Adeel said the figures changed his view. His concern had been that the trip would reward pupils the attendance team was working hardest with, while others who came in every day missed out. He accepted that a lottery treats all applicants the same and that the threshold mostly filtered out poorer families.
- Fiona asked that the allocation method should not require anyone to explain absences to win a place. The lottery meets that.
- Olumide confirmed the bursary fund could cover the remaining places once the Friends' grant was in.
- Gillian asked whether a lower threshold (90%) was a middle way. My analysis showed it still excluded 9 applicants, 6 of them bursary-eligible. Not taken up.

## Decisions

1. No attendance threshold for Tarnholm places.
2. Oversubscribed places allocated by lottery ([[lottery-allocation]]), drawn by the educational visits coordinator with a witness from outside the staff.
3. Every place conditional on a [[behaviour-agreement]] signed by pupil and parent or carer, with the trip leader able to withdraw a place for serious breaches between allocation and departure, after a meeting with the family.
4. 11 bursary places: the [[friends-of-corrandale-bursary-grant-2025|Friends' grant]] of 1,200 GBP first, then the school bursary fund.
5. The attendance team may use the trip in positive conversations with pupils, never as a condition.
