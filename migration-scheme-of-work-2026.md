# Migration Scheme Of Work 2026

Ruth's and my scheme for Migration, Empire and People c790-present in the two pilot classes, written July and August 2026.

## Summary

40 lessons across five periods matching booklets 1 to 5, plus the port site study. Factors tracked: economic, political, religious, empire. A 16-mark question at the end of each period, modelled on the April 2026 specimen paper.

Term-by-term detail for 10H1 is in [[10h1-migration-scheme-of-work-autumn-2026]]; each unit opens with two timeline lessons, as at Key Stage 3.

## Why It Matters

The plan the pilot will be judged on. Built after the specimen papers, unlike the February plan ([[thematic-switch-transition-plan-v1]]).
