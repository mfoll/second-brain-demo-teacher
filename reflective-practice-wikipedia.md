# Reflective Practice

English Wikipedia article on reflective practice.

## Summary

Describes reflective practice as the ability to examine one's own thoughts, actions, decisions and assumptions in order to take a critical stance towards improving practice and judgement, challenging automatic reactions and learning continuously from experience.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the check-in of 14 October 2026, when Catriona Dalgleish said section 5 of the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]] listed tasks instead of reflecting on them. The article's emphasis on questioning the assumptions behind everyday actions is the brief for the rewrite: which beliefs about tutors and families did the drive rest on, and which turned out wrong?
