# Merrow Levels Mark Scheme For The 16-Mark Question

The generic Merrow levels mark scheme for the "how far do you agree" essay, with the department's annotations added after the December 2025 moderation.

## Summary

Four levels, four marks each:

- Level 1 (1-4): general answer, simple knowledge.
- Level 2 (5-8): explains one side, or both sides with limited support.
- Level 3 (9-12): explains both sides with support; a judgement may be stated.
- Level 4 (13-16): sustained argument across both sides, reaching a supported judgement about "how far".

The examiner first decides the level from the whole answer, then the mark within it.

## Why It Matters

At the [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|moderation of 10 December 2025]] we had placed detailed one-sided answers in Level 3. The scheme does not allow that: one side caps the answer at Level 2 however much knowledge it shows. Our annotation now says so in red at the top. Every mock round starts with this sheet ([[mark-and-moderate-year-11-mocks]]).

## Caveats

Generic scheme; each paper's indicative content changes. The Migration version has extra expectations on range across periods ([[merrow-migration-specimen-papers-2026]]).
