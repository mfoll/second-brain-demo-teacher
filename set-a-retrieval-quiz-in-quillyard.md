# Set A Retrieval Quiz In Quillyard

How I set the five-question starter quiz for a lesson, in about two minutes. Anyone in the department can follow it.

## Inputs

- The lesson's place in the scheme of work.
- [[retrieval-quiz-bank-index]] in [[quillyard]].

## Steps

1. Open the class page in Quillyard, then `Quizzes > New from bank`.
2. Filter the bank by unit. Pick:
   - 1 question from last lesson;
   - 2 from the last two weeks;
   - 2 from last term or last year (use the "cause" or "sequence" types where possible).
3. Set "one question at a time" and "show answer after each question".
4. Set the quiz to open at the start of the lesson and close after 10 minutes.
5. In class: pupils answer on mini whiteboards or devices, then self-mark from the answers on screen. No names on the board.
6. Spend at most two minutes on the question most got wrong.
7. Once a half-term, export class-level results and note any question above 95% or below 30% correct for rewriting.

## Guardrails

- Results are not recorded in Ledgerwise or reported ([[retrieval-quizzes-should-stay-low-stakes]]).
- Exam classes: one ten-question quiz a fortnight instead of a starter in that lesson ([[how-many-retrieval-questions-per-lesson]]).
- Do not edit shared questions directly; email corrections to me and I update the index.

## Output

A quiz in the class's Quillyard history, and the half-term list of questions to rewrite.
