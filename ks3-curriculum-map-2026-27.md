# KS3 Curriculum Map 2026-27

The 15 Key Stage 3 enquiries as taught from September 2026. Priyanka owns the master copy on the shared drive ([[history-department-shared-drive]]); this is my working copy, checked against hers on 2026-09-02.

Each year opens with two timeline lessons ([[ks3-timeline-lessons-outline]]).

## Year 7 (one lesson a week)

| # | Enquiry | Concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why did William win in 1066? | Causation |
| 2 | How did the Normans keep control? | Change and continuity |
| 3 | How far did the Black Death change life for ordinary people? | Consequence |
| 4 | What can a medieval town's records tell us? | Evidence |
| 5 | Was the break with Rome a revolution? | Significance |

## Year 8 (two lessons a week)

| # | Enquiry | Concept |
