# Wyvernmoor Middle Leadership Programme Handbook

The trust's handbook for the 2025-27 cohort of the Middle Leadership Programme, issued at the first session on 1 October 2025.

## Summary

Six full-day sessions from October 2025 to January 2027, half-termly check-ins with the facilitator, a reading list, and a leadership project. The project must address a real school priority, involve leading people the participant does not line-manage, use evidence of impact, and be written up in 3,000 words with a 10-minute presentation.

## Why It Matters

The project criteria are why the attendance drive was a better project than tutor-time reading: it involved eight tutors, the pastoral manager and the attendance officer, and had data. See [[does-the-attendance-drive-count-as-a-leadership-project]].

## Key Details

Write-up deadline: 4 December 2026. Marking is pass or resubmit, by Catriona Dalgleish and one headteacher from another trust school.
