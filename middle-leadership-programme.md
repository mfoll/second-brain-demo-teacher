# Middle Leadership Programme

The Wyvernmoor Learning Trust's programme for middle leaders: six full-day sessions over a year and a half, a mentor-style facilitator, and a leadership project written up at the end.

## Outcome

Programme completed, with the Year 10 attendance project write-up submitted by 4 December 2026.

## Current State

- Six sessions attended out of six; the last, on presenting projects, is on 20 January 2027.
- Project: the [[year-10-attendance-drive]]. Attendance went from 91.4% in October 2025 to 93.2% by 17 July 2026; persistently absent pupils fell from 31 to 19. The 94% target was missed.
- Write-up draft v2 is about 2,100 of 3,000 words: [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]]. Catriona Dalgleish's feedback on 14 October: the analysis is sound, the reflection section is thin. See [[mlp-check-in-2026-10-14]].
- The missed target stays in the write-up as the headline, not buried: [[leadership-projects-should-report-misses]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Ewan's own programme. Attendance figures are year-group aggregates from the pastoral team's reports; no pupil-level data goes into the write-up or this vault. Colleagues are named only in their role in the drive.

## Working Context

- Cohort of 18 middle leaders from the seven trust schools. Facilitator [[catriona-dalgleish]].
- Session themes: leading change, coaching conversations, data and evidence, leading a team you do not line-manage, workload and wellbeing, presenting impact. Handbook: [[mlp-programme-handbook]].
- Original project idea was tutor-time reading; dropped in January 2026 because the attendance drive was already running and had better data: [[mlp-project-idea-tutor-time]].
- Proposal approved at the session of 21 January 2026: [[mlp-session-2026-01-21]], [[mlp-project-proposal-2026-01]].
- The method problem is attribution. With no comparison group, how much of the 1.8-point rise came from the huddle and first-day calls? See [[how-do-we-measure-attendance-impact-without-a-control-group]].
