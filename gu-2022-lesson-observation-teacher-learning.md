# A Myth in Language Teacher Learning: Lesson Observation

Gu M. Frontiers in Psychology, 2022.

## Summary

A study of 32 senior high school English teachers learning from three demonstration lessons, using oral reports and interviews. Teachers rarely questioned what they observed; their learning became individual copying of practical skills rather than shared discussion. The author identifies four reasons: how teachers saw the purpose of the lessons, how they made meaning, how they understood the observer-observed relationship, and their view of professional learning.
