# Tarnholm 2026 Budget

Trip budget and final reconciliation, built on [[olumide-bankole]]'s template. Final version 26 June 2026.

## Key Details

- Price per pupil: 268 GBP = centre 172 + coach 75 + contingency 21.
- Income: 52 x 268 = 13,936 GBP (families' payments plus 2,948 GBP in bursary funding for 11 places: 1,200 GBP from the Friends, 1,748 GBP from the school bursary fund).
- Centre: 8,944 GBP. Staff places free at one per ten.
- Coach: 3,900 GBP for a 70-seat coach with two drivers, both days.
- Contingency: 1,092 GBP budgeted, 866 GBP spent (second driver's overnight 310, travel insurance top-up 310, spare waterproofs 184, information evening 62).
- Underspend 226 GBP, returned to the bursary fund.
- Payment plan: 50 GBP deposit by 16 January, then three instalments; deposit waived for bursary places.
