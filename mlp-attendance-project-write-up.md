# MLP Attendance Project Write-Up

Working draft of Ewan's Middle Leadership Programme project write-up: "Raising attendance in Year 10 through first-day contact and a weekly huddle". Due 4 December 2026, 3,000 words. This note holds the structure, the agreed figures and the decisions about framing; the full draft lives in the trust's programme folder.

> [!info]
> Draft v2, about 2,100 words, reviewed by Catriona Dalgleish on 14 October 2026. Data section stands; the reflection section is being rewritten around a theory of change.

## Agreed figures

All year-group aggregates from the pastoral team's reports for Year 10 2025-26, checked against the July report in [[year-10-attendance-drive]]. No pupil-level data.

| Measure | October 2025 | 17 July 2026 | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Year 10 attendance | 91.4% | 93.2% | 94% |
| Persistently absent pupils (below 90%) | 31 | 19 | not set |

The target was missed by 0.8 points. The fall from 31 to 19 persistently absent pupils is the stronger result. [[persistent-absence]] explains why the average hides it.

## Structure

1. **Context** (300 words). Year group of about 210 in eight tutor groups. Attendance 91.4% in October 2025, below the school figure.
2. **What we did** (500 words). Tutor first-day calls from 13 October 2025 ([[first-day-response]]). A weekly attendance huddle with [[donna-whitlock]] and [[marek-nowicki]] from 14 October ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]). Stage 1 letters rewritten to sound less like a fine notice, and sent later and less often. Decision at [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]] not to use attendance as a gate for trip places, so the trip stayed a carrot for everyone.
3. **What happened** (700 words). The figures above; the half-termly curve, which flattened after February half-term; the persistently absent group.
4. **What I cannot claim** (400 words). No comparison group; national and school-wide patterns over the same period; regression to the mean for pupils who started very low. See [[how-do-we-measure-attendance-impact-without-a-control-group]].
5. **Leading it** (700 words, being rewritten). Leading tutors I do not line-manage; the huddle as the main tool; what I would change. Rewritten around [[theory-of-change]].
6. **Next year** (300 words). What transfers to the new Year 10 of 212 pupils.

## Decisions on framing

- The missed target goes in the first paragraph. Agreed with Catriona on 14 October; see [[leadership-projects-should-report-misses]].
- Credit the huddle as a team, not "my intervention". Donna and Marek did most of the calls.
- Do not use the trip-places analysis ([[trip-places-equity-analysis]]) as an attendance result. It belongs in the section on decisions, as an example of using data to stop a policy that would have backfired.
