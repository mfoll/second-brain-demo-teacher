# Textbook Decision 2025-06-11

Short department meeting item: do we spend the faculty capital money on the shortlisted Key Stage 3 textbook series? Decided no.

## Facts on the table

- Quote: 3,900 GBP for 180 copies across the three year books plus teacher guides ([[ks3-textbook-quote-2025]]).
- The series is a chronological survey; enquiry questions sit at chapter ends. Fit with our 15 enquiries: partial in 6, none in 9.
- [[bethan-lloyd-hughes]]'s reading check: about a third of Year 7 would struggle to read it independently.
- Booklet printing estimate: about 1,450 GBP a year for all three years.

## Decision

No purchase. Write our own booklets and spend the capital on library source packs ([[library-enquiry-source-packs]]). [[kofi-asamoah]] agreed provided the money was committed by 27 June. [[desmond-hartigan]] would have preferred the textbooks for homework reading but did not object.

The shortlist is archived as [[ks3-textbook-shortlist-2025]].
