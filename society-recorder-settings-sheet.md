# Society Recorder Settings Sheet

A laminated one-page card kept in the recorder case, written by Ewan on 2 May 2025 after a test recording clipped.

## Summary

WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo with one lapel microphone per channel. Input level set so speech peaks around -12 dB. Low-cut filter on. Auto-level off. Record 30 seconds of room tone first. File naming RVnn_YYYY-MM-DD.wav. Copy to two drives the same day.

## Why It Matters

RV02 was recorded with auto-level on and the pumping is audible; Nisha found it tiring to transcribe. Since the card, no recording has needed repair. Editing clips for school or web uses Audacity: [[audacity-manual]].
