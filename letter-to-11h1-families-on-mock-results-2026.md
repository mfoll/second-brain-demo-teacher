# Letter To 11H1 Families On Mock Results 2026

A short letter sent through Ledgerwise on 25 September 2026 explaining how 11H1's November mock results will be reported.

## Key Details

- Results will be given as a grade range (for example "4-5") with the mark on each paper.
- Explains that mock marking follows the board's mark scheme strictly and that one paper is a snapshot.
- Points to the revision guidance on the Quillyard class page.
- Approved by Ruth; checked by Donna Whitlock for tone.

## Why It Matters

Puts [[mock-grades-should-be-reported-as-ranges]] into practice before the [[november-mocks-2026]]. Last year's families got a single grade that then dropped after re-marking; this is meant to avoid a repeat.
