# How Do We Spot Emerging Persistent Absence Earlier?

## Why It Matters

By the time a pupil's year-to-date attendance falls below 90%, they have often missed two or three weeks. In 2025-26, 5 pupils became persistently absent during the year despite the huddle.

## What We Have Tried

- **Rolling four-week figure.** [[marek-nowicki]] built a flag for anyone whose last four weeks fall below 92%. In use since September 2026.
- **Fast-drop list.** Anyone down more than two points in a fortnight comes to the huddle.
- **Handover from Year 9.** The 24 pupils below 90% in Year 9 went onto the list from day one ([[year-9-to-10-handover-notes-2026]]).
- **Day-of-week patterns.** A cluster of Monday and Friday absences may be an early sign; there is research treating end-of-week absence as a social pattern rather than individual choice ([[alalwani-2026-end-of-week-school-absenteeism]]). Not yet tested here.

## Current Answer

Partial: the four-week flag catches most cases a fortnight earlier than the year-to-date figure. It also flags many pupils who recover on their own. Whether that false-alarm rate is acceptable is the open part.
