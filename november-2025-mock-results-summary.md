# November 2025 Mock Results Summary

Class-level summary of the November 2025 Year 11 history mocks, before and after the 16-mark re-mark.

## Key Details

- 11H2 (27 pupils), grade 4 or above: 70% on first marking, 59% after re-marking the 16-mark answers.
- Average 16-mark score fell from 9.8 to 7.3 out of 16 for 11H2 after re-marking.
- The other two Year 11 classes fell by 6 and 9 percentage points.

## Why It Matters

Shows the size of the effect found at [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10]]: one question, marked generously, moved a class profile by 11 points. Pupils received the re-marked grades at the [[mock-results-lesson-2026-01-15|results lesson of 15 January]].
