# Integration of local knowledge in the secondary school chemistry curriculum - A few examples of ethno-chemistry from Zambia.

Chibuye B, Singh IS, Heliyon, 2024.

## Summary

Documents examples of local and traditional chemical knowledge and argues for using them to teach secondary chemistry in culturally responsive ways, raising the profile of local knowledge alongside the standard curriculum.

## Why It Matters

An odd one for a history department, found while looking for anything on putting local knowledge into a secondary curriculum during the [[diverse-curriculum-audit]]. Its argument, that local knowledge is a way into the standard curriculum rather than an extra, shaped recommendation 2 (a local enquiry built around the main concepts, not bolted on).

## Caveats

Chemistry, another country, descriptive. An argument and examples, not evidence of learning gains.
