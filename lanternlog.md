# Lanternlog

Lanternlog is Corrandale School's secure safeguarding and pastoral concerns system. It is the only place safeguarding details live. This note is a pointer: it says what Lanternlog is for, who uses it, and why nothing recorded there is copied into this vault.

## Summary

Staff log any concern about a pupil's welfare in Lanternlog. Each entry is time-stamped and routed to the designated safeguarding lead, [[fiona-mcrae]], and her deputies, who decide the next step. Access is role-based: as head of Year 10 I can log concerns and see the actions assigned to me for Year 10 pupils; I cannot see the full chronology of a case unless the DSL shares it.

## Why It Matters

Head-of-year work runs close to safeguarding every week: attendance patterns, behaviour changes, things pupils say in tutor time. The rule for this vault is simple and absolute: if it belongs in Lanternlog, it goes there and only there. A vault note may say that something was "logged in Lanternlog and passed to the DSL", with no detail about the pupil, the concern or the outcome. That applies to the huddle notes in [[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]], to trip notes and to Recaps.

## Key Details

- **What goes in.** Concerns about harm, disclosures, changes in presentation, worries raised by families or other pupils, actions taken, contacts with outside agencies. Also low-level pastoral worries when in doubt.
- **What stays out of everything else.** Lanternlog content is not copied into email, [[ledgerwise]] notes, shared drives or personal notes. Families and agencies are referred to by the DSL, not by me.
- **When to log.** The same day, before leaving site. If the DSL is unavailable, a deputy; if the pupil is at immediate risk, phone first and log after.
- **Training.** Annual refresher for all staff in September; the Year 10 tutor refresher in 2026 was on 3 September ([[year-10-tutor-team-2026-27]]).
- **On trips.** Concerns arising on a residential are phoned to the DSL on call and logged on return. The trip plan names the DSL on call ([[plan-a-residential-visit]]).

## Caveats

- This note describes how I use Lanternlog. The school's safeguarding policy, owned by Fiona McRae, is the authority and may change.
- The vault rule protects pupils and also protects the vault: a vault with no safeguarding content can be read by an agent or shown to a colleague without risk.
