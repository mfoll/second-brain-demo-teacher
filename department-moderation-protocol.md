# Department Moderation Protocol

Writing and adopting a moderation protocol for the department's mock exams after the December 2025 moderation showed we were marking the 16-mark question generously. Completed on 14 January 2026; the protocol is now routine.

## Outcome

Blind double-marking of 10% of mock scripts and standardisation on board exemplars before marking, adopted by the department.

## Current State

> [!success]
> Adopted at the department meeting of 14 January 2026 ([[history-department-meeting-2026-01-14]]). Used on the March 2026 Year 10 and Year 11 mocks and the June 2026 Year 10 assessments; next use on the [[november-mocks-2026]].

- The written protocol is [[moderation-protocol-v1]]; the step-by-step is [[run-a-blind-double-marking-sample]].
- On the March 2026 mocks, the gap between first and second markers on the 16-mark question averaged 0.6 marks, down from the 2.1 found against [[vikram-iyer]] in December ([[double-marking-log-2026]]).
- Kittow Academy remains the external check once a year.

## Scope and Boundaries

All internally marked Key Stage 4 mocks and end-of-year assessments. Key Stage 3 assessed pieces are moderated more lightly (one meeting per enquiry, no double-marking) until the [[ks3-assessment-model]] is settled. The protocol does not change how marks are reported to pupils or parents.

## Working Context

At the [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|moderation meeting of 10 December 2025]], a 12-script sample marked blind by Vikram showed our marks on the 16-mark "how far do you agree" question averaging 2.1 above his. We had been putting detailed but one-sided answers in Level 3. Ruth asked me to draft something short that people would actually follow.

Options considered:

1. **External moderation of every mock series.** Rejected: Kittow cannot do it more than once a year, and it finds the problem after marking, not before.
2. **A fixed 12-script sample.** Rejected after Vikram's point that 12 shows a systematic gap but says nothing about individual markers.
3. **Standardise first, then blind double-mark 10% of scripts.** Chosen. Standardisation uses the Merrow exemplars ([[merrow-exemplar-scripts]]) for 30 minutes before marking starts. The 10% sample is drawn by the exams office so markers cannot pick easy scripts. Any script where markers differ by more than 2 marks on a question goes to a third reader.

[[desmond-hartigan]] asked that the time come out of a department meeting rather than an extra after-school slot; agreed. Workload: about 90 minutes per teacher per mock series, against roughly 3 hours of re-marking in January 2026.
