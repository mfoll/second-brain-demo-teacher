# Hollis Granger's Advice on the Draft Consent Form

Email from Hollis Granger, lecturer in public history at Esterbrook University, on 19 August 2025, replying to Ewan's draft of version 2 of the consent form. Summarised, not quoted.

## Summary

- Supported separate yes/no choices for archive, web and education use.
- Suggested adding a closure option (all or part of a recording closed for a set number of years), common in oral history collections and useful when people speak about living colleagues.
- Advised signing after the recording, not before, so the narrator knows what they are agreeing to release.
- Warned that "education" should say whose schools, or the society would be asked later.

## Why It Matters

Three of the four points went into [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]]. The fourth was only half-taken: the form says "local schools", which the form's own caveats note may need tightening.
