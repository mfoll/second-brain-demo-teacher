# Settling-In Survey 2026

The eight-question Year 10 settling-in survey, run in tutor time from 21 to 28 September 2026 in Google Forms. Anonymous; tutor group is the only identifier.

## Questions

1. Which tutor group are you in?
2. I know which adult to go to if something is wrong (yes / not sure / no).
3. I feel I belong in my tutor group (5-point scale).
4. I feel I belong in my option classes (5-point scale).
5. What are you most worried about this year? (workload / friends in different classes / new tutor / exams / other)
6. I like my new tutor so far (yes / not sure yet / no).
7. One thing that would make Year 10 better.
8. Anything else you want me to know? (Text, read by the head of year only.)

## Notes

Q8 answers that raised a worry were treated as a concern and logged in [[lanternlog]]; the survey intro told pupils that would happen. Results: [[settling-in-survey-findings-2026]].
