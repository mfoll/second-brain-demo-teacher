# Forum Workshop Proposal: Ask, Then Listen

Ewan's 300-word proposal to the Historical Teaching Forum conference, submitted on 2 March 2026 and accepted by Ben Akintola on 27 March.

## Summary

Pitch: pupils are asked to "use sources" but rarely to make one. A workshop on teaching interview skills, drawing on ten years of reporting and the society's oral history project. Promised outcomes: a 12-question pupil bank, a consent template for pupil interviews, and three practice activities.

## Why It Matters

The promises set the workshop's content. All three were delivered; the consent template was the most downloaded item afterwards.
