# The effectiveness of cumulative interventions on the classroom management-related performance of pre-service teachers in physical education: improvement of knowledge and situation-specific skills alone is not sufficient

Baumgartner M, Berthold C, Jeisy E. Zeitschrift fur Erziehungswissenschaft, 2025. Subtitle: improvement of knowledge and situation-specific skills alone is not sufficient.

## Summary

An intervention study with pre-service primary teachers, using classroom management in physical education as the case. Four groups received increasingly layered support. An internship alone did not improve classroom management performance. Adding knowledge training, and then training in perceiving and interpreting classroom situations, improved those measures but not performance. Only the group that also had coaching, video-based feedback, self-reflection and practice showed a significant gain in performance.

## Why It Matters

Read a week after the support plan of 9 February 2026. It matched what Ewan was seeing: the trainee knew the behaviour policy and could describe the problem, but that did not change what happened at the classroom door. It supported the choice of practice-heavy support (co-planning, Ewan in the room, rehearsed routines) over more reading. See [[trainee-support-plan-2026-02-09]].

## Caveats

Primary PE pre-service teachers, not secondary history trainees. Small groups per intervention stage.
