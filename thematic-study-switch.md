# Thematic Study Switch

Moving the Year 10 thematic study on the Merrow Certificate in History from Medicine Through Time c1250-present to Migration, Empire and People c790-present. Agreed for all classes in February 2026, cut back to a two-class pilot in May 2026.

## Outcome

The department moves the Year 10 thematic study from Medicine Through Time to Migration, Empire and People: two pilot classes in 2026-27, all classes from September 2027 if the pilot mocks hold up.

## Current State

> [!info]
> In 2026-27 only 10H1 (me) and 10H3 ([[ruth-calder]]) study Migration, Empire and People. 10H2 ([[desmond-hartigan]]), 10H4 ([[priyanka-sethi]]) and 10H5 ([[tomasz-zielinski]]) stay on Medicine Through Time. The whole department switches in September 2027 only if the pilot classes' March 2027 mock results are at least as good.

- Decision history: 5-1 vote for a full switch on 4 February 2026 ([[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04]]); partial reversal to a pilot on 13 May 2026 ([[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13]]).
- [[sunil-varma]] confirmed in May 2026 that two thematic study options in one year group can be entered under two codes; the pilot depends on that.
- Booklet 1 is taught and booklet 2 is in use; booklet 3 is being rewritten by Ruth after the first teaching weeks ([[migration-resources-tracker]]).
- How "at least as good" will be judged is not yet agreed ([[how-do-we-compare-pilot-and-medicine-mock-results]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Key Stage 4 thematic study only. The depth, British depth and period studies do not change. Year 11 (11H1) finishes Medicine this year whatever happens. Pupils' option choices are not affected: everyone still takes history the same way; only the content of one paper section differs.

## Working Context

Why switch at all: Medicine is well resourced and our results on it are steady, but the department has wanted a thematic study that connects to the town and to the [[diverse-curriculum-audit]] findings. Migration also links to Year 9 work on empire and to the ropeworks interviews (several workers came to Corrandale from elsewhere). Ruth and I argued for it; Desmond argued that Medicine is examined well, that the specimen materials were late and that the workload would land on an early career teacher.

Why the cut-back: the board published revised specimen papers and the mark scheme only on 28 April 2026 ([[merrow-migration-specimen-papers-2026]]), three months later than expected. In early May the migration booklets were about 40% drafted ([[migration-booklet-check-in-2026-05-06]]). [[kofi-asamoah]] asked whether we could prove the switch would not cost grades. A pilot answered that without abandoning the decision. My own lesson from this is in [[pilot-before-a-whole-department-switch]].

The superseded all-class plan is kept as [[thematic-switch-transition-plan-v1]].
