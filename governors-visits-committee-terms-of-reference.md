# Governors' Visits Committee Terms Of Reference

Terms of reference of the Corrandale governors' visits committee, 2025 revision, read before the 2026 trip went to committee.

## Key Details

- Members: three governors (chair [[elspeth-moran]]), the headteacher or a deputy, the educational visits coordinator in attendance.
- Meets termly; extra meetings by email for urgent day visits only.
- Approves residential and overseas visits after EVC sign-off in [[tripplan]].
- Considers educational purpose, cost to families, access for disadvantaged pupils, staffing and the risk assessment summary.
- Papers due ten working days before the meeting.

## Why It Matters

Sets the deadlines in [[plan-a-residential-visit]]: papers for the 9 December 2026 meeting are due by 25 November.
