# Field trip

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes a field trip or excursion as a journey by a group, such as school students, to a place away from their usual environment for education or leisure, at home or abroad. The article covers purposes, planning and the kinds of sites schools visit.

## Why It Matters

Read in October 2025 when I was writing the purpose section of the first TripPlan entry for [[tarnholm-residential-2026]]. The useful distinction was between education and leisure: [[rebecca-tull]] and the governors wanted the educational purpose of a residential spelled out, and the castle enquiry and mine visit had to be more than a nice day out. The aims in the [[tarnholm-2026-evaluation]] come from that exercise.

## Caveats

A short, general article; nothing specific on residentials or risk.
