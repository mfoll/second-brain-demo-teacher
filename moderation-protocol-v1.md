# Department Moderation Protocol v1

The two-page protocol I drafted after the December 2025 moderation and circulated on 9 January 2026. Adopted with three amendments on 14 January ([[history-department-meeting-2026-01-14]]).

## Summary

- Standardise on Merrow exemplars for 30 minutes before every mock marking window.
- Exams office draws a random 10% of scripts per class, minimum 3, names and first marks removed.
- Second marking crosses classes; early career teachers' samples go to Ruth or Desmond.
- Differences above 2 marks on a question go to a third reader; a marker whose mean gap exceeds 1.5 marks re-marks that question for the class.
- Kittow external moderation once a year.

## Why It Matters

The written basis for [[run-a-blind-double-marking-sample]]. The trigger, a 2.1-mark gap on the 16-mark question, is in [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10]].
