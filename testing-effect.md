# Testing effect

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on the testing effect: spending part of learning time retrieving information from memory improves long-term retention more than spending it re-studying. The article separates it from the general practice effect and reviews the classic laboratory studies and later classroom work.

## Why It Matters

The article I sent Ruth in January 2025 when I proposed the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]]. It made the case in a page: the point of a quiz is the act of recalling, not the score. That framing is why quiz results stay out of reports ([[retrieval-quizzes-should-stay-low-stakes]]).

## Caveats

An encyclopedia overview; for anything I would defend in a department meeting I go to the studies it cites. Most evidence is from word lists and university students, not 15-year-olds writing about Weimar.
