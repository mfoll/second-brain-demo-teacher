# Comparative Judgement Practice Round 2026-10-21

A dry run of the comparative judgement trial, using 12 Year 10 Medicine 8-mark answers from Tomasz's class (names covered), after school with Ruth and Tomasz.

- Judged in a shared spreadsheet with pairs drawn at random: 60 judgements, so each script appeared in ten pairs.
- Each judgement took 30 to 50 seconds once we stopped reading every word.
- Rank order broadly matched Tomasz's marks; the two biggest disagreements were both very short answers with one strong point.
- Ruth's worry: judging is quick but we lose the reasons. We agreed each judge writes one line on any pair that is hard to call.
- Spreadsheet works for 12 scripts; for 28 with ten comparisons each I need a better way to draw pairs. Fixed in [[comparative-judgement-trial-plan]].
