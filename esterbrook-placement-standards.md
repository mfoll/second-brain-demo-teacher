# Esterbrook Placement Standards

The assessment framework Esterbrook University uses for placement reports: eight standards, each judged "not yet met", "working towards" or "met" at the interim and final points.

## Summary

The eight standards: high expectations; pupil progress; subject and curriculum knowledge; planning; adaptive teaching; assessment; managing behaviour; professional conduct. Each has four or five indicators. The final report needs evidence for every standard, drawn from observations, planning and the trainee's own reflections.

## Why It Matters

Every observation and weekly target maps to one of these. In 2025-26 "managing behaviour" was the standard at risk in the spring; in 2026-27 "adaptive teaching" and "assessment" carry Yusuf's pacing and checking-understanding targets.

## Caveats

The standards describe outcomes, not routines. They say nothing on how to teach an entry routine, which is why the school adds its own week-one work: [[entry-routine]].
