# Society Listening Website

A public website where consented clips from [[ropeworks-voices]] can be heard, hosted by the Corrandale Local History Society.

## Outcome

A public website where consented ropeworks clips can be heard, hosted by the society. Not started.

## Current State

- Idea raised by [[maureen-castleford]] at the society committee on 3 August 2026, after the Courier ran a piece on the project.
- Only interviews with the web option ticked on [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]] qualify. That is 8 of the 12 version 2 interviews. None of the 2019-form interviews qualify unless re-consent adds web use.
- Three hosting routes priced in [[society-website-hosting-quotes]]; options compared in [[listening-website-options]].
- No decision. The committee wants the second deposit done first.

## Scope and Boundaries

Ewan advises on consent and on clip editing. He is not building or maintaining the site; the society needs a volunteer who will still be there in five years.

## Working Context

- Clip length: 1 to 3 minutes, edited in Audacity ([[audacity-manual]]), each with a provenance line: interview number, date, role at the works, consent version.
- Every clip must be traceable to its consent form. The interview log is the control: [[ropeworks-interview-log]].
- Risk: a clip online can be copied and cut out of context. Some interviewees are frank about named managers. Clips that name third parties are out.
