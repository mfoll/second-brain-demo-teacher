# Year 10 Parents' Evening 2026-03-12

Second Year 10 parents' evening of 2025-26, Thursday 12 March 2026, run in the hybrid format: video by default with 7-minute slots, and an in-person block from 17:00 to 18:30 in the library for families who asked when booking.

- 171 of 209 families booked (82%), up from 78% in November.
- 23 families asked for in-person appointments. Teachers moved between the library and their laptops in a planned 20-minute window; it worked, but two colleagues ran late on both sides.
- Dropped calls: three, all re-joined within a minute after the school's network change in January.
- Family feedback by short form: 88 responses, 84% satisfied or very satisfied.

Decided with [[adeel-chaudhry]] on 20 March to keep this format: [[parents-evening-review]], [[should-parents-evening-stay-online]].
