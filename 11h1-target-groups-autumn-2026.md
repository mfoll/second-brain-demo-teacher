# 11H1 Target Groups Autumn 2026

How I am grouping 11H1 for extra support this term. Groups are described by need and size; which pupils are in them is recorded in Ledgerwise only.

| Group | Size | Need | Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 7 | Knowledge gaps in Elizabethan religion and Weimar 1924-29 (baseline below 40%) | Weekly 15-minute quiz clinic at lunchtime, Thursdays |
| B | 8 | Secure knowledge but one-sided 16-mark answers | Planning-grid practice in lesson; first in line for comparative judgement feedback |
| C | 5 | Grade 7+ potential, need precision and judgement | Extension reading and one extra essay a fortnight |

The other 8 pupils are on track and get the normal lesson diet.

Review after the [[november-mocks-2026]]. Group A is the group I would invite to the revision programme first.
