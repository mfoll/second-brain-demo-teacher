# Corrandale Archives Centre Catalogue Entry: Corrandale Ropeworks Company Records

The archive's catalogue entry for the records of the Corrandale Ropeworks, deposited by the liquidator in 1987. Consulted in the search room on 21 June 2025 with Alistair Finnie.

## Summary

About 140 boxes: minute books 1868 to 1971, wage books to 1959, annual returns, plans of the site and ropewalk, photographs from the 1920s to the 1980s, and the closure correspondence of 1985 to 1986. Records after the 1971 takeover are thinner because head office kept them.

## Why It Matters

The documentary side of the Year 9 enquiry. Pupils will compare a 1985 board letter on short-time working with what interviewees remember of it. The workforce figures in [[corrandale-ropeworks]] come from the annual returns.

## Caveats

Wage books are closed for 100 years from the last entry under the archive's rules, so nothing after 1925 is open. Photographs need the archive's permission for reproduction in class booklets.

## Source Location

Corrandale Archives Centre search room; catalogue entry on the archive's own catalogue terminal.
