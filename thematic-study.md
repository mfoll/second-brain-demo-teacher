# Thematic Study

The part of the Merrow Certificate in History that follows one theme across a very long period, roughly a thousand years, instead of a short period in depth. Pupils study change and continuity, causation and significance over centuries. We teach it in Year 10 as the first unit of Paper 1.

## The two options

- **Medicine Through Time c1250-present.** What the department has taught since 2017. Well resourced, stable results. Includes a historic environment study of a hospital site.
- **Migration, Empire and People c790-present.** Piloted in 10H1 and 10H3 in 2026-27 ([[thematic-study-switch]]). Revised specimen papers published 28 April 2026 ([[merrow-migration-specimen-papers-2026]]). Includes a site study; we chose a port study from the board's list.

## What makes it hard to teach

- Breadth: pupils have to hold a thousand years in mind. The exam's 16-mark question asks for judgements across the whole period.
- Factors: both options are taught through recurring factors (in Medicine: individuals, institutions, science and technology, attitudes; in Migration: economic, political, religious, empire). Pupils must track them across periods.
- Chronological framework matters even more than at Key Stage 3 ([[chronological-framework]]).
