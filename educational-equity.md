# Educational equity

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Treats educational equity as a measure of fairness in schooling built on two linked ideas: fairness, meaning personal circumstances should not stand in the way of success, and inclusion, meaning a standard that covers every learner in the system. The article also touches on how equity and excellence relate.

## Why It Matters

The two ideas gave the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] its title and its structure: who a rule excludes (fairness) and whether the trip is open to the whole year group (inclusion). They also sit behind the cost and bursary sections of every residential plan ([[school-bursary-fund-guidance]]) and my stance in [[trip-places-should-not-be-earned-by-attendance]].

## Caveats

Conceptual overview; no evidence on trips or attendance.
