# Historical Teaching Forum Workshop

Ewan's workshop "Ask, then listen" at the Historical Teaching Forum conference, 27 June 2026, slot 3, 60 minutes, 34 attendees.

## What happened

- Ran to plan except the pairs activity, which overran by eight minutes because people enjoyed it.
- The five-second silence drill landed best: several teachers said they had never noticed how fast they fill a pause.
- Most questions were about consent for pupils interviewing family. Answer: short parent-signed form, school devices only, no publication.
- [[ben-akintola]] sat in for the second half.

## Afterwards

Feedback 4.5 out of 5 from 29 forms: [[forum-workshop-feedback-summary]]. Invitation for 2027 followed on 3 July.
