# Corrandale Local History Society Recording Agreement 2019

Superseded on 10 September 2025 by [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]]. Kept because interviews RV01 to RV07 were recorded under it and their permitted uses are still defined by it until re-consent forms are logged.

## Summary

The society's one-page recording agreement from 2019, written for a previous project on the town's chapels. One signature box agrees to deposit at the Corrandale Archives Centre "and use by the Society in its publications and events". There is a naming choice (named or anonymous) and no closure option.

## Why It Matters

It has no education option and no web option. Ewan used it for the first seven Ropeworks Voices interviews (April to August 2025) because it was what the society had. The gap only showed when an interviewee asked whether pupils would hear her. That is why [[can-2019-form-ropeworks-interviews-be-used-in-lessons]] exists and why the [[re-consent-letter-2026-10-05]] went out.

## Caveats

"Society events" might arguably cover a talk in a school hall, but not routine classroom use. Ewan and Maureen Castleford agreed not to rely on the stretch.

## Source Location

Society shared drive, "Paperwork / Superseded". Signed originals for RV01 to RV07 at the Archives Centre with the first deposit.
