# The Weekly Huddle Matters More Than The Letters

## Stance

For a year group, a short weekly meeting where three people who know the pupils agree one action per pupil does more for attendance than any letter sequence.

## Why I Think This

- In 2025-26 the persistent absence count fell from 31 to 19 while letters were reduced, not increased. Stage 2 letters produced no measurable change in 14 cases and two complaints ([[year-10-attendance-review-2026-02-10]]).
- The huddle forces ownership: every pupil leaves the meeting with a named adult and a next step.
- Letters are useful as a record and when the law requires them; they rarely start a conversation.

## What Would Change My Mind

If 2026-27 shows the same improvement in a year group without a huddle, the routine is not what made the difference. [[adeel-chaudhry]] is considering a huddle in Year 8, which would give a comparison.

## Caveats

One cohort, no control group, and a lot of the change came from a few pupils. Medium confidence.
