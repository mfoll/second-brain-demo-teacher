# How Many Enquiries Per Year At KS3?

## Question

How many enquiries should each Key Stage 3 year have, given one history lesson a week in Year 7 and two a week in Years 8 and 9?

## Current Answer

Five a year, 15 in total, decided in April 2025. Year 7, with one lesson a week, gets shorter enquiries of about six lessons; Years 8 and 9 about eleven lessons each.

## Options considered

- Six a year: Priyanka's first draft. Rejected: too thin in Year 7, and assessed pieces every five weeks overload marking.
- Four a year: too few second-order concepts covered twice across the key stage.
- Five: each concept appears at least twice across the 15 ([[second-order-concepts]]).

## Caveat

The two timeline lessons each September come out of the first enquiry's time.
