# Whole-Class Feedback

Instead of writing individual comments in each book, the teacher reads the whole set, records what is common (strengths, misconceptions, literacy errors) on one sheet, and teaches from it in the next lesson, ending with pupils redrafting their own work.

## Why it works for us

- Feedback arrives in the next lesson, not two weeks later.
- Pupils act on it during lesson time, which written comments rarely achieved.
- It cut my marking time per set from 2 h 40 min to 55 min ([[whole-class-feedback-marking-time-log]]).

## Limits

- Individual needs can be missed; the sheet keeps a margin for three or four individual conversations.
- Exam classes want individual targets; for Year 11 I add one line per essay ([[does-whole-class-feedback-work-for-year-11-essays]]).
