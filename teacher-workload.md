# Teacher Workload

Retrieval surface for how much time the job takes, where it goes and what has been done to cut it: marking, mentoring, pastoral work, volunteer work that leaks into term time.

## What lives here

- Ewan's own time log from autumn 2025: [[ewan-time-log-autumn-2025]]. The headline: 52 hours in a typical week, with marking and pastoral email the two largest blocks.
- The trust's workload commitments: [[wyvernmoor-workload-charter]].
- The department's switch to whole-class feedback, which saved about 3 hours a week on Year 9 books: [[whole-class-feedback-trial]].
- Mentoring cost: [[how-much-mentor-time-does-a-trainee-really-need]].
- Research on burnout and workload is filed under the Sources that link here.

## Recurring points

- Time savings get spent, not banked. The feedback trial freed time that went straight into Year 10 attendance calls.
- September and the mock marking weeks in November and March are the heaviest. Avoid starting new commitments in those weeks.
- The [[can-we-use-ai-to-draft-feedback-on-mock-essays]] question is partly a workload question.
