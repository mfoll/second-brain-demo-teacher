# Joanna Rees

My news editor at the Corrandale Courier for most of my ten years there. She now writes the paper's weekly local history column, "Corrandale Then", and is the person I ask when I need to know how the town told a story at the time.

## Current touchpoints

- Gave permission for the Courier's 1985-86 headlines to be reproduced in school booklets ([[courier-ropeworks-closure-1986]]).
- Her column ran two pieces on Ropeworks Voices in 2025 and is a good example of how a town tells its own past ([[public-memory-and-heritage]]).
- Offered to come into a Year 9 lesson in spring 2027 to talk about how a local paper covers a closure. Not yet booked.

## Working with her

Phone, not email. Expects facts checked before anything is printed, as she always did. No contact with pupils except through a planned visit signed off by the school.
