# Elizabethan era

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on England under Elizabeth I (1558-1603): government, religion, exploration, the economy and the culture of the period, and how the "golden age" picture has been used by historians.

## Why It Matters

Background for the 190 Elizabethan questions in the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]] and for the [[year-11-revision-booklet-elizabethan]]. The article's discussion of the "golden age" label gave me an interpretation question for 11H1: how far was Elizabeth's reign a golden age, and for whom? It works well as a 16-mark practice title.

## Caveats

Our British depth study covers 1568-1603, narrower than the article. The article is weighted to culture; the specification is weighted to religion, plots and the Armada.
