# Lottery Allocation

Allocating oversubscribed places by a random draw among all eligible applicants. Corrandale uses it for the Year 10 residential since December 2025.

## Why a Lottery

Every other rule we looked at rewarded something other than wanting to go: attendance (which tracked bursary eligibility and illness, see [[trip-places-equity-analysis]]), speed of reply (which rewards families with time and email habits), or staff judgement (which is hard to explain and easy to suspect). A lottery is simple to explain and gives each applicant the same chance. It is the same reasoning behind [[sortition]] in public life, at a much smaller scale.

## How We Run It

1. Close applications on a fixed date. Every applicant who returns the form is eligible.
2. Number applicants from a list sorted by tutor group.
3. The educational visits coordinator draws numbered cards from a box with the trip leader and a witness from outside the staff ([[tarnholm-lottery-draw-2025-12-12]]).
4. Places in draw order; the rest form an ordered waiting list.
5. Each place is conditional on a signed [[behaviour-agreement]] and the deposit (waived for bursary places).

## Limits

- It does not guarantee places for pupils who would benefit most; a school could reserve some places for that, but we chose not to, because the choice of who "would benefit" is exactly the judgement we wanted to avoid.
- Families who miss the deadline lose out. The 2027 window is three weeks with two reminders.
