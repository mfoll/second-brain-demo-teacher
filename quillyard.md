# Quillyard

The school's learning platform: homework setting, the quiz bank, class pages and resources. Every class at Corrandale has a Quillyard page; pupils log in with their school account.

## Summary

Three parts matter for my work:

- **Homework.** Set, collected and marked online. Late and missing homework flow to Ledgerwise overnight as behaviour points, which is useful as Head of Year and annoying as a teacher when the sync is late.
- **Quiz bank.** Multiple-choice and short-answer questions, tagged and shareable. The department's [[retrieval-quiz-bank]] lives here: 420 questions on Weimar and Nazi Germany and Elizabethan England. Quizzes self-mark; teachers see item-level results.
- **Class pages.** Lesson resources, booklets as PDFs, revision lists. Parents can see the class page through the parent app, not the quiz results.

## Why It Matters

Quillyard is where retrieval practice actually happens outside lessons, and it is the only approved place where pupil work is stored digitally for history. That second point came up in the [[dpo-ai-tools-decision-2026-09-30|DPO decision of 30 September 2026]]: Quillyard is covered by a trust data agreement; any AI tool would need its own.

## Key Details

- Quiz analytics: per-question percentage correct and time taken, per class. Summer 2025 analysis is in [[quillyard-quiz-analytics-summer-2025]].
- Question tags we use: unit, lesson number, question type (recall, sequence, odd one out, cause).
- Quizzes can be set to release one question at a time, which stops pupils looking ahead.
- Export to spreadsheet is class-level only; no pupil-level exports leave the system. That suits the vault rule of aggregates only.
- Quillyard has no AI feedback feature. The supplier mentioned one "in development" at a trust demo in June 2026; nothing since.

## Caveats

- Analytics count attempts, not learning. A question everyone gets right may be easy or may be well taught.
- Homework sync to Ledgerwise fails silently about once a half-term. Check before issuing behaviour points.
