# First-Day Call Script

One-page script for tutors making first-day absence calls, written 10 October 2025 and revised with [[jamal-osei]]'s routine in February 2026.

## Key Details

- Call by 10:00. Introduce yourself as the tutor, by name.
- Open with concern, not a question about the reason: "We noticed X isn't in today and wanted to check everything is all right."
- If ill: wish them better, ask if they expect to be in tomorrow, offer work on [[quillyard]] if more than two days.
- If no reason or vague: ask whether anything at school is making it hard to come in; offer a call back from the head of year or [[donna-whitlock]].
- Anything worrying: log in [[lanternlog]] for the DSL straight after the call.
- Record one line in [[ledgerwise]].
- No answer: one voicemail, one text, then pass to the attendance officer at 12:00.

## Why It Matters

The script is what made [[first-day-response]] consistent across eight tutors.
