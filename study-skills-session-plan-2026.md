# Year 11 Study Skills Session Plan 2026

Hannah Greaves's plan for six Year 11 tutor-time study skills sessions, autumn term 2026, shared on the Year 11 Quillyard page.

## Key Details

1. Why re-reading feels good and does little.
2. Making a revision timetable with spaced topics.
3. Flashcards that test, not tell.
4. Self-quizzing (uses three history quizzes from the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]] as examples).
5. Exam command words across subjects (history section from me).
6. The week before a mock: sleep, routine, what to bring.

## Why It Matters

Pupils hear the same message about [[retrieval-practice]] from tutors, science and history. I teach to the command words in session 5 so I do not repeat a different version.
