# School refusal

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes school refusal as a child avoiding school, or struggling to stay in class for the whole day, usually with emotional distress around the time of going in. It is set apart from absence caused by parents or by circumstances such as homelessness, and from truancy, where the pupil is not distressed but chooses to be elsewhere.
