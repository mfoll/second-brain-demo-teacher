# Parents' Evening Trial Findings

Summary of the two Year 10 trials, written on 20 March 2026 for [[adeel-chaudhry]] and the other heads of year.

## Figures

| Evening | Format | Families booked | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 2024 (before my time) | In person, 5-min slots | 61% | Sports hall |
| [[year-10-parents-evening-2025-11-27|27 Nov 2025]] | Video, 5-min slots | 78% (162 of 209) | About 9 dropped calls |
| [[year-10-parents-evening-2026-03-12|12 Mar 2026]] | Video 7-min + in-person block | 82% (171 of 209) | 23 in-person requests |

## What Families Said

From 118 survey responses in November ([[parents-evening-survey-2025]]) and 88 short-form responses in March, as themes:

- Not having to travel or arrange childcare was the most common reason given for preferring video.
- Five minutes was too short, especially when a parent had several teachers to see in a row.
- Some families wanted to meet the tutor or head of year in person for a bigger conversation.
- A small number struggled with the booking system; the office booked for them by phone.

## What Staff Said

Finishing on time and not standing in a cold hall. Harder to read a parent's reaction on video. Moving between video and in-person in March worked but made two colleagues late.

## Recommendation (accepted)

Video by default with 7-minute slots; in-person block from 17:00 to 18:30 on request; office phone booking for families who need it. See [[parents-evening-review]].
