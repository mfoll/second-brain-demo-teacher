# Year 10 Attendance Drive

A year-long push to raise Year 10 attendance for the 2025-26 cohort (209 pupils), started in my first half-term as head of year.

## Outcome

Year 10 attendance up from 91.4% in October 2025 to 94% by the end of the summer term. Closed on 17 July 2026 at 93.2%: the target was missed, but the number of [[persistent-absence|persistently absent]] pupils fell from 31 to 19.

> [!info]
> Target missed by 0.8 points; persistent absence down from 31 to 19 pupils. The headline average hides most of the change, which is why the write-up leads with the persistent absence count.

## Current State

- Completed. Final figures recorded at [[year-10-attendance-final-2026-07-17]].
- The work is now the subject of my Middle Leadership Programme project: [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]], due 4 December 2026. Figures there must match the [[year-10-attendance-dashboard]].
- The weekly huddle continued with the new Year 10 from September 2026 under [[lead-year-10]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Attendance of the 2025-26 Year 10 only. Individual pupils were discussed by name in the huddle and recorded in [[ledgerwise]]; this note and its children hold counts. Where absence touched a safeguarding concern, it was logged in [[lanternlog]] and passed to the DSL; none of that is here.

## What We Did

- **First-day calls.** From 13 October 2025 tutors called home on the first morning of any unexplained absence, using the [[first-day-call-script]]. See [[first-day-response]].
- **Weekly huddle.** Me, [[donna-whitlock]] (pastoral manager) and [[marek-nowicki]] (attendance officer), Tuesdays 08:10, 25 minutes, working from the dashboard. First one on [[attendance-huddle-launch-2025-10-14|14 October 2025]]. The procedure is [[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]].
- **Letters, later and fewer.** Stage 1 letters rewritten to sound less like a fine notice ([[attendance-letter-template-2025]]), replacing the [[attendance-letter-template-2024]].
- **Tutor time.** Attendance shown by tutor group, not by pupil, on the Monday briefing slide.
