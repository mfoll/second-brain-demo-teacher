# Informed Consent

English Wikipedia article on informed consent.

## Summary

Describes informed consent as an ethics principle: a person needs enough information and understanding before agreeing to something that carries risk, including the right to refuse. The article starts from healthcare but notes the principle applies more widely, for example to taking part in research.

## Why It Matters

Ewan read it the week after the July 2025 conversation in which an interviewee asked whether pupils would hear her recording. The point that consent depends on understanding, not only a signature, shaped two features of [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]]: separate choices for each use ([[layered-consent]]) and signing only after the interviewee knows what was recorded.

## Caveats

Mostly about medical and research consent. Oral history practice and the archive's terms set the specifics for the society.
