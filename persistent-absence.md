# Persistent Absence

A pupil is persistently absent when their attendance falls below 90% of possible sessions. At Corrandale it is the measure the attendance team acts on, and the one that showed what the [[year-10-attendance-drive]] actually changed.

## Definition

- **Threshold.** Below 90% attendance across the sessions counted so far. Ten percent is about one day missed every fortnight, or 19 days in a full school year of 190 days.
- **What counts.** Every absence counts, authorised or not: illness, appointments, holidays in term time, unexplained absence. Lateness after the register closes counts as an absence for that session.
- **What does not count.** Approved educational activity off site (trips, sport fixtures, work experience) is recorded as present.
- **Period.** The [[trust-attendance-policy-2025]] measures it year to date and reports half-termly. [[marek-nowicki]] also runs a rolling four-week figure for early warning.

## Why Averages Hide It

A year group average blends a large group near 96% with a small group far below. In October 2025 Year 10 averaged 91.4%: that looked like a whole year group slightly below target. The distribution told a different story. 31 pupils, 15% of the cohort, were below 90%, and they accounted for nearly half of all sessions missed. Moving the average from 91.4% to 93.2% by July 2026 hides that the persistent group shrank from 31 to 19.

> [!tip]
> Report attendance as two numbers: the average and the count below 90%. The second number is the one that names a caseload.

## How We Use It

- The weekly huddle works from the list of pupils below 90%, plus those dropping fast ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]).
- A pupil leaves the caseload after four consecutive weeks above 90%, not on the first good week.
- The count goes on the Monday tutor briefing slide by tutor group, never by pupil.

## Limits

- The threshold is arbitrary: a pupil at 89.9% and one at 90.1% are not different cases.
- Early in the year it is volatile; one week of illness in September can make a pupil persistently absent until November.
- It does not distinguish [[truancy]] from [[school-refusal]] or long-term illness, which need different responses. See [[gonzalvez-2026-school-absenteeism-profiles]].
