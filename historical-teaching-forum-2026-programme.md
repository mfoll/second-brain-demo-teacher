# Historical Teaching Forum Conference 2026 Programme

Programme for the two-day conference on 26 and 27 June 2026, published on 20 May.

## Summary

About 400 delegates, a keynote each morning, four workshop slots a day in eight rooms. Ewan's workshop was in slot 3 on day two. Other sessions he marked: a panel on thematic studies (Merrow's new migration option was discussed by teachers from three boards' centres), and a workshop on comparative judgement.

## Why It Matters

The thematic studies panel fed back into [[thematic-study-switch]] in the department. The comparative judgement session is relevant to [[comparative-judgement]].
