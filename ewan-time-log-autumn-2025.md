# Ewan's Time Log, Autumn 2025

A 15-minute-block time log kept for six sample weeks between 8 September and 12 December 2025, done for the MLP session on workload.

## Summary

Average 52 hours a week. Teaching 19.5; planning 7; marking 6.5; head-of-year work 10 (of which pastoral email 4); mentoring 3.5; meetings 3; oral history 2.5 (evenings and weekends). Heaviest week: 61 hours in mock marking week.

## Why It Matters

Mentoring took 3.5 hours a week against one protected period. Pastoral email was the biggest single block outside teaching. The log is why Ewan started batching parent email twice a day in January 2026.

## Caveats

Six weeks, self-recorded, by someone who knew he was measuring. Probably an underestimate.
