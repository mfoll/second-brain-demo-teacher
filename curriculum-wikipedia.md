# Curriculum

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines a curriculum as the totality of students' experiences in an educational process, often meaning a planned sequence of instruction, and distinguishes explicit, implicit, excluded and extracurricular curricula.

## Why It Matters

The idea of an 'excluded' curriculum, what a school chooses not to teach, gave Priyanka and me the frame for the [[diverse-curriculum-audit]]: we coded not just what the 2019 schemes taught but who they left out.

## Caveats

Definitions only; no guidance on subject sequencing.
