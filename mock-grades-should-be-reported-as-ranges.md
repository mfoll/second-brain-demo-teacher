# Mock Grades Should Be Reported As Ranges

## Stance

Pupils and families should get mock outcomes as a grade range plus marks, not a single grade. A single grade from one paper, one marker and borrowed boundaries claims more precision than it has.

## Why I Think This

- Boundaries move: the grade 4 line was 71 in 2025 and 74 in 2026 ([[grade-boundaries]]).
- Our November 2025 grades dropped 11 points after moderation; families who had heard a single grade felt misled ([[mock-results-lesson-2026-01-15]]).
- 11H2 then beat the March prediction by six points ([[why-were-the-2026-predictions-too-low]]).
- Moira Stenhouse asked teachers not to announce single grades in class.

## What Would Change My Mind

If families find ranges confusing or read the top of the range as the promise. I will ask at the Year 11 parents' evening in January.

## Implications

Used for 11H1 from November 2026 ([[letter-to-11h1-families-on-mock-results-2026]]). The department data drop still wants one grade; I enter the lower end.
