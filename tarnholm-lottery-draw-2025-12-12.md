# Tarnholm Lottery Draw 2025-12-12

Lottery for the 52 places on the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]], Friday 12 December 2025, 12:30, school library office.

Drawn by [[rebecca-tull]] (educational visits coordinator), with me and [[chris-delahaye]] (chair of the Friends of Corrandale School) as witness. All 61 applications were eligible; applicants were numbered from the application list sorted by tutor group, the numbers written on identical cards and drawn from a box. The first 52 numbers drawn got places; the next 9 formed an ordered waiting list.

Families heard by letter and an email through [[ledgerwise]] the same afternoon, with the [[behaviour-agreement]] to sign and return with the 50 GBP deposit by 16 January 2026. Bursary families were contacted separately by [[olumide-bankole]].

Afterwards: two places were declined in January and went to waiting-list numbers 1 and 2. The final group of 52 included 20 of the 23 applicants who would have been excluded under the 95% threshold ([[trip-places-equity-analysis]]).

The method is now the default in [[lottery-allocation]].
