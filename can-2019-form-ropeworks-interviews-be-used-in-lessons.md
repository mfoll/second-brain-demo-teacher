# Can 2019-Form Ropeworks Interviews Be Used In Lessons?

## Question

The first 7 Ropeworks Voices interviews were recorded on the society's 2019 consent form, which covers deposit at the Archives Centre but has no education-use option. Can clips from them go into the Year 9 enquiry?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> No, unless the interviewee signs a re-consent form that allows education use and the archive has logged it. Only the 11 version 2 interviews with education use ticked are usable now.

Agreed with [[maureen-castleford]] in October 2025, written into [[conduct-an-oral-history-interview]]. Re-consent letters went out on 5 October 2026 ([[re-consent-letter-2026-10-05]]); 3 of 7 have come back so far.

## Why not "archive deposit means public, so it is fine"

Archive deposit makes a recording available to researchers who visit and sign the reading-room rules. A classroom is a different use: clips played to 30 teenagers in the town, some of whom may know the speaker's family. The interviewees did not agree to that. [[hollis-granger]] gave a similar example of a resource withdrawn for exactly this reason.

## Caveats

A re-consent counts from the date the archive logs it, not the date it is posted. Check the [[ropeworks-interview-log]] before using any clip.
