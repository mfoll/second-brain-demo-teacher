# Conduct An Oral History Interview

Ewan's procedure for a [[ropeworks-voices]] interview, from first contact to deposit. Written in October 2025 after the first twelve interviews and revised after the Forum workshop in June 2026. A cut-down version is used for pupils in the [[local-history-enquiry]].

## When to use

Any recorded interview for the society. Not for informal conversations at open evenings: those are notes, not recordings, and nothing is kept without consent.

## Inputs

- Interviewee contact via the society secretary, and their preferred place and time.
- Blank [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]] and information sheet, two copies.
- Society recorder, charged, with the settings in [[society-recorder-settings-sheet]]; spare batteries; two lapel microphones.
- Question bank: [[ropeworks-interview-question-bank]].

## Steps

1. **Before the day.** Send the information sheet by post at least a week ahead. Phone to confirm and ask what they did at the works and when. Check the [[ropeworks-interview-log]] for anyone they worked alongside who has already been interviewed.
2. **Consent first.** On arrival, go through the information sheet in person. Say plainly that the three uses on the form are separate and they can say no to any of them. Do not ask them to sign yet; they sign after the recording, once they know what they said.
3. **Room and sound.** Quiet room, soft furnishings, phones off, away from fridges and clocks. Record 30 seconds of room tone. Sound check: ask what they had for breakfast and play it back.
4. **Opening.** State on the recording: date, place, interviewer, interviewee, interview number.
5. **Interview.** Start with life before the works, then the first day. Open questions ("Tell me about...", "What was it like when..."). One question at a time. Wait five seconds after an answer before speaking; the best material comes after the pause. Follow their story, not the question list.
6. **Hard topics.** Accidents, the closure and named managers come up. Do not steer away, but do not push. If they ask to stop, stop. Note any section they want closed.
7. **Closing.** Ask if there is anything they expected to be asked and were not. Thank them on the recording.
8. **Consent.** Summarise what was covered. Go through the form; record their three choices, naming choice and any closure period. Both sign. Leave them one copy.
9. **Same day.** Copy the WAV file to the society drive and the backup drive before reformatting the card. File name RVnn_YYYY-MM-DD.wav.
10. **Within a week.** Write a one-page summary with timestamps and send it with the file to [[nisha-kapoor]]. Add the row to the [[ropeworks-interview-log]], including the consent choices and a "safe for clips" flag.
11. **Deposit.** At the next batch, hand the WAV, transcript and original consent form to [[alistair-finnie]]: [[ropeworks-deposit-agreement]].

## Guardrails

- Never record without the information sheet having been explained.
- Health, family and money matters that come up stay in the recording and transcript only; they never go into summaries, the vault or teaching materials.
- No clip is used anywhere until the log shows the matching consent choice.

## Verification

After each interview, check that the log row, the WAV file name and the signed form all show the same interview number and date.
