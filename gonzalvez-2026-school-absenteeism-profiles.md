# Cross-Cultural Perspectives on School Absenteeism: Comparative Profiles from Italy, Spain, and Turkey

Gonzálvez C, Pérez-Marco M, Fuster A, and colleagues. *Continuity in Education*, 2026.

## Summary

The authors used one questionnaire covering truancy and school refusal with pupils in three countries and grouped them into four profiles: no absenteeism problem, a truancy profile, a school refusal profile and a mixed profile. The country differences in profile prevalence were not statistically significant; a gender difference appeared in one country only.

## Why It Matters

Useful vocabulary for the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]] and for the spring plateau ([[why-did-year-10-attendance-stall-in-the-spring-term]]). Our [[persistent-absence]] count lumps together pupils who skip school and pupils who find it hard to come in at all. The huddle treats them differently in practice; this paper is a reason to say so explicitly and to record the type of absence pattern in the huddle log, not just the figure.

## Caveats

- Self-report questionnaire; the profiles are statistical groupings, not diagnoses.
- No intervention tested, so it does not say what works for each profile.
