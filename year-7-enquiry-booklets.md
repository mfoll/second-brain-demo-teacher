# Year 7 Enquiry Booklets

The five printed Year 7 booklets, version 2 (July 2026).

## Summary

Enquiries 1 to 5, about 24 pages each, built to the [[enquiry-booklet-style-guide]]. Version 2 rewrote booklets 2 and 4 for reading level and replaced the assessed task in booklet 3. Printing: about 250 copies of each, 480 GBP.

## Why It Matters

The template for every later booklet. Teachers' most common request after a year: a one-page teacher overview, now in each folder.
