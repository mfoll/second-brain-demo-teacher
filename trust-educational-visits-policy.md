# Trust Educational Visits Policy

Wyvernmoor Learning Trust policy for educational visits, version adopted September 2024, reviewed every two years. Read in full before planning the first Tarnholm trip.

## Summary

Sets the approval levels, the role of the educational visits coordinator, the minimum staffing ratios, first aid cover, the risk assessment standard and what must happen before, during and after a visit.

## Key Details

- Minimum ratios for secondary residentials: one adult to fifteen, at least two adults, and mixed-gender staffing when the group is mixed. Higher ratios set by the leader's risk assessment ([[supervision-ratio]]).
- At least one qualified first aider per activity group when groups split.
- Adventurous activities run by a licensed or accredited provider; for Tarnholm the centre's instructors meet this.
- Residentials need governor approval at least eight weeks before departure.
- An evaluation is required within one month of return.
- Inclusion: cost must not be a barrier; schools should operate a bursary or hardship route. This line was useful at the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]].

## Why It Matters

The baseline for every decision in [[plan-a-residential-visit]]. Where our practice is stricter (ratio, head counts underground) the risk assessment says why.
