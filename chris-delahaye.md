# Chris Delahaye

Chair of the Friends of Corrandale School, the parent-teacher association. He appears here only in that role.

## Role

Leads the Friends' committee, which raises money through the summer fair, the quiz night and the uniform sale, and decides grants to departments and trips at its termly meetings.

## How We Work Together

- He secured the 1,200 GBP Friends grant towards Tarnholm bursary places in December 2025 ([[friends-of-corrandale-bursary-grant-2025]]). The committee's only condition was that the money funded places, not general costs, and that the evaluation reported how many.
- Witnessed the trip lottery draw on 12 December 2025 ([[tarnholm-lottery-draw-2025-12-12]]), at my request, so families could hear from someone outside the staff that it was fair.
- The Friends' feedback that Year 10 families heard from school mainly when something went wrong was one reason I started the [[year-10-newsletter]].
- Gave parent-side feedback during the [[parents-evening-review]]; he favoured keeping an in-person option.

## Current Threads

- A 2027 bursary contribution, on the agenda for the Friends' meeting on 10 November 2026 ([[can-the-bursary-fund-cover-2027-places]]).
