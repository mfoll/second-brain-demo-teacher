# 10H1 Migration Scheme Of Work Autumn 2026

My scheme for the first term of Migration, Empire and People with 10H1, one of the two pilot classes (Ruth teaches the other, 10H3). We share the scheme; Ruth wrote units 3 and 4.

## Key Details

- Unit 1 (September-October): c790-1500, invasion and settlement, 7 lessons.
- Unit 2 (October-December): 1500-1700, refugees, trade and the first colonies, 9 lessons.
- Each unit opens with two timeline lessons, following the department's Key Stage 3 practice.
- One 8-mark and one 16-mark practice question per unit, marked against the specimen mark scheme.
- Booklet for unit 1 cut from 14 to 9 pages after the first draft ([[cognitive-load]]).

## Why It Matters

The working document for 10H1's pilot. The March 2027 mocks will decide whether the whole department switches in September 2027, so this is the scheme being tested.

## Caveats

No Merrow exemplars until spring 2027. Retrieval questions for the units are not written yet: [[does-the-migration-pilot-need-its-own-quiz-questions]].
