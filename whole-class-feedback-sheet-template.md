# Whole-Class Feedback Sheet Template

Version 2 of the one-page sheet, in use since 23 May 2025 and adopted for Years 7-9 from September 2025.

## Key Details

Four boxes:

1. What went well across the class (two or three points, with an example phrase).
2. Common misconceptions to correct.
3. Literacy: spellings and sentence errors seen in several books.
4. Redraft task: pupils choose one paragraph to improve, with a model sentence.

Plus a margin for the three or four books I need to talk to individually.

## Why It Matters

The working tool of the [[whole-class-feedback-trial]] and of [[run-whole-class-feedback]]. Replaced [[whole-class-feedback-sheet-v1]], which had a praise box nobody filled in.
