# Ropeworks Site Land-Use Maps

Two land-use maps of the canal basin and ropeworks site that [[luis-ortega]] uses in Year 9 geography: one from the 1960s town plan and one from a 2000s regeneration survey.

## Summary

The 1960s map shows the full 400-yard ropewalk, twine sheds and rail siding. The 2000s map shows housing on most of the site, a retail park, and two surviving buildings by the basin.

## Why It Matters

Brought to the first planning session ([[local-history-enquiry-planning-2026-01-12]]). They anchor the site walk and the final lesson on what should happen to the remaining buildings.

## Caveats

Copies for teaching only; originals held by the Archives Centre and the planning department.

## Source Location

Geography department shared drive, Year 9 land-use folder; printed copies in my enquiry folder.
