# Year 10 Attendance Review 2026-02-10

Mid-year review of the [[year-10-attendance-drive]], Tuesday 10 February 2026, after the huddle. Me, [[donna-whitlock]], [[marek-nowicki]], with [[adeel-chaudhry]] for the second half.

## Figures

- Year 10 attendance 92.6% year to date, up from 91.4% in October.
- 26 pupils persistently absent, down from 31.
- First-day calls made for 89% of unexplained first-day absences across tutor groups, ranging from 78% to 96%.
- Since the start of January, weekly attendance had flattened at around 92%.

## Discussion

- The autumn gain came mostly from pupils who were drifting (between 90% and 93%), not from the persistent group.
- The January flattening: winter illness, a cluster of pupils with long-term difficulties that calls do not touch, and tutors' first-day calls slipping after Christmas. Not settled; see [[why-did-year-10-attendance-stall-in-the-spring-term]].
- Stage 2 attendance letters had produced no measurable change in the 14 cases where they were sent, and two families complained about the tone.

## Decisions

- Keep the huddle and first-day calls; re-launch the calls with tutors at the 23 February briefing.
- Drop the stage 2 letter for Year 10; replace it with a meeting offer from Donna.
- Adeel agreed the 94% target stays, though he and I both doubted it would be reached.
