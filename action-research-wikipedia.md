# Action Research

English Wikipedia article on action research.

## Summary

Describes action research as a research approach, mainly in the social sciences, that seeks change by taking action and studying it at the same time, linked by critical reflection. The term is credited to Kurt Lewin in the 1940s, who described a repeating cycle of planning, acting and finding out what the action did.

## Why It Matters

Gave Ewan the honest label for the attendance drive in the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]]: a practitioner cycle of plan, act, check, adjust, run by the people doing the work. That framing lets section 4 say plainly what cannot be claimed without a comparison group ([[how-do-we-measure-attendance-impact-without-a-control-group]]) without pretending the drive was an evaluation.

## Caveats

The article covers many traditions of action research. The programme only asks for evidence of impact, not a formal method.
