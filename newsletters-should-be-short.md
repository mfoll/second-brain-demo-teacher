# Newsletters Should Be Short

## Stance

A school newsletter to families should fit on one phone screen with one scroll: about 400 words, the important date first, one item per heading.

## Why I Think This

- Ten years on a weekly paper: the first paragraph is all most readers get. Families reading on a phone at the bus stop are no different.
- The first Year 10 issue ran to 1,100 words. Ledgerwise showed most opens lasted under a minute. From issue two the newsletter has been about 400 words, and the open rate held at around 58%.
- Every extra item makes the important item harder to find.

## What Would Change My Mind

Families asking for more detail, through the Friends or in the survey. So far the request has been the opposite: earlier dates, fewer words.

## Implications

Anything that needs more than two sentences gets its own letter or a link.
