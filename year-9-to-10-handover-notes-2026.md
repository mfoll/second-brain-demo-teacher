# Year 9 To 10 Handover Notes 2026

My notes from the handover meeting with the head of Year 9 on 9 June 2026, kept as counts and themes. The pupil-level handover sheet is in [[ledgerwise]]; anything sensitive was handed over by the DSL through [[lanternlog]], not in this meeting.

## Key Details

- Cohort: 212 pupils in eight tutor groups.
- Attendance in the summer term of Year 9: 93.8%, with 24 pupils below 90%.
- Behaviour: two tutor groups with a pattern of low-level disruption in Year 9; both get an experienced tutor in Year 10.
- Pupils with access arrangements or support plans: counts passed to [[sian-pritchard]] for exams planning.
- Themes the Year 9 team raised: anxiety about the jump to exam courses; friendship groups split by option choices; a group of pupils who respond well to sport, which supports the tutor-time competition.

## Why It Matters

Put the 24 below-90% pupils on the huddle list from the first week of September, the main change from last year ([[attendance-drive-lessons-2025-26]]).
