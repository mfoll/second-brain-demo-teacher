# Layered Consent

Consent split into separate, independently answered choices for each use of a recording or record, instead of one signature covering everything.

## In practice

The [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]] asks three yes/no questions: archive, web, education. Naming and closure are separate again. An interviewee can say yes to the archive and no to the web, and that is a normal answer, not a refusal.

## Why it matters

- People agree to bundles without reading them. Separate questions make each use visible.
- A no to one use does not cost the project the whole interview.
- It makes later decisions checkable: the interview log can answer "may we use this in class?" row by row.

## Costs

- Longer forms and longer conversations at the end of an interview.
- More states to track. The log and the archive copy must agree.
- New uses that nobody foresaw (a podcast, a museum display) still need re-consent.

## Elsewhere

Pupil interview projects use a cut-down version: the parent ticks "class only" or "class and school display". Used in the [[forum-conference-workshop]] template.
