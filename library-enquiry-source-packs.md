# Library Enquiry Source Packs

Boxed source packs in the library, one per Key Stage 3 enquiry, assembled by [[bethan-lloyd-hughes]] with the redirected faculty capital money (about 3,600 GBP of the original 4,000).

## Summary

Each box: 6 to 10 short books at mixed reading levels, a folder of printed sources, and a list of approved websites. Fifteen boxes planned; 11 ready as of October 2025, the rest finished by July 2026. Includes a class set of a local history of Corrandale for Year 9.

## Why It Matters

What we bought instead of textbooks ([[textbook-procurement]]). Booklets carry the core text; the boxes give range and extension, as audit recommendation 7 asked.

## Source Location

School library, history shelves, labelled by year and enquiry number.
