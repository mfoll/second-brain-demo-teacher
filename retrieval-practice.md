# Retrieval Practice

Everything in the vault about making pupils recall what they learned rather than re-read it: the research, the quiz bank, how I run quizzes and where it has gone wrong.

## What I mean by it

Pulling knowledge back out of memory, with no notes open, is what makes it stick. In my lessons that means a five-question [[low-stakes-quizzing|low-stakes quiz]] at the start of every lesson, mixing last lesson, last week and last term, and self-marked straight away. It is not a test and the marks are not recorded centrally; see [[retrieval-quizzes-should-stay-low-stakes]].
