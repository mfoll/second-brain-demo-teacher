# Interleaving

Mixing different topics or kinds of problem within one practice session, rather than practising one kind in a block. Pupils have to decide which knowledge applies, which is what an exam asks of them.

In history I use it in two ways. Quizzes mix units: a Weimar question next to an Elizabethan one. And timed-essay practice in Year 11 rotates question types and papers so pupils do not do six 16-mark Medicine questions in a row. The cost is that it feels messier and pupils score lower in the moment; that is part of [[desirable-difficulty]]. I do not interleave in the first teaching of a topic, only in revision and retrieval.
