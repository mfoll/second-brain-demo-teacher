# Should Revision Sessions Be Compulsory?

Should after-school history revision be compulsory for some Year 11 pupils, for example those below grade 4 in the March mocks?

## Arguments so far

- For: attendance fell to 12-17 by April 2026 ([[revision-session-attendance-2026]]); the pupils who most needed it were least likely to come.
- Against: compulsory sessions after school clash with jobs, caring and buses; Ruth thinks it turns revision into detention. Adeel's view as deputy head is that compulsory after-school sessions need parental agreement.
- Middle option I prefer: invite named groups personally, by letter home, with a fixed calendar, and track attendance as counts.

Decide by the January department meeting, as part of [[revision-programme-2027]].
