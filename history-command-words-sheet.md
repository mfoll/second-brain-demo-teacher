# History Command Words Sheet

The one-page list of Merrow history command words I gave Hannah Greaves for her study skills session 5, September 2026.

- **Describe** (4 marks): two features, each with a supporting detail.
- **Explain why** (8 marks): two causes, each explained with knowledge and linked to the outcome.
- **How useful** (8 or 12 marks): content, provenance and own knowledge for each source.
- **How far do you agree** (16 marks): both sides, then a judgement on "how far", with a reason for the weighting. One side alone stays in Level 2.

Same wording on the back of every 11H1 booklet and on the whole-class feedback sheets, so pupils hear one version.
