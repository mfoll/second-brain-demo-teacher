# Whole-Class Feedback Marking Time Log

My stopwatch log of marking sessions for Year 9 books, spring term 2025 (written comments) against summer term 2025 (whole-class sheets).

## Key Details

- Spring term, written comments: 8 sets logged, median 2 h 40 min per set of about 30 books.
- Summer term, sheets: 9 sets logged, median 55 min per set, including writing the sheet.
- Range for sheets: 40 min to 1 h 20 min; the long ones were the first two.

## Why It Matters

The main number in [[whole-class-feedback-trial-findings]] and the reason the department adopted the method.

## Caveats

Self-timed, one teacher, two classes. Spring and summer pieces were different tasks.
