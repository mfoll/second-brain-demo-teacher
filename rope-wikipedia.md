# Rope

English Wikipedia article on rope.

## Summary

Defines rope as yarns, plies, fibres or strands twisted or braided together into a larger, stronger form with high tensile strength, used for pulling and lifting, and distinguishes it from thinner cord, string and twine. The article goes on to cover fibres, construction and the history of rope-making.

## Why It Matters

Read before interviewing the ropewalk foreman on 14 May 2025 so that Ewan could follow the technical vocabulary (yarns, strands, laying) without stopping the interview to ask. It also explains to pupils why a ropewalk needed to be so long. See [[corrandale-ropeworks]].
