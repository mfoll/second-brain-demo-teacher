# Entry Routine

The fixed sequence a class follows from the door to the first task, taught explicitly and repeated until it runs without instruction.

## Corrandale history version

1. Teacher at the door, greets by name.
2. Pupils go straight to the seating plan.
3. Retrieval quiz is already on the board; books out, quiz started in silence.
4. Register taken while they work.
5. Quiz answers checked by cold-calling within five minutes.

## Why it matters

Most low-level disruption in a lesson starts in the first three minutes. A routine removes decisions from that window for the pupils and for the teacher. It is the first target in any trainee's behaviour work and was the root of the 2025-26 support plan.

## Teaching it

Explain it, model it, practise it with a deliberate reset if it goes wrong, and keep it identical every lesson for the first half-term. See [[yusuf-first-half-term-plan]] for the week-one version.
