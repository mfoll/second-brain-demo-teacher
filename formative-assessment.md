# Formative assessment

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on formative assessment (assessment for learning): formal and informal checks during learning, used to adjust teaching and give pupils feedback they can act on, as opposed to summative assessment, which measures outcomes for accountability.

## Why It Matters

I used its distinction when writing the proposal for the [[whole-class-feedback-trial]] in April 2025. Written comments in Key Stage 3 books were sitting in an awkward middle: too slow to change the next lesson, too individual to change the teaching. The feedback sheet is formative in the article's sense, because the next lesson starts with the redraft.

## Caveats

Broad and definitional. It does not settle whether written or whole-class feedback works better; our trial data did that for us, within its limits.
