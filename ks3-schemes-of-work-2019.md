# KS3 Schemes Of Work 2019

The previous Key Stage 3 schemes, written in 2019 and taught until July 2025 (Year 7) and July 2026 (Years 8 and 9).

## Summary

A chronological survey from 1066 to the late twentieth century, one topic per half-term, 168 lessons across three years. Heavy on narrative, light on extended writing; a textbook-based homework each week.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for the [[diverse-curriculum-audit]] and the thing Desmond's chronology argument defends: whatever its faults, it told the story in order.
