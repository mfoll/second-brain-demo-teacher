# Low-Stakes Quizzing

Frequent short quizzes whose results do not count for anything: not reported, not used for grades or sets, often self-marked. The point is the act of retrieval and quick feedback on what is not yet secure.

What changes with the stakes is behaviour. When a quiz counts, pupils cram, copy or avoid; when it does not, they guess honestly, which tells the teacher more. At Corrandale the history department agreed in 2025 that [[retrieval-quiz-bank]] results stay with the teacher. My stance on keeping it that way is [[retrieval-quizzes-should-stay-low-stakes]].

Practical markers: five questions ([[how-many-retrieval-questions-per-lesson]]), answers shown straight away, no names on the board, no rewards for top scores.
