# Teacher Education

English Wikipedia article on teacher education.

## Summary

Covers the programmes, policies and provision that prepare prospective teachers with the knowledge, skills and approaches they need in the classroom, school and wider community, and names those who train them as teacher educators.

## Why It Matters

Background reading when Ewan became a school-based mentor and, in Esterbrook's terms, a teacher educator for the first time. It helped him see the placement as one part of a programme with university-based study alongside it, which is why the weekly meetings now ask what the trainee is reading at Esterbrook that week.

## Caveats

International overview. The placement pattern and standards that apply here are Esterbrook's: [[esterbrook-placement-handbook-2025-26]].
