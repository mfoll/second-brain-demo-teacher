# Plan An Enquiry Unit

How I plan a new Key Stage 3 enquiry, from question to printed booklet. Written after the Year 7 units and used for Years 8 and 9 and the ropeworks enquiry. About 15 to 25 hours per unit.

## Inputs

- The slot in [[ks3-curriculum-map-2026-27]]: year, term, second-order concept.
- The audit recommendations ([[diverse-curriculum-audit]]).
- What pupils already know from earlier enquiries.

## Steps

1. **Write the [[enquiry-question]].** Test it against the six criteria in that note. Show it to Priyanka before writing anything else.
2. **Write the final assessed task and its four-point descriptor.** The task is the question. Decide what a "secure" answer must contain.
3. **List the substantive knowledge** a secure answer needs. That is the knowledge organiser. Cut anything that does not serve the answer ([[substantive-and-disciplinary-knowledge]]).
4. **Plan lessons backwards** from the task: knowledge lessons first, then factor or evidence lessons, then a lesson that weighs them, then the assessed piece.
5. **Choose sources** with [[bethan-lloyd-hughes]]. At least three named ordinary people. For any oral history clip, check consent in the [[ropeworks-interview-log]] first.
6. **Write retrieval quizzes** (six to eight) in [[quillyard]], including two chronology questions each.
7. **Draft the booklet** to the [[enquiry-booklet-style-guide]].
8. **Reading check** by Bethan; three weeks before printing.
9. **Teacher overview page**: one side, question, sequence, common misconceptions.
10. **Print and teach once** before revising.

## Verification

- Every lesson has a "back to the question" box.
- The knowledge organiser fits on one page.
- Under 24 pages.

## Do not

Do not use clips, names or photographs of living people without checked consent for education use.
