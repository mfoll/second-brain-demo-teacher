# First-Day Response

Contacting home on the first morning of an unexplained absence, by phone, from someone the family knows: at Corrandale, the tutor.

## Idea

An unexplained absence is cheapest to address on day one. A call from the tutor does three things at once: it checks the pupil is safe, it tells the family the school noticed, and it starts a conversation before a pattern sets in. The automated text from [[ledgerwise]] does the first job only.

## How We Do It

- The register closes at 09:00; Ledgerwise flags unexplained absences by 09:15.
- The tutor calls by 10:00, using the [[first-day-call-script]]. If the tutor is teaching periods 1 and 2, [[donna-whitlock]] calls.
- Outcome recorded in Ledgerwise in one line. No contact by 12:00 goes to [[marek-nowicki]].
- If anything in the call raises a welfare worry, it is logged in [[lanternlog]] for the DSL.

## What We Saw

Completion across tutor groups was 89% in February 2026, from 78% to 96%. It slipped after Christmas and recovered after a re-launch. Whether calls changed attendance on their own is not known: [[does-first-day-calling-change-attendance]].

## Limits

First-day calls catch drift and one-off absences. They do little for pupils with long-term difficulties, where absence is the symptom rather than the problem. Those cases need the huddle and, often, outside help.
