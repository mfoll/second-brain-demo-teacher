# Medicine Through Time Scheme Of Work

The department's Year 10 scheme for Medicine Through Time c1250-present, written in 2017 and lightly revised each year since.

## Summary

38 lessons in four periods (medieval, Renaissance, eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, modern), plus the historic environment study of a hospital site. Factor tracker for individuals, institutions, science and technology, and attitudes.

## Why It Matters

Taught in 2026-27 by Desmond (10H2), Priyanka (10H4) and Tomasz (10H5) while the pilot runs. If the department switches in 2027 it is retired after Year 11 sits it in summer 2028.
