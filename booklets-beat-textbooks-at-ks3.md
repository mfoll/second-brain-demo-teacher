# Booklets Beat Textbooks At KS3

## Stance

For Key Stage 3 history, in-house enquiry booklets are better than any commercial textbook we looked at, and cheaper over five years.

## Scope

Key Stage 3. At Key Stage 4 a good board-endorsed textbook is worth having alongside booklets, because pupils revise from it.

## Why I think this

- Fit: each booklet is built around one [[enquiry-question]]; no textbook was.
- Revision: we rewrote two Year 7 booklets after one year for reading level. A textbook locks content for years.
- Cost: about 1,450 GBP a year in printing against 3,900 GBP up front plus replacements ([[ks3-textbook-quote-2025]]).
- Range: we can include local and less-heard voices, which the [[diverse-curriculum-audit]] found missing from the series we shortlisted.

## What Would Change My Mind

If writing time stops being available. Booklets cost staff hours that do not show in the budget: roughly 70 hours for Year 7 alone. If the department loses a teacher or a year's goodwill, a textbook becomes the safer choice.

## Implications

Keep a booklet style guide so anyone can maintain them ([[enquiry-booklet-style-guide]]).
