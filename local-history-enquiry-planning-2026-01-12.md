# Local History Enquiry Planning 2026-01-12

First planning session for the Year 9 enquiry "Why did the ropeworks close?" with [[luis-ortega]], after school in the geography room.

## Agreed

- Six lessons plus an assessed piece, spring term 2027, the same weeks as Luis's Year 9 unit on the town's land use.
- Sources: consented interview clips from [[ropeworks-voices]], company records at the Archives Centre ([[corrandale-archives-ropeworks-catalogue]]), Courier coverage ([[courier-ropeworks-closure-1986]]), and Luis's land-use maps of the site from the 1960s and 2000s.
- A site walk in March 2027 to the canal basin and the line of the old ropewalk.
- Final lesson hands over to geography: what should happen to the remaining buildings?

## Constraint raised

Only interviews whose consent covers education use can be used. At this point that meant only the interviews recorded on version 2 of the form since September 2025; the 7 earlier ones on the 2019 form were out. I said I would confirm the rule with [[maureen-castleford]] before writing anything. Since then the usable set has grown to 11 ([[local-history-enquiry]]).
