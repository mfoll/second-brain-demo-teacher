# Social and Emotional Skills and Different Bullying Behavior of Students: Serial Mediating Role of Perceived Relationships with Teachers and Sense of Belonging at School

Li J, Chen Z, Wang X. *BMC Psychology*, 2026.

## Summary

Using an international survey of 3,580 fifteen-year-olds in one city, the authors linked pupils' social and emotional skills to bullying behaviour. Perceived relationships with teachers and sense of belonging at school sat between skills and bullying in their model. Building skills alone, without attention to teacher-pupil relationships and school climate, was not reliably linked to less exposure to traditional bullying.

## Why It Matters

I read it while designing the [[settling-in-survey-2026]], looking for a reason to ask about belonging and the new tutor separately. The study's age group matches Year 10 exactly. It supported spending tutor-team time on relationships in the first weeks rather than on a skills programme, and it is cited in [[settling-in-survey-findings-2026]] with the caveat below.

## Caveats

- Cross-sectional data; mediation here is statistical, not causal.
- One city in a very different system.
