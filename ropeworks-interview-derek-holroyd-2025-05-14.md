# Ropeworks Interview RV03: Derek Holroyd

Interview with [[derek-holroyd]], former ropewalk foreman, 14 May 2025, society room, 94 minutes. Consent on the 2019 form: archive and society publications; named.

## Summary

- Started in the ropewalk at 16 in 1962, foreman from 1974.
- Detailed account of how a rope was laid on the walk and why the walk had to be so long. Good on the noise, the smell of tarred hemp and the teams' rivalry.
- Describes the 1984 spinning machines as "too late and in the wrong shed". His view of the closure: the parent group had decided by 1983.
- Names two managers; those passages are flagged as not for clips.

## Follow-up

- Summary sent to Nisha Kapoor on 19 May.
- Strong material for the closure question in the Year 9 enquiry, but the 2019 form does not cover education use. Re-consent letter sent 5 October 2026; not yet returned.
