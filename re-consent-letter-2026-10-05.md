# Ropeworks Voices Re-Consent Letter

The letter sent on 5 October 2026 to the seven interviewees recorded under the 2019 form (RV01 to RV07), signed by Maureen Castleford and Ewan.

## Summary

One page in plain language. Explains that the society now offers three separate choices; that nothing changes unless they return the form; that saying no to the new uses changes nothing about their existing deposit. Encloses [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]] with their interview number pre-filled and a stamped return envelope. Offers a home visit or phone call if they want to talk it through.

## Why It Matters

It is the only route to using RV01 to RV07 in Year 9 lessons: [[can-2019-form-ropeworks-interviews-be-used-in-lessons]]. Ewan wrote it to avoid pressure: no deadline, no mention of the school's plans beyond one sentence.

## Caveats

One letter went to a new address supplied through the society secretary. One reminder only; nobody is chased beyond that.
