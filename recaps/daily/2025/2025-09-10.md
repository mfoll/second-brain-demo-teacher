# Wednesday 10 September 2025

## New consent form for Ropeworks Voices

The society committee adopted [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2|version 2 of the consent form]] in the evening, with separate archive, web and education choices. The case was the July conversation in which an interviewee asked whether pupils would hear her and the old form could not say. A single "all uses" box was proposed and turned down; the reasoning is in [[layered-consent|Layered consent]]. Going back to the first seven interviewees was deferred until the school's enquiry is confirmed. Minutes: [[society-committee-meeting-2025-09-10|committee meeting]].

## First mentor meeting of the year

First weekly meeting with the new trainee, after school. Mostly timetable, the behaviour policy and the Kittow placement dates. Agreed Wednesday period 5 as the standing slot. See [[trainee-mentoring-2025-26|Trainee Mentoring 2025-26]].
