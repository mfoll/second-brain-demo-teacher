# Wednesday 25 June 2025

## Chronology and the new Key Stage 3

At the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25|curriculum review]] Desmond Hartigan argued that stand-alone enquiries leave pupils without a sense of chronology. Ruth and Priyanka disagreed in different ways; I thought he had a point the draft did not answer. We considered leaving the draft as it was, going back to a chronological survey, and a single chronology unit in Year 7, and rejected all three.

## The compromise

Every year group will start with two timeline lessons, and pupils will keep a cumulative timeline in the back of their books, adding each enquiry. Desmond accepted it for now, with a review after a year of Year 7 teaching. Outline to write over the summer ([[ks3-timeline-lessons-outline|timeline lessons]]).
