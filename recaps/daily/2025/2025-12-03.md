# Wednesday 3 December 2025

## Tarnholm places: threshold dropped

Presented the [[trip-places-equity-analysis|equity analysis]] at the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03|senior leadership meeting]]. The 95% attendance threshold for the residential was dropped: places go by lottery among all 61 applicants, each place conditional on a behaviour agreement, with the Friends' grant and the bursary fund paying for 11 places. Adeel said the figures changed his mind; his worry had been fairness to pupils who attend every day, and he accepted that the lottery treats every applicant the same. Gillian asked about a 90% threshold as a middle way; it still excluded 6 of 17 bursary applicants, so it was not taken up.

## Letter to applicants

Agreed to write to all applicants by Friday explaining the lottery, and to draw it on 12 December with Rebecca Tull and an outside witness. Chris Delahaye is the obvious witness.

## Huddle carry-over

Short huddle catch-up with Donna at lunch: first-day call completion is good in most groups but slipping in two. Plan to name the routine again at Monday's briefing rather than chase individual tutors.
