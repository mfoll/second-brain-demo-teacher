# Wednesday 13 May 2026

## Migration switch cut back to a pilot

The [[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13|department meeting]] partly reversed the [[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04|February vote]] to move all five Year 10 classes to Migration, Empire and People. From September only 10H1 (mine) and 10H3 (Ruth's) pilot it; the other three classes stay on Medicine Through Time. The whole department switches in September 2027 if the pilot classes' March 2027 mocks are at least as good.

## Why

Merrow's revised specimen papers came out only on 28 April and changed what the booklets need; at last week's [[migration-booklet-check-in-2026-05-06|check-in]] they were about 40% drafted. Kofi Asamoah asked whether we could show the switch would not cost grades, and we could not. Keeping the full switch and reverting to Medicine were both considered and rejected.

## Desmond

Desmond Hartigan, the only vote against in February, accepted the pilot on condition that the comparison is fair. I said in the meeting that he had been right about the specimen papers. How "at least as good" is measured is still open ([[how-do-we-compare-pilot-and-medicine-mock-results|comparison question]]).

## Afterwards

Ruth and I agreed to finish booklets 3 to 5 for the two pilot classes by February half-term 2027. Priyanka is drafting the parent letter. Wrote up what I got wrong in [[pilot-before-a-whole-department-switch|my note on piloting]].
