# Monday 9 February 2026

## Trainee support plan

After school, with Laura Penhaligon on video and Rosalind Kerr in the room, we agreed a support plan for the trainee's Year 9 classes. Three targets only: entry routine, cold-calling, consequences given as the policy says. Support is weekly co-planning and Ewan in the room for the first ten minutes of each Year 9 lesson for three weeks. Moving the trainee off 9H5 was discussed and rejected, because it would remove the problem without building the skill. Review on 16 March.
