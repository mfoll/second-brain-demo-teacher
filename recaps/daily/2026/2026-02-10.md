# Tuesday 10 February 2026

## Attendance mid-year review

After the huddle, the [[year-10-attendance-review-2026-02-10|mid-year review]] with Donna and Marek, Adeel joining later. Year 10 is at 92.6% with 26 pupils persistently absent, against 91.4% and 31 in October. The gain is mostly from pupils who were drifting; since January the line has gone flat.

## Why the flattening

We talked through winter illness, first-day calls slipping after Christmas, and a remaining caseload of pupils with long-term difficulties. No agreement on which matters most; kept open as [[why-did-year-10-attendance-stall-in-the-spring-term|a question]].

## Letters

Stage 2 letters did nothing measurable in 14 cases and drew two complaints. Decided to replace them with a meeting offer from Donna; Adeel confirmed the trust policy allows it if the offer is in writing.

## Target

The 94% target stays. Adeel and I both said we doubt it will be reached, and agreed to report the persistent absence count alongside the average from now on.
