# Wednesday 4 February 2026

## Department votes to switch thematic study

The [[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04|department meeting]] voted 5-1 to replace Medicine Through Time with Migration, Empire and People for all five Year 10 classes from September 2026. Desmond Hartigan voted against and asked for his reasons to be minuted: no specimen papers yet, steady Medicine results, and the workload landing on everyone at once, including an early career teacher.

## My estimate

I put five booklets at about 75 hours across the department, ready by July. I am writing the rota by 25 February. Amelia asked whether we could pilot first; it was not pursued because nobody knew whether the board allowed two options in one year group.
