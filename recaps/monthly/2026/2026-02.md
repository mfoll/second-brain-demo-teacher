# February 2026

A busy first half of the spring term, with three separate pieces of work coming to a head in one week.

## The thematic study vote

The [[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04|department meeting of 4 February]] voted to move all Year 10 classes from Medicine Through Time to Migration, Empire and People from September, with Desmond Hartigan against and his reasons minuted. I owned the booklet estimate and the writing rota. A pilot was mentioned and not pursued. This decision was later cut back to a two-class pilot at the [[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13|meeting of 13 May]]; current state in [[thematic-study-switch]]. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-02-04|4 February]].

## Support plan for the trainee

On 9 February Laura Penhaligon, Rosalind Kerr and I agreed a [[trainee-support-plan-2026-02-09|support plan]] for the trainee's Year 9 classes: three targets and weekly co-planning, with a review in March. I acknowledged that the pattern had been visible since January, which became the answer to [[when-should-a-trainee-support-plan-start|when a support plan should start]]. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-02-09|9 February]].

## Attendance at mid-year

The [[year-10-attendance-review-2026-02-10|mid-year review on 10 February]] showed real progress since October but a flat line since January. We could not agree why, so it stays an [[why-did-year-10-attendance-stall-in-the-spring-term|open question]]. Stage 2 letters were replaced by a written meeting offer, and the persistent absence count now sits beside the average in every report. The target stayed, with both Adeel and me doubtful it would be met. This is the material for the [[middle-leadership-programme|Middle Leadership Programme]] project. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-02-10|10 February]].

## Carried into March

The migration writing rota, the trainee review on 16 March, the first full use of the [[department-moderation-protocol|moderation protocol]] on the March mocks, and the second Year 10 parents' evening on 12 March.
