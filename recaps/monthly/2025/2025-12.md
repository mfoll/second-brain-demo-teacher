# December 2025

Two decisions shaped the month: how Tarnholm places would be shared out, and how far to trust our own mock marking.

## Tarnholm places without an attendance test

The month opened with the [[trip-places-equity-analysis|equity analysis]], written to answer the attendance threshold floated at the November senior leadership meeting. It went to the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03|meeting of 3 December]], which dropped the threshold in favour of a lottery with a [[behaviour-agreement|behaviour agreement]] and bursary support for the places families could not fund. Adeel Chaudhry changed his view on the figures; a lower threshold was raised and set aside. The [[tarnholm-lottery-draw-2025-12-12|draw on 12 December]] went ahead as planned, with Chris Delahaye as outside witness. See [[recaps/daily/2025/2025-12-03|3 December]].

## Mock moderation and what followed

The [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|moderation with Vikram Iyer on 10 December]] found the department generous on the 16-mark question, my own marks most of all. We agreed to re-mark every 16-mark answer before results went to pupils in January, and I took on drafting the [[department-moderation-protocol|moderation protocol]]. For 11H2 it meant the class profile would fall and that I should stop giving informal grades until the re-mark was done. See [[recaps/daily/2025/2025-12-10|10 December]].

## Year 10 attendance

The weekly huddle kept running under the [[year-10-attendance-drive|attendance drive]]. First-day calls were holding in most tutor groups but slipping in two; the plan was to restate the routine at a briefing rather than chase individual tutors.

## Carried into January

The protocol draft for the department meeting of 14 January, the 11H2 re-mark, and behaviour agreements and deposits for Tarnholm due back by 16 January.
