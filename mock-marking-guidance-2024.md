# Mock Marking Guidance 2024

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 2026-01-14 by the [[department-moderation-protocol]]. Kept to show what changed.

The one-page department guidance used for the 2024 and November 2025 mocks. It told markers to "credit relevant knowledge generously in the 16-mark question" and had no standardisation step or second marking. That instruction is the most likely source of the generosity found at [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10]]. It predates the board's 2023 mark scheme change and was never updated.
