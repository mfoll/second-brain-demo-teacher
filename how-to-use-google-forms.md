# How to use Google Forms

Google Docs Editors Help, help page for Google Forms (computer version).

## Summary

The page walks through creating a blank form or quiz, creating one from Google Sheets or Google Drive, editing and formatting questions, sending the form to respondents, and reviewing responses as charts or in a linked Google Sheet. Linked pages cover question types, showing questions based on answers, and sharing a form with collaborators.

## Why It Matters

Every Year 10 survey I run is a Google Form in the school account: the [[parents-evening-survey-2025]], the [[settling-in-survey-2026]] and the [[tarnholm-2026-pupil-survey]]. Two things from the help pages I rely on: saving responses to a Sheet so I can aggregate by tutor group and then delete the raw sheet, and question branching so that pupils who say "not sure" about having a trusted adult get a follow-up question. I first used it properly for the parents' evening survey on 28 November 2025.

## Caveats

Product help pages change without notice. Data protection rules on what pupils may be asked and how long raw responses are kept come from the school, not from the tool.
