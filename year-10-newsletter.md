# Year 10 Newsletter

A half-termly newsletter from me to Year 10 families, sent through [[ledgerwise]]. Started in my first week as head of year because families told the Friends of Corrandale School they heard about Year 10 only when something went wrong.

## Outcome

A newsletter goes to every Year 10 family each half-term, with no pupil names, and no photographs unless the pupil's photo consent is recorded in Ledgerwise. It becomes a routine once two cohorts have had a full year of it; then it moves to the [[lead-year-10]] routines and this Project closes (July 2027).

## Current State

- Seven issues sent: six to the 2025-26 cohort, one (16 October 2026) to the new Year 10. Latest is [[year-10-newsletter-autumn-1-2026]].
- Ledgerwise open rate averages 58% across issues, highest for the issue with the trip lottery results (71%).
- Length held at one screen, about 400 words, after the first issue ran to 1,100 and the open-to-end reading data looked poor.

## Scope and Boundaries

What goes in: dates, what pupils are doing in lessons and tutor time, how to contact the year team, celebrations by group or class, never by name. What stays out: anything about an individual pupil, behaviour figures by tutor group, and anything that should be a letter (attendance concerns, trips needing consent).

## Active Workflows

- [[send-the-year-10-newsletter]]: drafting, consent check, translation request, send.
- Photo consent is checked in Ledgerwise for each pupil visible in a photograph; if one pupil has no consent the photo is not used. Settled in [[how-should-the-newsletter-handle-pupil-photos]].
- [[donna-whitlock]] reads each draft for anything that could identify a pupil.
