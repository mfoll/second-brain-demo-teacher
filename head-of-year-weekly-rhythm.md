# Head Of Year Weekly Rhythm

How my week works as head of Year 10 alongside a 70% teaching timetable. Written in November 2025 after the first half-term, when I realised I was answering email in every free period and planning nothing. Revised September 2026.

## Fixed Points

- **Monday 08:30.** Tutor briefing, ten minutes: attendance by group, the week's events, one pastoral reminder.
- **Tuesday 08:10.** Attendance huddle ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]). Actions email to tutors by noon.
- **Wednesday after school.** Fortnightly line meeting with [[adeel-chaudhry]].
- **Thursday.** Year 10 assembly, fortnightly, period 1 slot.
- **Friday period 5.** Behaviour points review with [[donna-whitlock]]: patterns by group, follow-ups for the week ahead.

## Protected Time

- Two free periods a week marked for history planning, with email closed. I lose one most weeks.
- Parent email answered in two slots a day, 12:30 and 15:30, with a 48-hour reply promise in the newsletter.

## What I Dropped

- Checking Ledgerwise behaviour points live during lessons.
- Writing individual replies to newsletter questions; common ones go in the next issue.

## Load

About 6 hours a week beyond the 3 hours of management time on the timetable in 2025-26; closer to 4 in 2026-27 with the routines settled. The trust's workload survey asks for this figure; see [[teacher-workload]].
