# Historiography

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes historiography as the study of historians' methods and, by extension, the body of historical writing on a topic, including how historians have used sources and theories. Notes the debated question of how far historians are shaped by their own groups and loyalties.

## Why It Matters

Background for the Year 9 interpretations enquiries and for the historiography strand I teach in the shared Year 12 group. The point about loyalties is the hook for 'How have historians judged the campaign for the vote?' in Year 8 ([[ks3-curriculum-map-2026-27]]).

## Caveats

Very wide; pupils need examples, not the overview.
