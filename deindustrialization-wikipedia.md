# Deindustrialization

English Wikipedia article on deindustrialization.

## Summary

Defines deindustrialization as the social and economic change that follows the removal or reduction of industrial activity in a country or region, especially heavy industry and manufacturing. The article goes on to discuss explanations and consequences.

## Why It Matters

Read after the archive visit of 21 June 2025, when it was clear the ropeworks closure was one local case of a wider pattern. It gives the Year 9 enquiry its bigger frame: pupils first explain why one works closed, then ask whether Corrandale's story looks like other towns'. Luis Ortega uses the same idea in geography for the town's land use. See [[corrandale-ropeworks]].

## Caveats

General and international. The specific causes at Corrandale come from the company records, the Courier and the interviews, not this article.
