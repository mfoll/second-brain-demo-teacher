# Forum Workshop Feedback Summary

Aggregated feedback forms from the workshop, sent by Ben Akintola on 3 July 2026.

## Summary

29 of 34 attendees returned forms. Usefulness 4.5 out of 5; delivery 4.6. Most valued: the five-second silence drill (mentioned on 17 forms) and the consent template (12). Most common request: more on turning a recording into a written source pupils can analyse (9). Two attendees wanted less of Ewan's newsroom stories.

## Why It Matters

The request for "recording to written source" is the missing piece in the department's own [[local-history-enquiry]] plan. It also shapes any 2027 version.
