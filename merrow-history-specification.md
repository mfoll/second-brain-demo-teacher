# Merrow Certificate in History Specification

The Merrow Examinations Board specification for the Merrow Certificate in History, the qualification every Corrandale pupil sits at the end of Year 11. The department keeps the current PDF and every update on the shared drive; this note records the parts we use and what changed.

## Summary

Two papers, each 1 hour 45 minutes, sat in May and June of Year 11, graded 9 to 1 with grade 4 a standard pass and grade 5 a strong pass.

- **Paper 1:** a thematic study (option A: Medicine Through Time c1250-present; option B: Migration, Empire and People c790-present) and a depth study (we teach Weimar and Nazi Germany 1918-39).
- **Paper 2:** a British depth study (we teach Elizabethan England 1568-1603) and a period study (we teach Conflict and Tension 1945-72).
- Each section has a mix of 4-, 8-, 12- and 16-mark questions, plus source and interpretation questions in the depth studies. The 16-mark question is always an essay on a "how far do you agree" statement, marked on four levels ([[merrow-levels-mark-scheme-16-mark-question]]).

## Why It Matters

Everything in [[year-11-exam-preparation]] plans back from this document. The thematic study choice is the one live department decision: the [[thematic-study-switch]] moves from option A to option B, piloted in 10H1 and 10H3 in 2026-27. The board published revised Migration specimen papers and a mark scheme only on 28 April 2026 ([[merrow-migration-specimen-papers-2026]]), which is part of why the [[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13|May 2026 meeting]] scaled the switch back to a pilot.

## Key Details

- Medicine Through Time has a required historic environment study (we use a hospital site in the specimen list). Migration has its own required site study; we have chosen a port study from the board's list for 2026-27.
- Spelling, punctuation and grammar marks: 4 marks on the depth-study 16-mark question only.
- Paper 1 weighting 50%, Paper 2 50%.
- Grade boundaries vary each year; see [[grade-boundaries]]. Summer 2025 grade 4 boundary was 71 of 168; summer 2026 was 74.
- Options must be the same for all pupils in a centre in a given year unless entered as separate entry codes. [[sunil-varma]] confirmed in May 2026 that two thematic study options in one year group is allowed with two codes, which made the pilot possible.

## Caveats

- The specification sets content, not teaching order or emphasis; examiners' reports ([[merrow-examiners-report-summer-2026]]) tell us more about what is rewarded.
- The April 2026 revision changed Migration content descriptions in three places. Check the version date before reusing older notes.
