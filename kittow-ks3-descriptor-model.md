# Kittow KS3 Descriptor Model

Kittow Academy's Key Stage 3 history assessment model, shared by [[vikram-iyer]] after the trust network meeting of 12 November 2025.

## Summary

Four descriptors per second-order concept, written as pupil-facing 'I can' statements, used on every assessed piece from Year 7 to Year 9. Reported to parents as descriptors with a short comment. Kittow reports no grades before Year 10.

## Why It Matters

The starting point for [[ks3-assessment-model-draft]]. Shows a trust school already reporting without grades, which matters for the argument in [[should-ks3-assessment-use-grades-or-descriptors]].

## Caveats

Shared for internal use only; not to be copied wholesale into our materials.
