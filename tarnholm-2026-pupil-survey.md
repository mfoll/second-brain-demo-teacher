# Tarnholm 2026 Pupil Survey

Ten-question survey of the 52 pupils who went to Tarnholm, run in tutor time on 22 June 2026 in Google Forms. 49 responses.

## Key Details

- Overall rating: 92% good or very good, none poor.
- Highlight (free choice): mine 38, campfire 21, castle 14, river 9.
- Least liked: night 1 food 12, rain 10, coach journey 8.
- "I got to know people in my year I did not know before": 41 of 49 agree.
- "I would recommend it to next year's Year 10": 44 of 49.

## Caveats

Taken in tutor time a week after return; answers visible to neighbours.

- Google Forms, school account; aggregate export in `Tarnholm/2026/Evaluation`.
