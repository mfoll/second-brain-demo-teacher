11H2 (27 pupils) sat the Merrow Certificate in History; results of 2026-08-20 reviewed: 72% at grade 4 or above against 66% predicted.

## Current State

- Closed on 27 August 2026 after the results review with Ruth. Summary in [[exam-season-2026-review]].

## Working Context

The year had three turning points.

1. November 2025 mocks. Marked by me; moderation with [[vikram-iyer]] on [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|10 December]] found the department 2.1 marks generous on the 16-mark question. I re-marked my 16-mark answers down; 11H2's mock profile fell from 70% to 59% at grade 4 or above.

3. Summer: 72%. Most of the gain was on 16-mark answers between March and June, after six weeks of fortnightly timed essays with whole-class feedback.
