# Ropeworks Enquiry Scheme Draft

Draft scheme for Year 9 enquiry 3, 'Why did the ropeworks close?', finished 3 June 2026 and revised after the October re-consent letters.

## Summary

1. The works at its peak: what it made and who worked there.
2. Demand and imports: farms, baler twine and cheaper synthetic competition.
3. The 1971 takeover: decisions move out of town.
4. Short time and falling orders in 1985: workers' voices against the Courier and the company records.
5. The 1984 machines, rescue or run-down? Weighing the causes in a debate.
6. The last shift, and what should happen to the remaining buildings (handover to geography).
7. Assessed piece: the enquiry question.

Interview clips appear in lessons 2 to 6, each preceded by the interviewer's question. The shortlist, with the lesson each clip serves, is in [[clip-selection-for-year-9-enquiry]].

Clip rule written into the scheme: only interviews on version 2 of the consent form with education use, checked in the [[ropeworks-interview-log]]; at present 11 of 19 ([[can-2019-form-ropeworks-interviews-be-used-in-lessons]]).
