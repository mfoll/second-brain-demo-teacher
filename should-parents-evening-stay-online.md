# Should Parents' Evening Stay Online?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Mostly. Since 20 March 2026 Year 10 parents' evenings are video by default with 7-minute slots, plus an in-person block from 17:00 to 18:30 for families who request it when booking.

## Why

- Booking rose from 61% (in person, 2024-25) to 78% (online, November 2025) and 82% (hybrid, March 2026).
- 71% of survey respondents preferred video ([[parents-evening-survey-2025]]).
- The families who most needed a face-to-face conversation were less likely to book a video slot, so the in-person block stays.

Detail: [[parents-evening-trial-findings]]. Decision record: [[parents-evening-review]].

## What Would Reopen It

A fall in booking below 75%, or tutors reporting that difficult conversations are being had badly on video.
