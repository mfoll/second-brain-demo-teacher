# School Bursary Fund Guidance

Corrandale School guidance on the bursary fund for trips and curriculum costs, issued by [[olumide-bankole]], September 2025.

## Key Details

- Eligibility: pupils eligible for free school meals, or families meeting the school's hardship criteria on application.
- Covers up to 100% of the cost of curriculum trips and residentials; uniform and equipment through a separate route.
- Decisions by the business manager, confidential to the family; trip leaders are told only how many places are funded.
- Funded from the school budget line and donations (including the Friends of Corrandale School); underspends on trips may be returned to the fund.

## Why It Matters

Fixed how the 11 Tarnholm bursary places were funded in 2026 and how the 2027 gap is calculated ([[can-the-bursary-fund-cover-2027-places]]).
