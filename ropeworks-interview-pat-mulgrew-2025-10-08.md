# Ropeworks Interview RV09: Pat Mulgrew

Interview with [[pat-mulgrew]], former spinner, 8 October 2025, society room, 71 minutes. Consent on version 2: archive yes, web yes, education yes; named.

## Summary

- Joined the spinning floor in 1971 at 17; one of the first women to move to the new synthetic line in 1979.
- Vivid on the short-time working of 1985: three-day weeks, the queue at the wages office, a sense that "everyone knew and nobody said".
- Disagrees with several of the men's accounts of the closure; thinks the 1984 machines were a real attempt to save the works.
- Asked twice whether young people would hear it, and was pleased that the form now let her say yes.

## Follow-up

- Her account and Derek Holroyd's ([[ropeworks-interview-derek-holroyd-2025-05-14]]) disagree about 1984, which is exactly the kind of evidence the Year 9 enquiry needs. Only hers is usable in class for now.
