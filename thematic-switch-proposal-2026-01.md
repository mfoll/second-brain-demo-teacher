# Thematic Switch Proposal 2026-01

Ruth's one-page proposal, circulated on 30 January 2026, to replace Medicine Through Time with Migration, Empire and People for all Year 10 classes from September 2026.

## Summary

- Reasons: links to Key Stage 3 enquiries and the audit; local relevance; stale Medicine resources.
- Risks named: no specimen papers yet; workload; no endorsed textbook.
- Mitigation: five booklets over the summer term, a training session in July.
- No pilot option was included.

## Why It Matters

What the department voted on, 5-1, on 4 February ([[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04]]). Read now, the risks section was accurate and the mitigation was not.
