# KS3 Timeline Lessons Outline

The two lessons that open each Key Stage 3 year, agreed at the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25|June 2025 review]].

## Lesson 1: the long sweep

- Year 7: c.1000 to the present in six broad periods, each with one image and one sentence. Pupils order cards, then place three family or town events.
- Years 8 and 9: recap last year's five enquiries on the timeline, then extend.
- Ten-question chronology quiz at the end, kept as a baseline.

## Lesson 2: this year's enquiries

- Place the year's five enquiry questions on the timeline.
- Pupils start or update their cumulative timeline in the back of the book ([[cumulative-timeline-template]]).
- Set the rule: every enquiry ends with three dates added.
