# Does Whole-Class Feedback Work For Year 11 Essays?

Key Stage 3 adopted whole-class feedback in September 2025, but Year 11 essays kept an individual comment. Should exam classes move fully to sheets?

## What I know

- In 2025-26 I used a sheet plus one line per essay with 11H2. The 16-mark scores rose most between March and June, during that mix ([[exam-season-2026-review]]). I cannot separate the sheet from the line.
- Pupils in exam classes ask for "my grade and what to do". A one-line target answers that cheaply.
- Desmond is against dropping individual comments in Year 11.

## Next

Keep the mix for 11H1. Compare with Ruth's class, which uses sheets only, at the March 2027 moderation.
