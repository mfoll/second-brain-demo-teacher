# Tarnholm Parents' Meeting 2026-05-06

Information evening for families of the 52 pupils going to Tarnholm, Wednesday 6 May 2026, 18:00 to 19:00, main hall. About 70 adults came, representing 44 of the 52 families; slides ([[tarnholm-2026-information-evening-slides]]) sent to the rest the next day.

## Covered

- Programme day by day, with photos from the centre's pack ([[tarnholm-field-and-heritage-centre]]).
- The underground section of the mine: groups of 10, centre instructor plus a named member of our staff, surface alternative for anyone who prefers it. [[jamal-osei]] took most of the questions.
- Phones: collected at lights-out, returned at breakfast. Families can reach the trip through the school office and the trip phone.
- Medication and dietary information: through TripPlan forms only, checked by me and the trip first aider; not discussed in the meeting.
- The [[behaviour-agreement]] and what happens if it is broken.

## Questions Worth Remembering

- Several families asked whether pupils could share rooms with friends. We collected requests for one named friend; all were met.
- One parent asked why attendance had not been used to allocate places. I explained the lottery in two sentences and nobody pushed further.
- Kit list requested earlier than the Summer 1 newsletter. Noted for 2027.
