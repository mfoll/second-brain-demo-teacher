# History Department Meeting 2026-05-13

Summer term department meeting. The decision of 4 February to switch all five Year 10 classes to Migration, Empire and People was partly reversed: only two classes pilot it in 2026-27.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Only 10H1 (Ewan) and 10H3 (Ruth Calder) study Migration, Empire and People in 2026-27. 10H2, 10H4 and 10H5 stay on Medicine Through Time. The whole department switches in September 2027 if the pilot classes' March 2027 mock results are at least as good.

## Who and where

Wednesday 13 May 2026, 15:30 to 17:30, history office. Present: [[ruth-calder]] (chair), [[desmond-hartigan]], [[priyanka-sethi]], [[tomasz-zielinski]], [[amelia-fairweather]], me. [[kofi-asamoah]] attended for this item at Ruth's invitation. Minutes: Priyanka.

## Why it came back

- Merrow published the revised specimen papers and mark scheme on 28 April 2026, about three months later than signalled ([[merrow-migration-specimen-papers-2026]]). The 16-mark question in the specimen asks for a judgement across the whole period, which changes how the booklets need to be built.
- At my check-in on 6 May ([[migration-booklet-check-in-2026-05-06]]) the booklets were about 40% drafted: booklets 1 and 2 complete in draft, booklet 3 half done, 4 and 5 not started.
- Tomasz said he would be teaching it from booklets he had not seen, in a year he is also starting Year 11 for the first time.
- Kofi asked whether we could show the switch would not cost grades. Nobody could.

## Options discussed

1. **Keep the full switch** and write all summer. Ruth and I estimated 50 more hours at least, mostly over the holidays. Rejected: quality risk and workload for five teachers at once.
2. **Revert entirely** to Medicine and revisit for 2027. Desmond's preference. Rejected by the others: it throws away the work done and the reasons for switching still stand.
3. **Pilot in two classes** taught by the two people who wrote the materials, with the rest on Medicine. Proposed by Ruth. Needs two entry codes; [[sunil-varma]] had confirmed on 11 May that Merrow allows it.

## Decision

Option 3, agreed without a formal vote; Desmond said he could live with it "as long as the comparison is fair". Conditions:

- Pilot classes: 10H1 (me) and 10H3 (Ruth). 10H2 (Desmond), 10H4 (Priyanka) and 10H5 (Tomasz) teach Medicine.
- Whole department switches in September 2027 if the pilot classes' March 2027 mock results on the thematic study are at least as good as the Medicine classes'. Method to be agreed before the November mocks ([[how-do-we-compare-pilot-and-medicine-mock-results]]).
- Families of the two pilot classes get a letter before the summer ([[migration-pilot-parent-letter-2026-06]]).
- The superseded full-switch plan is archived ([[thematic-switch-transition-plan-v1]]).

## Actions

- Ruth: confirm the two-code entry with [[gillian-mortimer]].
- Me and Ruth: complete booklets 3 to 5 for the pilot classes by February half-term 2027.
- Priyanka: draft the parent letter.
- Desmond: write down what he would accept as a fair comparison. Done 20 May; built into [[migration-pilot-comparison-plan]].
