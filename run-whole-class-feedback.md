# Run Whole-Class Feedback

How I mark a set of books or essays with a whole-class feedback sheet and teach the feedback lesson.

## Inputs

- The set of work (about 30 books or essays).
- [[whole-class-feedback-sheet-template]].
- For Year 11: the [[merrow-levels-mark-scheme-16-mark-question]].

## Marking (about 55 minutes a set)

1. Read every piece without writing. Note on the sheet, in pencil, anything you see three or more times.
2. Fill the four boxes: what went well, misconceptions, literacy, redraft task with a model sentence.
3. Mark three or four books with a dot in the margin for an individual conversation.
4. Year 11 only: add one line per essay, the next step, and the mark.
5. Tick each book so pupils know it was read.

## Feedback lesson (first 20-25 minutes)

1. Show the sheet. Read "what went well" with examples, no names.
2. Teach the main misconception again, briefly.
3. Pupils fix literacy errors in their own work.
4. Pupils redraft one paragraph of their choice in green pen.
5. While they redraft, talk to the dotted pupils.

## Check

Book scrutiny looks for green-pen redrafts, not teacher comments.
