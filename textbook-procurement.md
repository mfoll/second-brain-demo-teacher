# Textbook Procurement

Buying a new Key Stage 3 textbook series to replace the worn 2014 sets. Superseded in June 2025 by in-house enquiry booklets.

## Outcome

Superseded by in-house enquiry booklets after a quote of 3,900 GBP for a Key Stage 3 textbook set.

## Current State

- Closed on 27 June 2025. No textbooks bought. The department writes and prints its own booklets for each enquiry ([[ks3-curriculum-redesign]]).
- The shortlist and the quote are kept for reference ([[ks3-textbook-shortlist-2025]], [[ks3-textbook-quote-2025]]).

## Working Context

In February 2025 [[kofi-asamoah]] found 4,000 GBP of faculty capital money that had to be committed by the end of the financial year. Ruth asked me to compare series. Three were shortlisted; the best fit was quoted at 3,900 GBP for 180 copies across three year books plus teacher guides.

Why we did not buy:

- None of the series matched the enquiries we were designing. Each was a chronological survey with enquiry questions bolted onto chapters.
- The [[diverse-curriculum-audit]] found the leading series told the same stories the old schemes did.
- Reading level: two of three were pitched above what about a third of Year 7 can read independently, on [[bethan-lloyd-hughes]]'s check.
- Booklets could be revised every year for about 1,450 GBP of printing; a textbook set locks content for five to eight years.

The money went instead on library source packs and a class set of local history books for Year 9 ([[library-enquiry-source-packs]]).
