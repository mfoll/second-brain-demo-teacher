# Cultural proximity reveals a local premium in collective memory.

Macedo M, Oyarzun M, Candia C, Hidalgo CA, PNAS Nexus, 2026.

## Summary

Separates local and global components of collective memory using multilingual online attention to more than 50,000 Wikipedia biographies and a large recognition experiment with over 41,000 participants. Reports a robust local premium: people are noticeably more likely to recognise figures from their own culture.

## Why It Matters

Supports, loosely, my hunch that local material sticks ([[local-history-makes-enquiries-stick]]), and gives the [[public-memory-and-heritage]] cluster an empirical anchor. Shared with [[hollis-granger]].

## Caveats

About adults recognising famous people, measured through online attention. Says nothing about classroom learning or a single town's industry. I must not cite it to the department as evidence for the ropeworks enquiry.
