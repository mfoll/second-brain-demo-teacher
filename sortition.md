# Sortition

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Sortition is choosing people for public roles, such as jurors or members of an assembly, by random draw. The idea is that a lottery gives everyone the same chance and produces a group that reflects the wider population, without favouring those with money, contacts or time.

## Why It Matters

Read the night before the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]], looking for a short way to explain why a lottery is fair rather than lazy. The point I took into the meeting: a random draw is used where any other rule would favour some people for reasons unrelated to the task. That is the argument in [[lottery-allocation]] and the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]].

## Caveats

About public office, not school trips; I used it as an analogy, not as evidence.
