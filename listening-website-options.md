# Listening Website Options

Comparison prepared by Ewan for the society committee, 21 September 2026.

| Option | Cost a year | Who maintains it | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audio page on existing society site | about 60 GBP | Current site volunteer | One volunteer, already stretched |
| Hosted website builder | about 190 GBP | Any committee member | Monthly cost; lock-in |
| Archive hosting | 0 | Archive | Ruled out by the deposit agreement |

## Recommendation

Option 1, if the current volunteer agrees and a second person is trained. Start with 8 to 10 clips, all from interviews with web use ticked and no third parties named.

## Before anything goes live

- A takedown statement and a contact address.
- A provenance line on every clip.
- A check of each clip against the archive's consent record.
