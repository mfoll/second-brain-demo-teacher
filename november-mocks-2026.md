# November Mocks 2026

The first full mock round for 11H1, run to the department moderation protocol and used to try comparative judgement on the 16-mark essay.

## Outcome

Year 11 history mocks sat 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-27, marked and moderated under the department protocol, feedback lessons done by 2026-12-11.

## Current State

- Timetable fixed with [[sunil-varma]] on 7 October: Paper 1 on 17 November, Paper 2 on 24 November ([[mock-timetable-november-2026]]).
- Papers built from the 2024 and 2025 Merrow past papers, not the new migration specimens: 11H1 is still on Medicine Through Time.
- Access arrangements: counts confirmed with [[sian-pritchard]] on 14 October; separate room booked.
- Marking window 28 November to 4 December, then standardisation and the 10% blind double-mark under the [[department-moderation-protocol]].
- I had hoped to draft feedback with an AI tool. [[paul-ingram]] said no on 30 September until the trust DPIA is finished. Interim: mark by hand and trial [[comparative-judgement]] on the 16-mark question.

## Scope and Boundaries

11H1 only, though Ruth's and Desmond's Year 11 classes sit the same papers and share the moderation. No pupil work goes into any tool outside [[quillyard]] and the school network until the trust approves it. Individual marks go into Ledgerwise; the vault gets the class distribution.

## Working Context

The 2025 mocks taught us the 16-mark question is where our marking drifts (2.1 marks generous against [[vikram-iyer]]'s at the [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|December 2025 moderation]]). The comparative judgement trial is aimed at exactly that question: judge 28 scripts in pairs, compare the rank order with our levels marks, and see whether disagreement concentrates in the middle levels. Method and success criteria are in [[comparative-judgement-trial-plan]]. The reliability benchmark I am using is from [[kinnear-2025-comparative-judgement-reliability]]: about ten comparisons per script.
