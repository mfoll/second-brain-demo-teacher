# Institutional Time and Collective Behavior: End-of-Week School Absenteeism as a Social Pattern

Alalwani S. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 2026.

## Summary

A mixed-methods study of absence on the last day of the school week, drawing on 411 teachers, pupils and parents. It identifies eleven factors, among them cumulative weekly fatigue, how the week is organised, absences coordinated among peers, messaging between pupils and families, weak supervision and a disengaging school environment. The argument is that end-of-week absence is a shared social habit shaped by the school timetable, not only individual non-compliance.

## Why It Matters

[[marek-nowicki]] flagged in October 2026 that Friday absence in the new Year 10 is higher than any other day. This paper gave me a way to think about it as a group pattern, which suggests group responses (what happens on Friday afternoons, tutor-time reasons to be in) rather than more letters. Logged against [[how-do-we-spot-emerging-persistent-absence-earlier]] as something to test.

## Caveats

- Context very different from ours, and the factor ranking comes from perceptions, not attendance records.
- Not a test of any intervention.
