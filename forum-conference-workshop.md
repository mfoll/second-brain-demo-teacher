# Forum Conference Workshop

A 60-minute workshop for history teachers on teaching pupils to interview, drawing on Ewan's reporting years and the Ropeworks Voices interviews.

## Outcome

Workshop delivered at the Historical Teaching Forum conference on 27 June 2026. Done.

## Current State

- Delivered to 34 teachers in the second afternoon slot: [[historical-teaching-forum-2026-06-27]].
- 29 feedback forms returned; average 4.5 out of 5 for usefulness: [[forum-workshop-feedback-summary]].
- [[ben-akintola]] asked on 3 July whether Ewan would run a longer version in 2027. Reply pending.

## Scope and Boundaries

No recordings of real interviewees were played. Two clips from consenting society volunteers who recorded a mock interview for the purpose were used instead; no ropeworks interview left the society.

## Working Context

- Title: "Ask, then listen: interviewing skills for history classrooms".
- Proposal submitted on 2 March 2026, accepted on 27 March: [[forum-workshop-proposal]].
- Structure in [[forum-workshop-outline]]: the closed versus open question drill, the five-second silence, pairs interviewing each other about a school memory, then turning an interview into evidence with provenance.
- Slides: [[forum-workshop-slides]]. Conference programme: [[historical-teaching-forum-2026-programme]].
- The procedure behind it is [[conduct-an-oral-history-interview]], cut down for pupils.
- Most-asked question on the day: how to handle consent when pupils interview family. Answer given: use a short parent-signed form and keep recordings off personal phones.
