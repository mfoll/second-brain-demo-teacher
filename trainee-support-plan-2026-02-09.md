# Trainee Support Plan of 9 February 2026

The support plan agreed for the 2025-26 history trainee on 9 February 2026 by Ewan, Laura Penhaligon and Rosalind Kerr, with the trainee. Recorded here as targets and progress only.

## Targets

1. **Entry routine.** Meet at the door, seating plan enforced, retrieval quiz on the board, silent start within two minutes.
2. **Cold-calling.** No hands up for checking questions; names drawn after the question is asked; wait time before naming.
3. **Consequences as the policy says.** Warning, then move, then removal, given calmly and logged in Ledgerwise the same day.

Support: weekly co-planning of the two Year 9 lessons; Ewan in the room for the first ten minutes of each Year 9 lesson for three weeks; one joint observation with Laura in week four.
