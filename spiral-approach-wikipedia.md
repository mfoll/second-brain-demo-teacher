# Spiral approach

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Explains the spiral approach as starting with a subject's basic facts and adding detail as learning progresses, revisiting the earlier basics and their links to later material so they enter long-term memory.

## Why It Matters

Read after the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25]] to justify revisiting each second-order concept at least twice across the 15 enquiries ([[second-order-concepts]]), and recurring substantive concepts such as empire and trade ([[substantive-and-disciplinary-knowledge]]).

## Caveats

Short article. It supports revisiting concepts, not Desmond's point about chronology, which a spiral alone does not solve.
