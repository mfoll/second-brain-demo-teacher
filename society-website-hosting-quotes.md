# Society Website Hosting Quotes

Three options priced by a society volunteer for the committee on 14 September 2026.

## Summary

1. Add an audio page to the society's existing site on its current hosting: about 60 GBP a year extra for storage; volunteer time to build.
2. A general-purpose hosted website builder with an audio plan: about 190 GBP a year.
3. Ask the Archives Centre to host clips on its own pages: no cost, but the archive does not publish online under the [[ropeworks-deposit-agreement]].

## Why It Matters

Option 3 is ruled out by the agreement. The real choice is cost against who maintains it. See [[listening-website-options]].
