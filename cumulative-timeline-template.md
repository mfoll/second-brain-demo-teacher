# Cumulative Timeline Template

The four-page fold-out timeline glued into the back of every Key Stage 3 exercise book, from 1000 to the present.

## Summary

Six coloured period bands, a century scale, and blank rows for each enquiry's three key dates. Pupils carry it forward into a new book each year.

## Why It Matters

The physical half of the chronology compromise ([[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25]]). Whether it works is [[is-a-cumulative-timeline-enough-for-chronology]].

## Caveats

Books get lost; about one in ten Year 8s started 2026-27 with no timeline from Year 7.
