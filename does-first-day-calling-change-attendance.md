# Does First-Day Calling Change Attendance?

## Why It Matters

First-day calls cost tutors time every morning: about 15 minutes per tutor per week across eight tutors. If they do not change attendance, that time could go elsewhere. [[jamal-osei]] asked this directly in September 2026.

## Evidence So Far

- Tutor groups with the highest call completion in 2025-26 improved more than those with the lowest, but the groups differed in other ways and there are only eight of them.
- Families mention the calls positively in the parents' evening survey, which suggests they change the relationship even if the attendance effect is unclear.
- Interventions in schools tend to have small average effects, with more variation between settings than between methods ([[li-2026-interventions-to-reduce-school-absenteeism]]).

## Current Answer

Unknown. Calls are cheap, families like them, and they catch welfare worries early. I am keeping them, but I will not claim in the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]] that they raised attendance.
