# Ropeworks Voices Launch Meeting

Corrandale Local History Society meeting, 7 April 2025, library meeting room. 14 members present.

## What happened

- Maureen Castleford presented the [[ropeworks-voices-project-plan-2025]]: 25 interviews by June 2027, budget 640 GBP.
- Ewan agreed to lead the interviews. Maureen had asked him in March, on the strength of his Courier years.
- [[nisha-kapoor]] volunteered to transcribe.
- Members suggested 31 names of former workers; 12 were known to be willing.
- Agreed to use the society's 2019 recording agreement. Nobody raised school use. With hindsight, that was the moment to fix the form: see [[ropeworks-consent-form-2019]].

## Actions

- Ewan: question bank by the end of April ([[ropeworks-interview-question-bank]]).
- Maureen: tell the Archives Centre a deposit is coming.
- First interview booked for 23 April.
