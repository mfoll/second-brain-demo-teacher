# Oral History Belongs in Key Stage 3

## Stance

Every pupil should meet oral history evidence at least once before Year 10, ideally local voices, because it teaches source questions that written sources hide: memory, hindsight, who is asking and why.

## Scope

Key Stage 3 enquiries. Not the exam years, where the specification sets the source types.

## Why I Think This

- Pupils at Corrandale know the ropeworks building site and the supermarket that replaced it, not the works. A voice from their own streets makes the past local.
- The disagreement between interviewees about 1984 is a better lesson in interpretation than any textbook pair of historians.
- The Forum workshop showed other schools want this and lack the material.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the first run of the Year 9 enquiry shows pupils treating the clips as anecdotes and not as evidence. Desmond Hartigan thinks they will. We will see in the spring.

## Implications

Argue for keeping the enquiry in the [[ks3-curriculum-redesign]] even if it costs a lesson elsewhere.
