# Why Were The 2026 Predictions Too Low?

11H2 got 72% at grade 4 or above in summer 2026 against my prediction of 66%. Why was the prediction low?

## Current Answer

Mostly because the March mocks were marked strictly under the new protocol and the 16-mark essays improved after March. Between March and June the class did six fortnightly timed essays with whole-class feedback; the 16-mark question moved most. A prediction taken from one strictly marked mock plus quiz trend did not capture that late gain. The smaller factor is noise: with 27 pupils, one or two pupils move the figure by four to seven points.

Accepted at the results review on 27 August. Changed practice: predictions now given as ranges ([[mock-grades-should-be-reported-as-ranges]]), and I expect a spring gain rather than treating March as final. Detail in [[exam-season-2026-review]].
