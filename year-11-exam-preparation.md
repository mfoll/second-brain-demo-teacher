# Year 11 Exam Preparation

Getting 11H1 ready for the Merrow Certificate in History in summer 2027. Umbrella for the mock rounds and the revision programme; it ends when the class sits its last paper.

## Outcome

11H1 (28 pupils) ready for the summer 2027 Merrow Certificate, with at least 70% at grade 4 or above in the March 2027 mocks.

## Current State

- 11H1 studied Medicine Through Time, Weimar and Nazi Germany and Elizabethan England in Year 10; Conflict and Tension 1945-72 is this autumn's content and finishes by February half-term. They are not affected by the [[thematic-study-switch]].
- September baseline (a 40-question retrieval quiz on Year 10 content) averaged 54%. Weakest: Elizabethan religious settlement and the Weimar recovery years.
- [[november-mocks-2026]] are fixed for 16-27 November: Papers 1 and 2 in full, sat in the sports hall.
- Three target groups for after-school support are in [[11h1-target-groups-autumn-2026]], described by need, not by name.
- AI-drafted feedback is blocked until the trust DPIA ends; see [[can-we-use-ai-to-draft-feedback-on-mock-essays]].

## Scope and Boundaries

My class only. Department decisions on the thematic study and moderation sit with [[history-department-development]]. Pupil-level marks and predictions live in Ledgerwise; anything here is an aggregate. Access arrangements are handled by [[sian-pritchard]] and [[sunil-varma]]; I record counts only.

## Working Context

Last year's lesson from [[exam-season-2026-review]]: the class did better than the adjusted mock prediction because the 16-mark essay improved most between March and June, and that was the question where we had been marking generously before the [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|December 2025 moderation]]. So I mark this year's mocks to the protocol from the start and report grades as ranges to pupils (see [[mock-grades-should-be-reported-as-ranges]]).

Weekly shape: two lessons of content, one of exam practice; a [[low-stakes-quizzing|low-stakes]] retrieval quiz at the start of every lesson from the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]]; one timed 16-mark question a fortnight, fed back with [[run-whole-class-feedback|whole-class feedback]].

[[hannah-greaves]] runs Year 11 study skills sessions in tutor time; I gave her the history command words so we say the same thing. [[moira-stenhouse]] runs an exam-stress drop-in from January.
