# DPO Decision On AI Tools 2026-09-30

Paul Ingram's reply, by email and then a ten-minute call, to my request of 22 September to use an AI tool to draft whole-class feedback on the November 2026 mocks.

## The decision

> [!note]
> No pupil work may go into an AI tool that the trust has not approved until the trust's data protection impact assessment is finished, expected December 2026. Anonymising scripts does not change this.

## Reasons he gave

- Mock scripts are personal data; essays can identify a pupil by content and handwriting.
- No AI tool has a trust data processing agreement yet; [[quillyard]] has one, which is why it is fine.
- The [[trust-interim-ai-guidance-2026]] already says this; he was applying it, not making a new rule.

## What I decided after

- Mark the November mocks by hand.
- Trial [[comparative-judgement]] on the 16-mark question as the interim: no data leaves school. Plan: [[comparative-judgement-trial-plan]].
- Revisit after the DPIA; possible small check on the March 2027 mocks.
