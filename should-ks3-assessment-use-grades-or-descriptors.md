# Should KS3 Assessment Use Grades Or Descriptors?

## Question

Should Key Stage 3 history assessed pieces be reported as 9 to 1 grades, to match Key Stage 4, or as descriptors of progress in the enquiry's concept?

## Status

Deferred with the [[ks3-assessment-model]] in March 2026 until the trust sets its data calendar ([[faculty-meeting-2026-03-04]]).

## Arguments so far

- For grades: parents and senior leaders understand them; four of seven trust schools use them ([[trust-history-network-meeting-2025-11-12]]).
- For descriptors: a 9 to 1 grade is defined by a Year 11 exam; applying it to a Year 7 essay is false precision. Descriptors tell pupils what to improve.
- Department majority: descriptors. Senior leadership leaning: grades.

## Revisit when

The trust calendar is published.
