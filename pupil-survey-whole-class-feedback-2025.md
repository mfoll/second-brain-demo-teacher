# Pupil Survey On Whole-Class Feedback 2025

An anonymous five-question form for my two Year 9 classes at the end of the trial, 7-10 July 2025. 54 responses from 59 pupils.

## Key Details

- Preferred the sheet: 61%. Preferred written comments: 24%. No preference: 15%.
- "I knew what to do to improve": 78% agreed for the sheet.
- Most common free-text comment: the redraft time helped; second most common: missing having something personal.

## Why It Matters

Pupil view for [[whole-class-feedback-trial-findings]]. The "something personal" point is why the margin for individual conversations stays on the sheet.

## Caveats

Self-report, anonymous, from pupils who knew I ran the trial.

## Source Location

Google Forms on the school account; summary exported to the trial folder.
