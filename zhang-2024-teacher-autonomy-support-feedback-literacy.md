# Influence of teacher autonomy support in feedback on high school students' feedback literacy: the multiple mediating effects of basic psychological needs and intrinsic motivation.

Zhang S, Xu J, Chen H, Jiang L, Yi X. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 2024.

## Summary

A survey of 704 senior high school students testing a model from self-determination theory. Students who saw their teachers' feedback as supporting their autonomy reported higher feedback literacy, the ability to understand and use feedback; part of the link ran through basic psychological needs and intrinsic motivation.

## Why It Matters

Read halfway through the [[whole-class-feedback-trial]], when Desmond asked whether pupils would ignore a sheet that was not about them. The redraft task on the sheet gives pupils a choice of which paragraph to fix, a small piece of autonomy. I kept the choice in the template after reading this.

## Caveats

Cross-sectional survey in a very different school system; self-reported measures; no outcome on attainment. Supports a design choice, does not prove it.
