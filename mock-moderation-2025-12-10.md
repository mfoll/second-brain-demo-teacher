# Mock Moderation 2025-12-10

Moderation of the November 2025 Year 11 history mocks with Vikram Iyer from Kittow Academy. The meeting that showed our marking on the 16-mark question was generous, and the start of the department moderation protocol.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> On a 12-script sample, the department's marks on the 16-mark "how far do you agree" question averaged 2.1 marks above Vikram Iyer's. Other questions were within half a mark. We agreed to re-mark all 16-mark answers before the mock results went to pupils, and Ruth asked me to draft a moderation protocol, adopted on 14 January 2026.

## Who and where

- Wednesday 10 December 2025, 15:30 to 17:45, history office.
- [[vikram-iyer]] (Kittow Academy, moderator), [[ruth-calder]], [[desmond-hartigan]] and me. [[priyanka-sethi]] joined for the last half hour.

## What we did

- Sample: 12 Paper 1 scripts, 4 from each of the three Year 11 classes (my 11H2, Ruth's and Desmond's), chosen by Ruth to cover the range from grade 2 to grade 8 on our marks. Names covered.
- Vikram marked the sample blind, the week before, with our marks hidden. We compared question by question.

## Findings

- 16-mark question (Medicine Through Time, "how far do you agree"): our marks averaged 2.1 above Vikram's. In 9 of the 12 scripts we had put a one-sided answer in Level 3 because it was detailed. Vikram, reading the [[merrow-levels-mark-scheme-16-mark-question|Merrow mark scheme]], kept one-sided answers in Level 2 however much knowledge they showed.
- 8-mark and 4-mark questions: within 0.5 marks on average; no pattern.
- Source questions: agreed within one mark on every script.
- Spread by marker: all three of us were generous on the 16-mark question, Desmond least (1.4), me most (2.7). I noted that against myself.

## Decisions

- Re-mark every 16-mark answer across the three classes before results go to pupils on 15 January. Done by 9 January; my class's profile fell from 70% to 59% at grade 4 or above.
- I would draft a protocol: standardise on board exemplars before marking, then blind double-mark a sample. Adopted on 14 January 2026 as the [[department-moderation-protocol]].
- Ask Kittow for a reciprocal moderation in March. Done: [[march-mocks-2026-moderation-2026-03-25]].

## Discussion worth keeping

Desmond said the department had drifted because the old Medicine mark scheme rewarded knowledge more openly, and we had kept marking to it. Ruth disagreed: the scheme changed in 2023 and we should have re-standardised then. Both are probably right. Vikram's point was simpler: no one in our department had marked against exemplars since the change.

I asked whether 12 scripts was enough. Vikram's view: enough to see a 2-mark systematic gap, not enough to say anything about individual markers. That is why the protocol uses 10% of all scripts rather than a fixed 12.
