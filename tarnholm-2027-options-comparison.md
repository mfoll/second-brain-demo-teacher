# Tarnholm 2027 Options Comparison

A quick comparison made on 28 September 2026 before rebooking, because [[elspeth-moran]] asked in January whether we had looked at alternatives.

| | Tarnholm | Coastal outdoor centre (trust-approved list) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel | About 3 hours | About 2 hours |
| Centre price, 2 nights | 184 GBP | 205 GBP |
| History programme | Castle, lead mine, village | None built in; adventurous activities |
| Capacity 2027 | 50 | 64 |
| Our experience | One trip, good evaluation | None |

## Decision

The distinction that settled it is the one in [[outdoor-education]]: adventurous activity is a different purpose from history in place.


Stay with Tarnholm. The point of the trip is history in place, and the coastal centre would turn it into an activity holiday. The extra capacity was attractive, but the cost per pupil would be about 15 GBP higher even with the shorter coach journey.

Checked with [[jamal-osei]] and [[rebecca-tull]]; both agreed. Recorded in [[tarnholm-residential-2027]].
