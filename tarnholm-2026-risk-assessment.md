# Tarnholm 2026 Risk Assessment

The risk assessment for the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]], version 3 of 12 January 2026, as approved in [[tripplan]] by [[rebecca-tull]]. Centre risk assessments for the mine and river attached.

## Summary

Covers travel, accommodation, the castle site, the lead mine (underground section), river study, evening activities, missing pupil, illness and injury, and emergency contact. Each hazard has controls, the person responsible and residual risk.

## Key Details

- **Mine.** Groups of ten with a centre instructor and a named member of our staff; head counts at the three gates; centre water-level check each morning; surface alternative for any pupil, no reason needed. Residual risk: low.
- **River.** Wellies and waterproofs; nobody above knee depth; instructor decides on the day; wet-weather alternative. Residual: low.
- **Castle.** Stay inside the fenced paths; no climbing on walls. Residual: low.
- **Night.** One member of staff per dormitory corridor; lights-out 22:30; phones collected.
- **Missing pupil.** Buddy pairs, head counts at every transition, a named meeting point at each site.
- **Medical.** Individual needs via TripPlan forms; trip first aiders [[jamal-osei]] and [[hannah-greaves]]; nearest hospital 25 minutes away.
- **Communication.** Poor mobile signal: centre landline and a satellite messenger carried by the leader.
