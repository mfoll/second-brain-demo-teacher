# Tarnholm Residential 2026

The first Year 10 residential I led: three days at the [[tarnholm-field-and-heritage-centre]], 15 to 17 June 2026, for 52 pupils and 6 staff.

## Outcome

Three-day residential at Tarnholm, 15 to 17 June 2026, for 52 Year 10 pupils and 6 staff, within budget and with an evaluation written. Closed on 26 June 2026.

## Current State

- Completed under budget: 13,710 GBP spent against 13,936 GBP income. The 226 GBP left went back to the bursary fund, agreed with [[olumide-bankole]].
- Evaluation: [[tarnholm-2026-evaluation]]. 49 of 52 pupils returned the survey; 92% rated the trip good or very good.
- What carries forward is in [[tarnholm-residential-2027]] and [[plan-a-residential-visit]].

## How Places Were Decided

61 pupils applied for 52 places by the 14 November 2025 deadline. At the [[slt-meeting-2025-11-19]], deputy head [[adeel-chaudhry]] proposed giving places only to pupils above 95% attendance. My [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] of 1 December showed that rule would exclude 23 of the 61 applicants, 14 of them bursary-eligible. At the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]] the threshold was dropped. Places went by [[lottery-allocation|lottery]] on [[tarnholm-lottery-draw-2025-12-12|12 December]], each conditional on a signed [[behaviour-agreement]]. The school bursary fund and 1,200 GBP from the Friends of Corrandale School paid for 11 places ([[friends-of-corrandale-bursary-grant-2025]]).

> [!note]
> Attendance did not decide who went. Places were allocated by lottery among all 61 applicants, with a behaviour agreement as the only condition.
