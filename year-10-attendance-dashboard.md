# Year 10 Attendance Dashboard

My aggregate attendance spreadsheet for Year 10, built from [[marek-nowicki]]'s weekly [[ledgerwise]] extracts. One row per week from October 2025, aggregates only. Final snapshot taken 17 July 2026; a new tab started for the 2026-27 cohort.

## Summary

Weekly Year 10 attendance, year-to-date attendance, count below 90%, count dropping more than two points, first-day call completion, and attendance by tutor group. No pupil-level data: the pupil list stays in Ledgerwise.

## Key Details

- Year 10 2025-26 (209 pupils): 91.4% and 31 persistently absent in October 2025; 92.6% and 26 in February 2026; 93.2% and 19 at 17 July 2026.
- Last six weeks of 2025-26: weekly average 94.0%.
- First-day call completion: 92% autumn, 81% January, 90% after the 23 February re-launch.
- Year 10 2026-27 (212 pupils): 94.3% and 20 persistently absent at 16 October 2026.

## Why It Matters

The canonical source for every attendance figure in the [[year-10-attendance-drive]] and the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]]. If a figure elsewhere disagrees, this one wins, and the other should be corrected.

## Caveats

Year-to-date figures change when codes are corrected. The 17 July figures are frozen in a separate tab.
