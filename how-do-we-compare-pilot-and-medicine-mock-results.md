# How Do We Compare Pilot And Medicine Mock Results?

## Question

"At least as good" was agreed on 13 May 2026 without saying how it would be measured. Two classes on Migration, three on Medicine, different papers, different teachers, different pupils. What comparison is fair enough to base the 2027 decision on?

## Options on the table

1. **Raw percentage at grade 4 or above on the thematic section.** Simple; confounded by class prior attainment.
2. **Difference between each class's thematic section and its depth study section in the same mock.** Each class acts as its own control: if a pilot class does relatively worse on the thematic section than on Weimar, that points at the option. My preference.
3. **Against the same teachers' Medicine results in previous years** (Desmond's suggestion). Fair to teachers; different cohorts.
4. **Comparative judgement of a sample of 16-mark answers across both options.** Interesting but the questions differ ([[comparative-judgement]]).

## Current thinking

Use option 2 as the main measure and option 3 as a check, agreed in writing before the November mocks so nobody chooses the measure after seeing results. Draft in [[migration-pilot-comparison-plan]]. Both mocks marked under the [[department-moderation-protocol]], with cross-class second marking.
