# AI Feedback Is A Workload Tool, Not A Marking Tool

## Stance

If an AI tool is ever approved, I would use it to draft the whole-class feedback sheet, which I then correct, and never to award marks. The mark is a professional judgement I sign; a draft of common errors is clerical work I check.

## Why I Think This

- The 16-mark question rewards argument against a levels scheme; the research I have read ([[atkinson-2025-llm-hybrid-essay-scoring]], [[debelak-2026-ai-essay-scoring-testing-standards]]) is about scoring general essays and says plainly that validity has to be shown, not assumed.
- Spotting patterns across 28 scripts is the slow part of [[whole-class-feedback]] and the part where an error costs least: I read every script anyway.
- As a reporter I learned to check every claim a source gives you. A draft is a source.

## What Would Change My Mind

A properly validated tool for our mark scheme with evidence on fairness for weaker writers. Or, the other way, a check showing drafts miss the misconceptions I find myself, in which case it is not even a workload tool.

## Implications

Any trial compares the tool's draft against a sheet I write first, on the same scripts.
