# KS3 Assessment Model

A common way of assessing and reporting Years 7 to 9 history: what the assessed piece in each enquiry measures, how it is marked and what goes to parents. Paused since March 2026.

## Outcome

A common Years 7-9 assessment model; paused until the trust sets its data collection calendar.

## Current State

- Paused at the faculty meeting of 4 March 2026 ([[faculty-meeting-2026-03-04]]). Resume condition: the Wyvernmoor Learning Trust publishes how often and in what form it wants Key Stage 3 data. [[aled-pryce]] expects that in the spring term 2027.
- Meanwhile each enquiry's assessed piece is marked on a four-point descriptor (working towards, secure, strong, exceptional) and only the descriptor is reported.
- A draft model exists ([[ks3-assessment-model-draft]]).

## Working Context

The argument is about grades versus descriptors ([[should-ks3-assessment-use-grades-or-descriptors]]). Senior leaders like a 9 to 1 "flight path" from Year 7 because it matches Key Stage 4 reporting. Most of the department thinks a grade on a Year 7 enquiry essay is false precision. At the trust history network on 12 November 2025 ([[trust-history-network-meeting-2025-11-12]]) the trust said it would set a common calendar, so there was no point building something it might overrule.
