# How Should The Newsletter Handle Pupil Photos?

## Current Answer

A photograph goes in the [[year-10-newsletter]] only if every pupil visible in it has photo consent recorded in [[ledgerwise]], and no pupil is named in the caption. If one pupil in a group shot has no consent, we use a different photo or none. Agreed with [[donna-whitlock]] and confirmed with the school data lead in October 2025.

## Why It Came Up

The first issue had a sports photo with a pupil whose family had declined photo consent. It was spotted before sending, but only by chance. The check is now a step in [[send-the-year-10-newsletter]].

## Caveats

Consent can change during the year. The check is done against Ledgerwise on the day of sending, not when the photo was taken.
