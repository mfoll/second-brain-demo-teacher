# Parents' Evening Review

Choosing how Year 10 parents' evenings run after the school agreed to trial online appointments with one year group. Year 10 was the trial year.

## Outcome

A Year 10 parents' evening format chosen after the online trial. Decided on 20 March 2026: video appointments by default, with an in-person block for families who ask.

> [!success]
> Decided 20 March 2026: video by default with 7-minute slots, plus an in-person block from 17:00 to 18:30 that families request when booking. Bookings rose from 61% (in person, Year 10 2024-25) to 82%.

## Current State

- Completed. The format is in use for the November 2026 evening under [[lead-year-10]].
- Findings and figures in [[parents-evening-trial-findings]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Format and logistics of the Year 10 evening only. Other year groups follow their own heads of year; [[adeel-chaudhry]] asked for this note to be shared with them, which it was on 23 March. Parent comments from the survey are kept as themes, never quotes, and no family is identifiable.

## Working Context

- **Before.** The 2024-25 Year 10 evening was in person in the sports hall, with 5-minute slots; 61% of families booked at least one appointment.
- **Trial 1, [[year-10-parents-evening-2025-11-27|27 November 2025]].** Fully online, 5-minute slots, booked through [[ledgerwise]]. 162 of 209 families booked (78%). About nine appointments dropped and had to be rebooked by phone.
- **Survey.** 118 family responses ([[parents-evening-survey-2025]]): 71% preferred video, 19% in person, 10% no preference. The main complaints were slots too short and no way to meet a teacher face to face when it mattered.
- **Trial 2, [[year-10-parents-evening-2026-03-12|12 March 2026]].** Hybrid: video slots lengthened to 7 minutes, an in-person block on request. 171 of 209 families booked (82%); 23 asked for in-person slots.
- **Staff view.** Teachers liked finishing on time and not standing in a cold hall; the [[teacher-workload]] point was raised by [[ruth-calder]] for the history department and echoed by others.

## Rejected Options

- Fully online with no in-person option: families who most needed a face-to-face conversation were the least likely to book a video slot.
- Back to fully in person: lower booking, and the hall clashed with evening fixtures twice a year.
