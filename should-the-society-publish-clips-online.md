# Should the Society Publish Clips Online?

Should the Corrandale Local History Society put Ropeworks Voices clips on a public website, and if so, on what terms?

## Current thinking

- For: the interviewees who ticked web use want to be heard; the town has no other public record of the works in workers' own voices; it would help recruit the last interviewees.
- Against: clips travel and lose context; several interviewees speak frankly about named managers whose families still live in the town; a volunteer-run site may not outlast its volunteer.
- Ewan's position: yes, but only for clips that name no third parties, each with a provenance line, and only with a named site owner and a takedown statement in place.

## Constraints

8 of the 12 version 2 interviews allow web use. None of the 2019-form interviews do. See [[listening-website-options]].

## What would close it

A committee decision after the January 2027 deposit.
