# Tarnholm Residential 2027

The second Year 10 residential, for the cohort that started Year 10 in September 2026. Same centre, same shape as 2026, with 50 places.

## Outcome

The 2027 residential approved by the governors' visits committee on 9 December 2026 with 50 places and a confirmed bursary fund.

## Current State

- Provisional booking held for 14 to 16 June 2027 after the [[tarnholm-provisional-booking-call-2026-10-06|call with Gwen Tarrant on 6 October]]. Deposit of 500 GBP due by 30 November.
- Estimated cost 289 GBP per pupil, against 268 GBP in 2026 ([[tarnholm-2027-provisional-quote]]): centre up from 172 to 184 GBP, coach up from 75 to 82 GBP; contingency up from 21 to 23 GBP.
- 50 places, two fewer than 2026, because the centre has closed one dormitory for refurbishment.
- Bursary: about 3,200 GBP needed to fund 11 places at the 2027 price. The fund holds 1,974 GBP including the 226 GBP left over from 2026. Gap open: [[can-the-bursary-fund-cover-2027-places]].
