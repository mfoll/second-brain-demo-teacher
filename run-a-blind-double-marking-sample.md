# Run A Blind Double-Marking Sample

The steps of the [[department-moderation-protocol]] for one mock series. I run them as the department's moderation lead; any of us can.

## Before marking

1. Book a 30-minute standardisation slot in the department meeting nearest the end of the mock window.
2. Pick three Merrow exemplar answers for each extended question, one per level boundary ([[merrow-exemplar-scripts]]). For the 16-mark question always include a detailed one-sided answer.
3. Everyone marks the exemplars cold, then compares with the board's marks. Discuss any difference over 2 marks.

## Sample

4. Ask [[sunil-varma]] to draw a random 10% of scripts per class, minimum 3, stratified so every teacher's class is in it. He removes names and first marks and gives each a code.
5. Second markers are assigned across classes. Early career teachers' samples go to Ruth or Desmond.

## Compare

6. Enter both marks per question in the [[double-marking-log-2026]].
7. Any question where markers differ by more than 2 marks goes to a third reader (Ruth, or me if it is Ruth's script). The third mark stands.
8. If one marker's mean difference on a question exceeds 1.5 marks across their sample, they re-mark that question for their whole class.

## After

9. Summarise the mean gap per question at the next department meeting.
10. Once a year, send the sample to [[vikram-iyer]] at Kittow for external moderation.

## Time

About 90 minutes per teacher per series.
