# Tarnholm Residential 2026-06-15

The residential itself: Monday 15 to Wednesday 17 June 2026. 52 Year 10 pupils, 6 staff: me (leader), [[jamal-osei]] (deputy), [[luis-ortega]], [[hannah-greaves]], [[tomasz-zielinski]], [[donna-whitlock]].

## At a Glance

> [!success]
> All 52 pupils went and came back on time. Programme run as planned apart from the river study, cut to 75 minutes after rain on day 2. One minor behaviour incident, handled under the behaviour agreement.

## Day by Day

- **Day 1.** Coach left 08:15, arrived 11:40. Castle enquiry after lunch, framed as "Why was a castle built here, and why did it fall into ruin?" Pupils worked from three sources supplied by the centre, then compared with a timeline of the valley. Evening: map quiz.
- **Day 2.** Lead mine in five groups of ten plus two pupils on the surface alternative, each group with a centre instructor and one of our staff. Head counts at all three gates. River study shortened after rain. Evening: campfire, cut short by more rain, moved indoors.
- **Day 3.** Village heritage walk, then coach at 12:30, back at 15:50.

## Incidents

- One dormitory talking after lights-out on night 1; warning and a conversation the next morning under the [[behaviour-agreement]]. Nothing further.
- Two pupils homesick on night 1; both fine by breakfast. No calls home needed.
- No first aid beyond plasters and one sprained finger at the river.
