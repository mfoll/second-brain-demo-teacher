# Attendance Huddle Launch 2025-10-14

First weekly Year 10 attendance huddle, Tuesday 14 October 2025, 08:10 to 08:40, Year 10 office. Me, [[donna-whitlock]] and [[marek-nowicki]].

## What We Agreed

- Same time every week, 25 minutes, standing if needed. Cancelled only for INSET days.
- Marek brings the Ledgerwise extract: everyone below 90%, plus anyone who dropped more than two points in a fortnight.
- Each pupil on the list gets one owner and one next step before we move on. Actions go into [[ledgerwise]] during the meeting, not afterwards.
- Anything touching safeguarding: "passed to DSL", logged in [[lanternlog]] by the owner, and not discussed further in the huddle.
- Tutors get one weekly email with their actions, on Tuesday by noon.

## First List

31 pupils below 90% (15% of the year), and 12 more dropping fast. We got through 22 in the first meeting, which showed the list was too long for 25 minutes. From week 2 we split it: persistent cases on odd weeks, fast drops on even weeks, any new first-day pattern every week.

## Why It Matters

This became the core routine of the [[year-10-attendance-drive]], and the procedure [[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]] is written from the first six weeks of it.
