# Faculty Meeting 2026-03-04

Humanities faculty meeting chaired by [[kofi-asamoah]]. History, geography and RE heads plus Priyanka and me for the Key Stage 3 item.

## Key Stage 3 assessment

Priyanka presented the [[ks3-assessment-model-draft]]: four-point descriptors per enquiry, reported three times a year, no grades before Year 10. Geography wanted to align with whatever history does.

Kofi's view: a good model, but the trust has still not published its data calendar, and senior leaders will want a grade-based version if the trust asks for grades. Building it twice is a waste.

## Decision

[[ks3-assessment-model]] paused until the trust publishes its calendar. Meanwhile, keep the four-point descriptors on each enquiry's assessed piece and report only those.
