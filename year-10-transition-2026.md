# Year 10 Transition 2026

Taking on the new Year 10 of September 2026 while last year's group moved up: handover from the head of Year 9, tutor team set-up, the first weeks, and a settling-in survey.

## Outcome

The new Year 10 of 212 pupils settled in eight tutor groups, the tutor team set, and the settling-in survey analysed. Closed on 2 October 2026.

## Current State

- Completed. Survey analysed on 2 October: 191 of 212 pupils responded (90%). Findings in [[settling-in-survey-findings-2026]].
- First half-term attendance 94.3%, 20 pupils persistently absent, against 93.8% and 24 for the same pupils in the summer of Year 9.
- Ongoing work is back under [[lead-year-10]].

## Scope and Boundaries

At Corrandale the tutor team is attached to the year, not the group: pupils keep their tutor group letter and classmates, but meet a new tutor in Year 10 and again in Year 11. That change, plus the start of exam courses, is why Year 10 transition gets its own project. Handover of individual needs went through [[ledgerwise]] and, for anything sensitive, through [[fiona-mcrae]] and [[lanternlog]], never through this vault.

## Working Context

- **Handover.** Head of Year 9 meeting on 9 June; notes kept as counts and themes in [[year-9-to-10-handover-notes-2026]]. The 24 pupils below 90% in Year 9 went straight onto the huddle list for September.
- **Tutor team.** Three tutors stayed from 2025-26 ([[jamal-osei]] 10C, [[luis-ortega]] 10D, [[hannah-greaves]] 10F); five are new to the year. Roles agreed at the [[year-10-tutor-team-meeting-2026-09-02]], recorded in [[year-10-tutor-team-2026-27]].
- **First weeks.** Assembly on the first Thursday about what changes in Year 10; a welcome issue of the [[year-10-newsletter]] on 16 October; attendance huddle running from week one rather than week six as last year.
- **Survey.** Eight questions in tutor time, 21 to 28 September, built in Google Forms ([[how-to-use-google-forms]]). Instrument in [[settling-in-survey-2026]].
