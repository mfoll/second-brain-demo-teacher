# Mock Results Lesson 2026-01-15

11H2 received their November 2025 mock results after the 16-mark re-mark.

- Gave each pupil their paper, marks by question and a grade. Explained that the 16-mark answers had been re-marked after moderation and that marks had gone down for most.
- Several pupils had already been told an informal grade in December and were upset by the drop. Two families emailed the next day; themes were fairness and whether summer marking would be as strict. I replied that it would be stricter.
- The lesson I took: a single grade given early is a promise. Hence [[mock-grades-should-be-reported-as-ranges]].
- The second half of the lesson was a whole-class feedback session on one-sided answers. It went well.
