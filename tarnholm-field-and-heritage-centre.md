# Tarnholm Field and Heritage Centre

The residential centre we use for the Year 10 trip: a converted mine owners' house and bunkhouse beside a ruined castle and a former lead mine, about three hours from Corrandale by coach. This note keeps what I gathered from the centre's group pack, my planning visit and two years of bookings.

## Summary

Tarnholm takes school groups of up to 60 for two- or three-night stays, full board, with its own instructors for underground and river sessions. It suits a history residential because the castle, the mine and the miners' village tell one story about land, power and work, which links back to Corrandale's own industrial past ([[corrandale-ropeworks]]).

## Why It Matters

It is the centre for [[tarnholm-residential-2026]] and [[tarnholm-residential-2027]]. The prices and capacity here drive the per-pupil cost and the number of places, which drive the bursary sums ([[can-the-bursary-fund-cover-2027-places]]). [[jamal-osei]] knew the centre from a previous school and suggested it in October 2025.

## Key Details

- **Contact.** [[gwen-tarrant]], bookings and education manager. Phone Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- **Capacity.** 60 beds in six dormitories in 2026; 50 in 2027 while one dormitory is refurbished. Staff rooms separate, on each dormitory corridor.
- **Programme used.** Day 1 castle enquiry (centre-led, 2 hours, then our own source work); day 2 guided lead mine visit, including a 45-minute underground section in groups of 10, and river study; day 3 village heritage walk. Evening sessions optional.
- **Prices.** 2026: 172 GBP per pupil for two nights, full board and programme; staff free at one per ten pupils. 2027: 184 GBP, a 7% rise ([[tarnholm-2027-provisional-quote]]).
- **Deposit and payment.** 500 GBP deposit to hold dates; balance six weeks before arrival.
- **Safety.** The centre provides its own risk assessments for the mine and river; helmets, lamps and wellingtons supplied. Our assessment builds on theirs ([[tarnholm-2026-risk-assessment]]).
- **Access.** Ground-floor dormitory with accessible bathroom; the underground section is not step-free, so the centre offers a surface alternative led by an instructor.

## Caveats

- Prices are quotes, confirmed only on deposit.
- Mobile signal is poor in the valley; the centre landline and the trip satellite messenger are the fallback.
