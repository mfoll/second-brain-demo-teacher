# Automated essay scoring

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on automated essay scoring: computer programs that assign grades to essays, treated as a statistical classification problem. Covers the history of such systems, how they are evaluated against human raters, and the criticisms.

## Why It Matters

Read before I emailed Paul Ingram in September 2026. It made clear that the field is about scores, while what I wanted was feedback. That is why I framed my request as drafting whole-class feedback, not marking ([[ai-feedback-is-a-workload-tool-not-a-marking-tool]]).

## Caveats

Mostly about large-scale testing with thousands of essays to train on. We have 28 scripts and a mark scheme that changed in 2023.
