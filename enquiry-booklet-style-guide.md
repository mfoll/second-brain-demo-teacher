# Enquiry Booklet Style Guide

The house style for every Key Stage 3 enquiry booklet, agreed after Tomasz asked for a common structure in March 2025. One page, pinned in the department office.

## Structure

1. Cover: the [[enquiry-question]] and a single image.
2. Knowledge organiser (one page, substantive only).
3. Lessons, two to four pages each: starter retrieval questions, a short narrative text (300-500 words), sources or tasks, and a "back to the question" box.
4. Assessed piece and its four-point descriptor.
5. Timeline page: what to add to the cumulative timeline.

## Rules

- 24 pages maximum.
- Narrative text checked by [[bethan-lloyd-hughes]] for reading level before printing.
- At least three named ordinary people per enquiry ([[diverse-curriculum-audit]] recommendation 1).
- Every source credited; no image without a known licence.
- No pupil work, names or photographs in booklets.
- Version number and date in the footer.
