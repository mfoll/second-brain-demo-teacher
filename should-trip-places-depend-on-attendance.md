# Should Trip Places Depend On Attendance?

## Why It Matters

Attendance gates are a tempting lever: trips, prom, rewards days. If the residential is gated, the same logic will be applied to other things. The answer also decides who goes on the trip, which is the most visible thing a head of year organises.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> No. Answered at the SLT meeting of 3 December 2025: places on the Tarnholm residential are allocated by lottery among all applicants, with a behaviour agreement as the only condition. Attendance is not used.

## Evidence

- A 95% threshold would have excluded 23 of 61 applicants, 14 of them bursary-eligible ([[trip-places-equity-analysis]]).
- For 15 of the 23, the gap was mostly authorised illness.
- The trip's real risks are about conduct, which a [[behaviour-agreement]] addresses directly.
- Some research suggests that experiences outside the classroom can raise belonging for pupils who feel on the edge of school ([[drewery-2023-immersive-field-trip-and-belonging]]); that is a reason not to filter those pupils out, though the study is about university students.

## What Is Still Open

Whether other rewards (the Year 11 prom, the end-of-year rewards day) should follow the same principle. That is for [[adeel-chaudhry]] and the heads of year, not this vault. My stance: [[trip-places-should-not-be-earned-by-attendance]].
