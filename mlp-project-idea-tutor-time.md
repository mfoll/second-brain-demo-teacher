# MLP Project Idea: Tutor-Time Reading

Ewan's first idea for the Middle Leadership Programme project, sketched in November 2025. Dropped on 21 January 2026 in favour of the Year 10 attendance drive: see [[mlp-project-proposal-2026-01]] and [[mlp-session-2026-01-21]].

## The idea

Replace two of the five Year 10 tutor-time slots with a shared reading programme: a class novel or long-form non-fiction read aloud by the tutor, with three questions.

## Why it was dropped

- The attendance drive was already running and had weekly data; the reading idea had none and no baseline.
- Tutors were already asked to make first-day calls. Adding a reading programme in the same year would have been too much change at once.
- The English department was planning something similar for Year 7.

## Kept because

Might be worth revisiting for the new Year 10 in 2027-28, once the attendance routines are embedded.
