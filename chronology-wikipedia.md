# Chronology

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines chronology as the science of arranging events in order of occurrence, as in a timeline, and of determining the actual sequence of past events. The article goes on to dating methods and calendar systems.

## Why It Matters

Read while writing the timeline lessons after the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25]]. The distinction between ordering events and dating them helped: pupils need the order and the rough distances between events, not dates for their own sake ([[chronological-framework]]).

## Caveats

Mostly about dating techniques and calendars, which is more than Key Stage 3 needs.
