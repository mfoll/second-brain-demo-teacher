# Classroom Management

English Wikipedia article on classroom management.

## Summary

Describes classroom management as the processes teachers use to keep lessons running without disruption, covering both preventing disruptive behaviour in advance and responding when it happens. Disruption ranges from ordinary peer conflict to serious problems that stop pupils concentrating.

## Why It Matters

Shared with the 2025-26 trainee two days after the support plan, as a neutral overview to discuss. The prevention-first framing is the argument for teaching an [[entry-routine]] before anything else, which became target one of the [[trainee-support-plan-2026-02-09]] and the first fortnight of [[yusuf-first-half-term-plan]].

## Caveats

Broad and international. The school's own behaviour policy, not this article, sets what consequences a teacher uses.
