# Wyvernmoor Learning Trust Workload Charter

The trust's workload charter, reissued for September 2025.

## Summary

Commitments: no more than three data drops a year; no requirement for written marking in every book; emails not expected outside 7.30am to 6pm or at weekends; meetings capped at one hour with an agenda; directed time published at the start of the year.

## Why It Matters

Gave cover for the department's switch to whole-class feedback ([[whole-class-feedback-trial]]). The email commitment is the one Ewan breaks most often as head of year; see [[ewan-time-log-autumn-2025]].

## Caveats

Mentoring and trip planning are not mentioned. Both fall outside the charter's main levers.
