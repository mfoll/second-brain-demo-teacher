# Law of comparative judgment

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on Thurstone's law of comparative judgment: a model for turning many pairwise comparisons ("which is heavier?", "which statement is more extreme?") into a measurement scale. It is the statistical basis of comparative judgement in assessment.

## Why It Matters

I read it after Vikram Iyer mentioned comparative judgement at the March 2026 moderation swap. It explained why judging pairs can produce a scale at all: each comparison is noisy, but many of them place every script on one line. That is the basis of the [[comparative-judgement-trial-plan]].

## Caveats

Psychometric and abstract; nothing on essays or schools. For practical numbers I use [[kinnear-2025-comparative-judgement-reliability]].
