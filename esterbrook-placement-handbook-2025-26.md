# Esterbrook University Secondary Placement Handbook 2025-26

The School of Education's handbook for partner schools hosting secondary trainees in 2025-26, sent by Laura Penhaligon on 28 August 2025.

## Summary

Sets the placement pattern (main placement September to June with a six-week contrasting placement in the autumn), the mentor's duties (weekly meeting, one formal observation a fortnight, interim and final reports), the timetable ramp (about 50% in autumn rising to 65% by spring), and the cause-for-concern route: an informal conversation, then a support plan with the link tutor, then a formal review.

## Why It Matters

The support plan of 9 February 2026 followed its route exactly; the lesson Ewan took is that the first stage could have started earlier. See [[when-should-a-trainee-support-plan-start]].

## Key Details

Mentor time: the university pays the school a mentoring fee; the handbook "expects" an hour of protected time weekly. In practice it took more. See [[how-much-mentor-time-does-a-trainee-really-need]].
