# Whole-Class Feedback Trial Findings

Findings written up for the department, 15 July 2025.

## Results

- **Time.** Median marking time per set of about 30 books: 2 h 40 min with written comments (spring), 55 min with sheets (summer). [[whole-class-feedback-marking-time-log]].
- **Action.** Book scrutiny of 30 books: redrafted paragraphs in 26 (summer) against 9 (spring).
- **Pupil view.** 61% preferred the sheet, 24% comments ([[pupil-survey-whole-class-feedback-2025]]).
- **Quality.** End-of-term assessed piece: no clear difference from the previous year's Year 9 on the same task. I would not claim a gain in attainment.

## Limits

One teacher, two classes, one term; different tasks in spring and summer; I knew what I hoped to find.

## Recommendation (accepted)

Adopt for Years 7-9 from September 2025, with a margin for individual conversations. Keep a line of individual comment in Year 11 for now. Decided at [[whole-class-feedback-trial-review-2025-07-14]].
