# Developing historical thinking skills and creativity of visually impaired middle school students.

Akhan O, Uzun A, Frontiers in Psychology, 2025.

## Summary

Studies activities designed to develop historical thinking and creativity in a group of visually impaired middle school pupils who follow the general curriculum. Starts from the observation that history's abstract concepts make standard activities hard to access, and argues for activities adapted for these pupils.

## Why It Matters

Passed on by [[sian-pritchard]] when we reviewed whether the enquiry booklets work for pupils with access needs. Prompted two changes to the [[enquiry-booklet-style-guide]]: every image has a text description, and timeline tasks have a tactile or spoken alternative on request.

## Caveats

Small group in one country; read from the abstract. Not evidence about our booklets, only a prompt to check them.
