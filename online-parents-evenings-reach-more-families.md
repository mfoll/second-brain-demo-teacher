# Online Parents' Evenings Reach More Families

## Stance

Video appointments reach more families than an evening in the sports hall, especially families with shift work, younger children at home or no car. They do not replace a face-to-face conversation when one is needed.

## Why I Think This

- Booking went from 61% to 78% to 82% across the three Year 10 evenings ([[parents-evening-trial-findings]]).
- Families who said they had never come to an in-person evening appeared in the bookings, by their own account in the survey.
- The 23 in-person requests in March were mostly for conversations about worries, where a screen would have been worse.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the families we most need to see stop booking, or if survey comments start saying the calls feel like a production line. 7-minute slots were the answer to the second risk; I will watch it.

## Implications

Keep the hybrid. Do not let the in-person block shrink to save staff time.
