# AI Feedback Options Comparison

Options I weighed in September 2026 for getting help with feedback on 28 mock essays. Written before Paul Ingram's answer; updated after.

| Option | Data leaves school? | Time saved (estimate) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General AI chatbot, anonymised extracts | Yes | 3-4 h | Ruled out by DPO and by me |
| Specialist essay-marking service | Yes | 4-5 h | Not approved; needs DPIA |
| Future Quillyard AI feature | No (covered by agreement) | Unknown | No release date |
| Comparative judgement, done by us | No | None, costs about 2 h | Chosen for November 2026 |
| Hand marking with whole-class sheet | No | Baseline | Always the fallback |

The time estimates are guesses from what colleagues at other schools said at the trust network, not measurements. The benchmark accuracy reported in papers such as [[atkinson-2025-llm-hybrid-essay-scoring]] does not translate into these numbers.
