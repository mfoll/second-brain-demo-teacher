# Forum Workshop Outline

Running order for "Ask, then listen", 60 minutes, as delivered on 27 June 2026.

1. **Why interview? (5 min).** Pupils analyse sources but rarely make one. A story from the Courier newsroom about a wrong quote.
2. **Closed and open (10 min).** Rewrite five closed questions as open ones. Swap and test on a partner.
3. **The five-second silence (10 min).** Pairs: one asks, one answers, the asker must wait five seconds after each answer before speaking. Debrief: what came after the pause?
4. **Pairs interview (15 min).** Interview a partner for five minutes about a school memory, then swap. Make a one-paragraph summary with provenance.
5. **Consent and ethics (8 min).** Why the society uses [[layered-consent]]; the pupil consent template; recordings on school devices only.
6. **Recording to evidence (7 min).** Summary, timestamps, short quotation, provenance line. Two mock-interview clips.
7. **Questions (5 min).**

## Handouts

12-question pupil bank, pupil consent template, summary sheet.

## Changes for any 2027 version

Longer section 6, as the feedback asked; shorter newsroom story.
