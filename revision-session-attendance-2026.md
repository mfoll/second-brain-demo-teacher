# Revision Session Attendance 2026

Weekly headcounts at the Tuesday history revision sessions, January to May 2026, across the three Year 11 classes. Counts only.

## Key Details

- January: 36-41 pupils a week.
- February: about 30.
- March, after the mocks: a rise to 38, then a fall.
- April: 12-17.
- May: 20-25, sessions before each paper.
- Sessions were run by whoever was free; topics announced on the day.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for [[revision-programme-2027]]. The April drop is the problem a published calendar is meant to fix.

## Caveats

Headcounts only; no way to tell who came regularly. Easter holidays fell in April.
