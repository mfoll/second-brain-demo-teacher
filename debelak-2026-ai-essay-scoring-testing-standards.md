# Testing standards for AI-based scores in automated essay scoring.

Debelak R, Ziegler M. *PLOS ONE*, 2026.

## Summary

A methods paper on how to evaluate scores that AI models give to essays in educational and psychological assessment. Because essays are not made of test items, the usual checks for validity, fairness and reliability do not transfer directly. The authors review existing methods, propose new ones and illustrate them.

## Why It Matters

Useful for the conversation with Paul Ingram after the trust DPIA. If a tool is approved, I should be able to say how I checked it. My planned check on the March 2027 mocks ([[can-we-use-ai-to-draft-feedback-on-mock-essays]]) is small, but this paper gives it a structure: does the draft agree with my judgement (reliability), does it miss what the mark scheme rewards (validity), does it do worse for weaker writers (fairness).

## Caveats

About scores. I want feedback drafts, which no standard covers. I read the abstract and the framework section, not the worked example in full.
