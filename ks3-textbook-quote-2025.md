# KS3 Textbook Quote 2025

The publisher's quote of 21 March 2025 for shortlisted series A.

## Summary

- 180 pupil copies: 60 of each of the three year books.
- Three teacher guides and a one-year digital licence.
- Total 3,900 GBP including delivery, valid to 30 June 2025.

## Why It Matters

The figure the department weighed against booklet printing at about 1,450 GBP a year ([[textbook-decision-2025-06-11]]). Kept because the question will come back for the Migration option ([[should-we-buy-a-textbook-for-the-migration-option]]).
