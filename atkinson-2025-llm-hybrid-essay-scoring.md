# An LLM-based hybrid approach for enhanced automated essay scoring.

Atkinson J, Palma D. *Scientific Reports*, 2025.

## Summary

Proposes an essay scoring model that combines features from several linguistic levels, including large language model representations of structure and meaning, rather than surface features such as word counts. On standard essay datasets it outperforms earlier approaches based on shallow features or neural networks alone.

## Why It Matters

Tomasz sent it to me in September 2026 as evidence that AI marking "works". It shows progress on public benchmark datasets. It does not show anything about a 16-mark history argument judged against a levels mark scheme, which is the case in [[ai-feedback-options-comparison]]. I kept it as the example of why benchmark accuracy is not the question for us.
