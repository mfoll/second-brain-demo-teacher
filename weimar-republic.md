# Weimar Republic

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on the German state from November 1918 to March 1933: its constitution and semi-presidential system, the crises of 1919-23, the recovery years, the depression and the collapse into Nazi rule.

## Why It Matters

Used as a fact check while writing the 230 Weimar and Nazi Germany questions in the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]], mainly dates and the names of governments. The depth study in the [[merrow-history-specification]] runs 1918-39. The September 2026 baseline showed 11H1 weakest on the recovery years, so this is the article I check when I rewrite those questions.

## Caveats

Far more detail than the specification needs, and the emphasis differs from the board's. Quiz answers follow the specification and our booklet, not the article.
