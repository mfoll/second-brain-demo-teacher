# Lessons From the 2025-26 Placement

What Ewan would do differently as a mentor, written on 22 June 2026 after the final review. About the mentoring, not the trainee.

## Changes for next time

1. **Teach routines in week one, before any content lesson.** Last year we assumed routines would be picked up by watching. They were, for the classes the trainee took over in September. The January Year 9 classes had different habits and nobody re-taught them.
2. **Name patterns in week three.** The log shows the issue from mid-January. I waited because I hoped it would settle. It did not.
3. **Keep support plans to three targets.** Three was right. A fourth would have diluted them.
4. **Co-plan the hardest class from the start,** not only once there is a problem.
5. **Hand over classes with the class teacher in the room** for the first lesson, so the routines carry over.

## What went well

- The autumn: strong subject planning and good use of the Kittow placement.
- The support plan itself: framed as teaching, closed early, held through the summer.
