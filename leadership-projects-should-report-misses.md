# Leadership Projects Should Report Their Misses

## Stance

A leadership write-up should lead with whether the target was met. If it was missed, say so first, then explain what moved and what did not.

## Scope

The MLP write-up and any report on the attendance drive to senior leaders or governors.

## Why I Think This

- The 94% target was missed by 0.8 points. Burying that would make every other figure look like spin.
- The more useful result, persistently absent pupils down from 31 to 19, is more credible next to an honest miss.
- Ten years of reading press releases. The paragraph that hides the bad number is always the one people remember.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the programme's marking treated a missed target as a fail. Catriona Dalgleish has said it does not.

## Implications

Opening paragraph of the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]] states the miss. Next year's target for the new Year 10 is set on persistent absence, not the average.
