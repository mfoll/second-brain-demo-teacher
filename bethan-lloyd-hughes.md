# Bethan Lloyd-Hughes

School librarian. Curates the source packs for our enquiries and checks reading level before booklets are printed. Without her the booklets would be harder to read and narrower in whose voices they include.

## How we work together

- I send a booklet draft at least three weeks before printing; she returns it with a reading-age estimate per page and suggestions for replacement extracts.
- She built the library source packs bought with the redirected textbook money ([[library-enquiry-source-packs]]).
- Implements audit recommendation 7: packs reviewed for range as well as reading level ([[diverse-curriculum-audit]]).
- Runs a lunchtime history book club for Year 8 that I visit twice a term.
