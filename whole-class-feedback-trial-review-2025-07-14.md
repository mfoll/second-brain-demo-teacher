# Whole-Class Feedback Trial Review 2025-07-14

Review meeting with Ruth Calder to decide whether to recommend whole-class feedback to the department.

## What we looked at

- Marking time: 2 h 40 min median per set down to 55 min ([[whole-class-feedback-marking-time-log]]).
- Book scrutiny: redrafted paragraphs in 26 of 30 sampled books, against 9 of 30 in spring.
- Pupil survey: 61% preferred the sheet ([[pupil-survey-whole-class-feedback-2025]]).

## Decisions

- Recommend adoption for Years 7-9 from September 2025. Ruth took it to the department meeting the next day; agreed.
- Year 11 essays keep a short individual comment for now. Whether to change that is [[does-whole-class-feedback-work-for-year-11-essays]].
- Book scrutiny will check redrafts, not comments.

## Not decided

Ruth asked whether the time saved should be "given back" or spent on essays. Left to each teacher.
