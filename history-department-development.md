# History Department Development

My share of running the history department with [[ruth-calder]] (head of history) and [[priyanka-sethi]] (second in history, Key Stage 3 lead): what we teach in Years 7 to 9, which exam options we take at Key Stage 4, how we mark and moderate, and the shared resources everyone uses. It has no end date because the curriculum is never finished; each piece of finite work becomes a Project below.

## Scope

- Key Stage 3 curriculum (Years 7 to 9): enquiries, booklets, timeline lessons, the assessment model.
- Key Stage 4 option choices on the Merrow Certificate in History ([[merrow-history-specification]]), above all the [[thematic-study-switch]].
- Moderation and standardisation across the department, with [[vikram-iyer]] at Kittow Academy as external partner.
- Department meetings, minutes and the resources on the shared drive ([[history-department-shared-drive]]).
- Not here: my own classes and their marking ([[teach-history-classes]]), Year 10 pastoral work ([[lead-year-10]]), the society's oral history work ([[local-oral-history]]), even where the Year 9 enquiry borrows from it.

## Current State

- Six of us teach history: Ruth, [[desmond-hartigan]], Priyanka, [[tomasz-zielinski]], [[amelia-fairweather]] (part-time, also RE) and me. Line management runs through [[kofi-asamoah]], head of humanities.
- The new Key Stage 3 curriculum of 15 enquiries is taught in all three years from September 2026; Year 7 has had it since September 2025 ([[ks3-curriculum-redesign]]).
- Migration, Empire and People is piloted in 10H1 (me) and 10H3 (Ruth) in 2026-27; 10H2, 10H4 and 10H5 stay on Medicine Through Time. The full switch depends on the March 2027 mocks ([[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13]]).
- The moderation protocol has run on two mock series (March 2026 and the summer internal assessments) and runs again on the November 2026 mocks.
- The Key Stage 3 assessment model is paused until the trust publishes its data collection calendar ([[ks3-assessment-model]]).
