# Send The Year 10 Newsletter

Steps for each half-termly issue of the [[year-10-newsletter]].

## Timing

Draft in the second-to-last week of each half-term; send on the last Friday before the break, or Thursday if Friday is an early finish.

## Steps

1. **Collect.** Tutors' celebration items (one per group, by group, no names) by the Monday of send week. Dates from the school calendar. One lead item.
2. **Draft.** About 400 words: the date that matters first, then three to five short items, then contacts. Template in the Year 10 shared drive.
3. **Photos.** For each photo, check photo consent in [[ledgerwise]] for every visible pupil on the day of sending. Any pupil without consent: use a different photo or none ([[how-should-the-newsletter-handle-pupil-photos]]). No names in captions.
4. **Read-through.** [[donna-whitlock]] checks for anything that could identify a pupil (small clubs, single-pupil achievements, unusual circumstances).
5. **Translation.** Request translated versions from the office for the families who have asked for them; allow two working days.
6. **Send.** Through Ledgerwise to all Year 10 contacts with parental responsibility. Upload a copy to the Year 10 page on [[quillyard]].
7. **Log.** Add the issue and lead item to the issue log in [[year-10-newsletter]]; note the open rate a week later.

## Never

Individual attendance or behaviour, anything that should be a letter, anything from [[lanternlog]].
