# How Many Retrieval Questions Per Lesson?

How long should the starter quiz be?

## Current Answer

Five questions, about five minutes including self-marking. Tried ten in spring 2025; the quiz ate 12-15 minutes and pupils rushed the later questions. Three felt too thin to cover last lesson, last week and last term. Five keeps the mix ([[spacing-effect]]) and leaves the lesson its time. Exam classes get a ten-question quiz once a fortnight instead of one starter.
