# Attendance Huddle Log 2025-26

The meeting-level log of the Year 10 huddle for 2025-26: one row per meeting with counts, not pupils. Individual actions are in [[ledgerwise]].

## Key Details

- 34 huddles held between 14 October 2025 and 14 July 2026; two cancelled for INSET days, one for snow.
- Average 11 pupils discussed per meeting; 362 actions agreed in the year.
- Action types: tutor call 41%, meeting offer 24%, home visit 9%, referral (counsellor, SENCo, outside services) 8%, passed to DSL counted separately and not described, other 18%.
- Actions completed by the following week: 81%.

## Why It Matters

Evidence of the process for the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]]: what the huddle actually did, week to week.
