# Parent–teacher conference

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes a short meeting between parents and teachers about a pupil's progress and how to handle academic or behavioural concerns, known in different places as a parent-teacher interview, parent-teacher night or parents' evening. Such meetings add to reports by looking at a pupil's particular strengths and weaknesses in each subject and at wider skills.

## Why It Matters

Read at the start of the [[parents-evening-review]] in September 2025 to get the purpose straight before arguing about format. The article frames the meeting as a conversation about one pupil's next steps, which is what 5-minute slots squeezed out. That fed the move to 7-minute slots and the in-person block ([[parents-evening-trial-findings]]).

## Caveats

General and descriptive; nothing on online formats.
