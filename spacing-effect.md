# Spacing effect

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on the spacing effect: material studied in sessions spread over time is retained better than the same amount of study massed together ("cramming"). Covers spaced repetition and the main explanations offered for the effect.

## Why It Matters

Behind the five-question quiz structure in the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]]: one question from last lesson, two from last week or two, two from last term. Also the main argument for a weekly [[revision-programme-2027]] from January rather than an Easter blitz. Hannah Greaves uses the same idea in her study skills sessions.

## Caveats

Says little about how long the gaps should be for school content over months. I chose the mix by judgement, not from the article.
