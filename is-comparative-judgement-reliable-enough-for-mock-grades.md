# Is Comparative Judgement Reliable Enough For Mock Grades?

Could comparative judgement replace, or check, levels marking for the 16-mark mock question, well enough that we would base predicted grades on it?

## Current Answer

Unknown until the November 2026 trial. My test: three judges, 28 scripts, ten comparisons per script. If scale separation reliability reaches 0.8 (the threshold suggested in [[kinnear-2025-comparative-judgement-reliability]]) and the rank order agrees broadly with our moderated levels marks, it is good enough to use as a check. Replacing levels marking would also need anchor scripts to turn ranks into grades.

## What would change the answer

- Reliability below 0.8: use it for feedback discussion only.
- Large disagreements concentrated in the middle levels: that is where our marking drifts, so it may still be useful there.
