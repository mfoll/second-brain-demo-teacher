# History Department Meeting 2025-03-05

Spring term meeting where the department agreed to rebuild Key Stage 3 around enquiries and to start with an audit.

## Decisions

- Rebuild Years 7 to 9 as enquiries, Year 7 first for September 2025, Years 8 and 9 for September 2026. Priyanka leads; I write most of Year 7 ([[ks3-curriculum-redesign]]).
- Start with an audit of whose stories the 2019 schemes tell, Priyanka and me, report by half-term ([[diverse-curriculum-audit]]).
- Keep the textbook comparison going in parallel but buy nothing until the audit reports ([[textbook-procurement]]).

## Discussion

Ruth framed it as "less coverage, more argument". [[desmond-hartigan]] asked who would write it all and when, and whether anyone had costed the time. Fair: nobody had. I offered a rough 70 hours for the Year 7 units; it turned out about right ([[year-7-enquiry-units]]).

Tomasz asked for a common lesson structure across enquiries so that early career teachers can find their way round any booklet. Agreed in principle, written up later as [[enquiry-booklet-style-guide]].
