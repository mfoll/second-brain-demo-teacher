# When Should a Trainee Support Plan Start?

How early should a mentor name a pattern and move to a support plan?

## Answer

Name the pattern in writing at the weekly meeting as soon as it shows in three consecutive weeks, normally by week three of a term. Move to a formal support plan with the link tutor if the targets set then are not improving within another two to three weeks.

## Why

- In 2025-26 the Year 9 behaviour issue was visible from mid-January and recorded as "settling new classes" until week 4. The plan came on 9 February. The trainee said afterwards that earlier would have been easier.
- Laura Penhaligon's view: a plan is a teaching tool; delay makes it feel like a verdict.
- Esterbrook's 2026-27 handbook now asks mentors to raise concerns in writing by week 4: [[esterbrook-placement-handbook-2026-27]].
