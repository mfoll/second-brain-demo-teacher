# Retrieval Quiz Bank

A bank of low-stakes quiz questions on the two Year 10 and 11 depth studies, built in Quillyard so that every history teacher could start lessons with a retrieval quiz without writing one from scratch. Completed in July 2025.

## Outcome

420 low-stakes quiz questions on Weimar and Nazi Germany and Elizabethan England live in Quillyard and shared with the department.

## Current State

- Done. 420 questions: 230 on Weimar and Nazi Germany, 190 on Elizabethan England. Each tagged by unit, lesson and question type (recall, sequence, odd one out, cause). Index in [[retrieval-quiz-bank-index]].
- Shared with the department on 9 July 2025 ([[retrieval-quiz-bank-launch-2025-07-09]]). [[tomasz-zielinski]] and [[amelia-fairweather]] use it most.
- Not covered: Medicine Through Time, Conflict and Tension and the new migration option. Migration questions for the 10H1 pilot are a live question: [[does-the-migration-pilot-need-its-own-quiz-questions]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Questions only, no assessment. Results in Quillyard are for the teacher's planning; they are not reported to parents and not used for predictions. That was the condition Ruth set and it still holds ([[retrieval-quizzes-should-stay-low-stakes]]).

## Working Context

Built on the [[testing-effect]] and [[spacing-effect]] reading and on the [[desirable-difficulty]] idea: quizzes mix last lesson, last week and last term, five questions each. Writing rules I settled on after the first 60 questions:

- One fact per question; no "all of the above".
- Wrong options must be plausible to a pupil, not a teacher.
- Every cause question names a date or person so it cannot be answered by guessing the shape.

The summer 2025 Quillyard analytics ([[quillyard-quiz-analytics-summer-2025]]) showed 11 questions answered correctly by more than 95% of pupils every time; I rewrote them.
