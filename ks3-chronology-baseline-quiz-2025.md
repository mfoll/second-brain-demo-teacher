# KS3 Chronology Baseline Quiz 2025

Results of the ten-question chronology quiz at the end of the first timeline lesson, September 2025, all Year 7 classes. Aggregates only.

## Summary

- Mean 5.8 of 10 across eight classes (range of class means 4.6 to 7.1).
- Weakest item: ordering 'Black Death' and 'Norman Conquest' (correct in 52%).
- Strongest: placing 'Second World War' (correct in 91%).

## Why It Matters

The only baseline we have for the current Year 8, who sit the chronology test in January 2027 ([[is-a-cumulative-timeline-enough-for-chronology]]).

## Caveats

Ten items, one lesson in; a weak baseline.
