# First Ropeworks Voices Deposit

First deposit at the Corrandale Archives Centre, 18 March 2026. Ewan and Maureen Castleford with Alistair Finnie.

## What happened

- Deposited RV01 to RV10: ten WAV masters, eight transcripts (RV09 and RV10 to follow), ten signed consent forms and the batch spreadsheet from the [[ropeworks-interview-log]].
- Alistair checked every row against the paper forms. One discrepancy: RV06's naming choice was "first name only" on paper and "named" in the log. Log corrected.
- Accessioned as one collection under the [[ropeworks-deposit-agreement]].

## Lesson

Check the log against the forms before the deposit, not at the desk. Added to the verification step of [[conduct-an-oral-history-interview]].
