# Adeel Chaudhry

Deputy headteacher for behaviour and attendance at Corrandale School, and my line manager for the head-of-year role.

## Role

Leads the school's attendance strategy, the behaviour policy and the pastoral team, and sits on the senior leadership team with [[gillian-mortimer]] and [[fiona-mcrae]]. Signs off attendance letter templates and any change to how heads of year use behaviour points.

## How We Work Together

- Fortnightly line meeting, Wednesdays after school, 30 minutes. He wants figures before opinions: bring the [[year-10-attendance-dashboard]] extract.
- He is direct and changes his mind when shown data. The trip threshold is the clearest case: he proposed a 95% attendance condition for Tarnholm places at the [[slt-meeting-2025-11-19]], asked me to model it, and dropped it at the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]] once the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] showed who it would exclude. He has since said he will not reopen it for 2027.
- Backed the online parents' evening trial and the hybrid format that came out of the [[parents-evening-review]].
