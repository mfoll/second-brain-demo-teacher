# Human migration

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines human migration as people moving from one place to another intending to settle, permanently or temporarily, often over long distances and across borders, while noting that internal migration is the dominant form globally.

## Why It Matters

Background while Ruth wrote the [[thematic-switch-proposal-2026-01]]. The point that most migration is internal is useful in booklet 3 of the Migration option: many ropeworks recruits came from elsewhere in the same country, not from abroad ([[migration-resources-tracker]]).
