# Diverse Curriculum Audit Presentation 2025-05-21

Priyanka and I presented the audit of the 2019 Key Stage 3 schemes to the department. All nine recommendations accepted.

## What we showed

Coding of 168 lessons ([[diverse-curriculum-audit-spreadsheet]]): 71% of named individuals were rulers, generals or politicians; women named in 9% of lessons; nothing local. Full list of recommendations in [[diverse-curriculum-audit]].

## Discussion

- [[desmond-hartigan]] agreed with most of it and added a ninth recommendation: no change made purely for representation if it costs the enquiry its question. Accepted unanimously.
- [[amelia-fairweather]] pointed out RE had done a similar audit in 2023 and offered their coding frame for the re-audit.
- [[bethan-lloyd-hughes]] (invited) offered to review source packs for range as well as reading level.

## Decisions

All nine recommendations accepted. The local enquiry (recommendation 2) to be Year 9, on the ropeworks, subject to the society's interviews being usable ([[local-history-enquiry]]).
