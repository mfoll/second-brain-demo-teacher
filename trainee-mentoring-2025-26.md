# Trainee Mentoring 2025-26

Mentoring [[kit-ansell|Kit Ansell]], Esterbrook University history trainee, through the main school placement at Corrandale from September 2025 to June 2026.

## Outcome

Kit completes the Corrandale placement and meets the placement standards. Reached on 19 June 2026.

## Current State

- Completed. Final review on 19 June 2026 confirmed all standards met: [[trainee-placement-final-review-2026-06-19]].
- Kit now teaches as an early career teacher at another Wyvernmoor school. No further mentoring role here.
- The reusable lessons are in [[lessons-from-the-2025-26-placement]] and are already shaping [[trainee-mentoring-2026-27]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Mentor's professional notes only: targets, observations, progress, paperwork. No judgement of Kit as a person, nothing about health or circumstances. The support plan is recorded as three targets and their progress.

## Working Context

- Timetable: 7H1, 8H4 and 9H2 from September, adding 9H5 in January. Roughly 50% of a full timetable in the autumn, 65% in spring and summer.
- Six-week contrasting placement at Kittow Academy from 3 November to 12 December 2025.
- Autumn went well; the Year 9 classes in January did not. Low-level disruption in 9H2 and 9H5 grew over three weeks. The triggers were a loose entry routine, hands-up questioning that let the same six pupils answer, and consequences threatened but not given.
- Support plan agreed on 9 February 2026 with Laura Penhaligon and Rosalind Kerr: [[trainee-support-plan-2026-02-09]]. Three targets: a fixed entry routine, cold-calling, consequences used as the behaviour policy says. Weekly co-planning of the two Year 9 lessons.
- Review on 16 March: entry routine secure, cold-calling secure in 9H2 and improving in 9H5, consequences used consistently from late February. Plan closed on 27 April.
- Esterbrook paperwork: [[esterbrook-placement-handbook-2025-26]], [[esterbrook-placement-standards]], [[lesson-observation-form-esterbrook]]. Weekly meetings logged in [[weekly-mentor-meeting-log-2025-26]].
