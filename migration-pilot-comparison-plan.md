# Migration Pilot Comparison Plan

Draft, 2026-10-14. How we will judge whether the pilot classes' March 2027 mock results are "at least as good" (rule from [[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13]]). For agreement at the November department meeting, before the November mocks are marked.

## Main measure

For each of the five Year 10 classes: mean percentage on the thematic study section minus mean percentage on the Weimar depth study section of the same mock. Each class is its own control for prior attainment and teacher. Pilot is "at least as good" if both pilot classes' gaps are no worse than the mean gap of the three Medicine classes by more than 5 percentage points.

## Check

Desmond's measure: each teacher's thematic-section percentage this year against their own Medicine classes' thematic-section percentage in the March 2025 and March 2026 Year 10 mocks.

## Marking

- All five classes' thematic sections marked under the [[department-moderation-protocol]].
- Second marking crosses options: a Medicine teacher second-marks the pilot sample and the other way round, after standardising on both mark schemes.

## Dry run

The November 2026 mocks are Year 11 only, so the Year 10 dry run is the end-of-unit assessment in December. Use it to test the arithmetic, not to judge.

## Not agreed yet

- The 5-point tolerance (Ruth thinks 3).
- Whether to report by class to the department or only as pilot versus Medicine.
