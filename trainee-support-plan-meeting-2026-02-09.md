# Trainee Support Plan Meeting

Meeting on 9 February 2026, after school, with the 2025-26 trainee, Ewan, Laura Penhaligon (by video) and Rosalind Kerr.

## Decision

A support plan with three targets for the rest of the spring term: entry routine, cold-calling, consequences used as the behaviour policy says. Weekly co-planning of the Year 9 lessons and Ewan present for the first ten minutes of each for three weeks. Review on 16 March. Detail: [[trainee-support-plan-2026-02-09]].

## Discussion

- Laura framed it as a teaching plan, not a warning. That helped.
- The trainee identified the entry routine as the root problem before anyone else said it.
- Rejected: moving the trainee off 9H5. It would have solved the symptom and left the skill unlearned.
- Ewan acknowledged the issue had been visible since mid-January and he had waited too long to name it. See [[when-should-a-trainee-support-plan-start]].
