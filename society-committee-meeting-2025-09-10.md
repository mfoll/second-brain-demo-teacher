# Society Committee Meeting: Consent Form Version 2

Corrandale Local History Society committee, 10 September 2025. Seven committee members, Ewan attending for item 3.

## Decision

Adopted [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]] for all Ropeworks Voices interviews from RV08 onwards, with separate archive, web and education choices. Unanimous.

## Discussion

- Ewan explained the July conversation with RV05, who asked whether pupils would hear her, and that the 2019 form could not answer.
- One member proposed a single "all uses" box to keep the form short. Rejected: people tick the bundle without reading, and a no to one use should not mean a no to all. See [[layered-consent]].
- Alistair Finnie had checked the archive wording by email; Hollis Granger's suggestion of a closure option was included.
- Whether to go back to the first seven interviewees now: deferred until the school's enquiry was confirmed, to avoid asking twice.

## Actions

- Ewan: print the new form and information sheet before RV08.
- Maureen: tell the Archives Centre.
