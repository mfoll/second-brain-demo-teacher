# Behaviour Agreement

A short written agreement, signed by a pupil and a parent or carer, setting out how the pupil will behave on a trip and what happens if they do not. At Corrandale it is the only condition attached to a place on the Tarnholm residential.

## Why It Replaced an Attendance Threshold

The trip's real risk is conduct on the trip: following instructions underground, staying with the group, lights-out. An attendance figure says nothing about that. A behaviour agreement addresses it directly and asks the same of every pupil. It came out of the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]] as the alternative to the 95% threshold.

## What It Contains

- Five commitments: follow staff instructions immediately, stay with your group, respect the centre and other guests, no phones after lights-out, look after each other.
- The process if broken: warning on the trip; serious breaches mean a call home and possibly collection at the family's cost.
- Before departure: the trip leader may withdraw a place for a serious breach of the school behaviour policy, but only after a meeting with the family. In 2026 this was never used.

The form itself: [[tarnholm-behaviour-agreement-form]].

## Limits

An agreement is only as strong as the conversation around it. In 2026 the one breach (talking after lights-out) was handled with a conversation, which is what the agreement is for.
