# Run The Weekly Attendance Huddle

How the Year 10 attendance huddle runs. Written from the first six weeks of the [[year-10-attendance-drive]] (from [[attendance-huddle-launch-2025-10-14|14 October 2025]]) and revised for 2026-27. Anyone covering for me, or another head of year starting a huddle, should be able to run it from this.

## Who and When

- Head of year (chair), pastoral manager ([[donna-whitlock]]), attendance officer ([[marek-nowicki]]).
- Tuesdays 08:10 to 08:35, Year 10 office. Not moved for anything except INSET days.
- The DSL joins by invitation only.

## Inputs

- Marek's [[ledgerwise]] extract by Monday 16:00: every pupil below 90% year to date, every pupil whose rolling four-week figure is below 92%, and every pupil down more than two points in a fortnight.
- Last week's action list.

## Steps

1. **Last week's actions** (5 minutes). Done, not done, carried. Anything not done twice gets a new owner.
2. **New names** (5 minutes). Anyone new on the fast-drop or four-week list. One owner, one next step each.
3. **Caseload** (12 minutes). Odd weeks: [[persistent-absence|persistently absent]] pupils. Even weeks: fast drops. One action per pupil, entered in Ledgerwise during the meeting.
4. **Patterns** (3 minutes). Tutor group, day of week, subject clashes.
5. Chair sends the tutor actions email by Tuesday 12:00.

## Guardrails

- Any welfare or safeguarding element: the owner logs it in [[lanternlog]] the same day for the DSL; the huddle records only "passed to DSL" and moves on.
- No pupil names in anything outside Ledgerwise: not in email subject lines, not in this vault, not on slides.
- Actions are things a named adult will do this week: a call, a meeting offer, a home visit, a referral. "Monitor" is not an action.

## Outputs

- Updated Ledgerwise actions.
- Tutor email.
- Monthly counts for the [[year-10-attendance-dashboard]].

## Verification

At each half-term, compare the caseload count with Marek's whole-year figure. If the huddle caseload is smaller than the number below 90%, someone has fallen off the list.
