# Should The Mine Visit Stay In The Tarnholm Programme?

## Why It Matters

The underground section of the lead mine is the highest-risk activity on the residential and the one pupils remember most. Two parents raised it at the launch in October 2025 and one governor asked about it in January.

## Current Answer

Yes, with the 2026 controls, confirmed after the trip at the [[tarnholm-debrief-2026-06-24]]:

- Groups of ten, each with a centre instructor and a named member of our staff.
- Head counts at each of the three gates, not only at entry and exit ([[rebecca-tull]]'s change).
- A surface alternative led by an instructor, offered without needing a reason. Two pupils chose it in 2026.
- No underground section if the centre's water-level check fails that morning.

## Evidence

No incidents underground in 2026. In the evaluation, 38 of 49 respondents named the mine as their highlight ([[tarnholm-2026-evaluation]]).
