# Run a Weekly Mentor Meeting

How Ewan runs the 45-minute weekly meeting with a trainee. Written after 2025-26 and used with Yusuf Demir from September 2026.

## Inputs

- Last week's targets and the meeting log entry.
- Observation notes or drop-in notes from the week.
- The trainee's planning for next week, sent by Sunday.

## Steps

1. **Five minutes: how was the week?** Let the trainee talk first. Note anything they raise themselves.
2. **Ten minutes: last week's targets.** For each, what evidence do we have? Met, progressing or stuck.
3. **Name patterns.** If something has appeared in three weeks running, say so plainly and write it down. Do not wait for it to become a support plan matter.
4. **Fifteen minutes: co-plan one lesson.** The class they find hardest. See [[co-planning]].
5. **Ten minutes: set one or two targets.** Each specific, observable, and mapped to a placement standard.
6. **Five minutes: admin.** Paperwork, cover, dates, anything from Esterbrook.

## Outputs

A log entry in Esterbrook's portal the same day, with targets and the standard each maps to.

## Guardrails

- No more than two targets a week.
- Nothing about the trainee's personal life goes in the log unless they ask.
- If a concern is serious, phone the link tutor the same week.
