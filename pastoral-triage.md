# Pastoral Triage

How I decide, as head of year, where a worry about a pupil goes: to the pastoral team, to the attendance huddle, to the counsellor, or to the DSL through [[lanternlog]].

## The Rule

> [!warning]
> If there is any doubt whether something is a safeguarding matter, it is logged in Lanternlog and passed to the DSL. Triage never means holding on to a concern while deciding.

## Routes

- **Safeguarding or welfare concern**: Lanternlog, same day; [[fiona-mcrae]] or a deputy decides. Nothing in this vault.
- **Wellbeing without a safeguarding element** (friendship breakdown, exam stress): tutor first, then [[donna-whitlock]], then a referral to the school counsellor, [[moira-stenhouse]], with the pupil's agreement.
- **Attendance pattern**: the weekly huddle ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]).
- **Learning or access needs**: [[sian-pritchard]], the SENCo.
- **Behaviour**: behaviour policy, with Donna; repeated issues to [[adeel-chaudhry]].

## Why Write It Down

New tutors ask the same question in their first weeks: "who do I tell?" A one-page version of this note is in the [[year-10-tutor-handbook-2026-27]].
