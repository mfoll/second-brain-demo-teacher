# Friends Of Corrandale Bursary Grant 2025

Letter from [[chris-delahaye]], chair of the Friends of Corrandale School, of 8 December 2025 confirming a grant of 1,200 GBP towards bursary places on the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]].

## Key Details

- Amount: 1,200 GBP, paid to the school trip account on 15 December 2025.
- Conditions: used only for pupil places, not general trip costs; the evaluation to report how many places it helped fund.
- Raised mostly from the summer fair 2025.

## Why It Matters

With the school fund, it paid for 11 places. The evaluation reported back as asked ([[tarnholm-2026-evaluation]]). A similar request for 2027 is on the Friends' agenda for 10 November 2026.
