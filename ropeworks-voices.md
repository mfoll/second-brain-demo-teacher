# Ropeworks Voices

The Corrandale Local History Society's project to record former Corrandale Ropeworks workers before their memories are lost. Ewan leads the interviews; [[nisha-kapoor]] transcribes; the [[corrandale-archives-ropeworks-catalogue|Corrandale Archives Centre]] keeps the recordings.

## Outcome

25 interviews recorded, transcribed and deposited at the Corrandale Archives Centre by June 2027.

## Current State

- 19 interviews recorded (RV01 to RV19), 13 transcribed, 10 deposited. Log: [[ropeworks-interview-log]].
- RV01 to RV07 (April to August 2025) used the society's 2019 form, which has no education option: [[ropeworks-consent-form-2019]].
- RV08 to RV19 used [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]], adopted on 10 September 2025. 11 of these 12 allow education use; RV15 chose archive only.
- Only those 11 can supply clips for the Year 9 enquiry. Re-consent letters went to the 7 earlier interviewees on 5 October 2026; 3 have come back and are with [[alistair-finnie]] to log against the deposit. Until logged, the usable count stays at 11. See [[re-consent-letters-sent-2026-10-05]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Interviewees are consenting adults. This vault records their role at the works, the consent version and their choices, plus short content summaries. No transcripts, no health or family detail. Audio and transcripts live with the society and the Archives Centre.

## Working Context

- Started at the society meeting of 7 April 2025: [[ropeworks-voices-launch-meeting-2025-04-07]]. Plan: [[ropeworks-voices-project-plan-2025]].
- Interview method: [[conduct-an-oral-history-interview]], question bank [[ropeworks-interview-question-bank]], recorder settings [[society-recorder-settings-sheet]].
- Why the form changed: RV05, a former twine-finishing supervisor, asked in July 2025 whether "the school" would hear her. The 2019 form could not answer that. Version 2 gives three separate choices. See [[layered-consent]] and [[society-committee-meeting-2025-09-10]].
- Deposit terms with the Archives Centre: [[ropeworks-deposit-agreement]]; first batch deposited on 18 March 2026 ([[archives-deposit-2026-03-18]]).
- Recruitment has been through the Courier, the society newsletter and word of mouth. Former spinners and twine finishers (mostly women) are now well represented; maintenance fitters and office staff are not.
