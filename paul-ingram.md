# Paul Ingram

Data protection officer for the Wyvernmoor Learning Trust, covering all seven schools. Decides whether a tool that processes pupil data may be used, and runs the trust's data protection impact assessments.

## How we work together

- I asked him on 22 September 2026 whether I could use an AI tool to draft feedback on the November mocks. His answer on [[dpo-ai-tools-decision-2026-09-30|30 September]]: no pupil work into any AI tool that the trust has not approved, until the trust's [[data-protection-impact-assessment]] is finished, expected December 2026.
- He was clear and quick, and offered to tell me when the DPIA is done. He also said that anonymising scripts would not change his answer: essays can identify pupils by content and handwriting.
- Contact by email through the trust office; he visits Corrandale once a term.
