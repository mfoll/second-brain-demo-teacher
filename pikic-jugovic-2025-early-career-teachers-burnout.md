# Early Career Teachers' Social and Emotional Competencies, Self-Efficacy and Burnout: A Mediation Model

Pikić Jugović I, Marušić I, Matić Bojić J. BMC Psychology, 2025.

## Summary

A survey of 657 subject teachers with up to five years' experience, analysed with structural equation models. Self-management and social awareness were the social and emotional competencies most associated with lower burnout, and teacher self-efficacy partly or fully mediated those associations. The pattern held broadly across the four burnout dimensions measured.

## Why It Matters

Rosalind Kerr circulated it before the October 2025 early career teacher session. For Ewan it sharpened one point about mentoring: building a trainee's sense that they can manage a class is not a nicety; it is linked to whether they stay well in the job. It sits behind the stance in [[mentoring-is-mostly-co-planning]] and the decision to front-load routines.

## Caveats

Cross-sectional, so associations rather than causes. One country's subject teachers; self-report measures.
