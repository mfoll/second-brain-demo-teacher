# KS3 Pupil Voice Survey 2026-07

Google Forms survey of Year 7 at the end of their first year on the new curriculum, run by Priyanka in tutor time. 201 responses from about 236 pupils. Anonymous; aggregates only here.

## Summary

- 78% agreed booklets 'helped me understand'.
- 64% said they knew what the enquiry question was for each unit.
- Timeline lessons: 41% 'useful', 33% 'boring'; most common free-text phrase 'boring but useful'.
- Favourite enquiry: Black Death (enquiry 3), despite its length.

## Why It Matters

Discussed at the [[history-department-meeting-2026-07-08]]. Supports the booklets; tells us nothing about learning.
