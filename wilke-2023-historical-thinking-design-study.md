# Fostering Secondary Students' Historical Thinking: A Design Study in Flemish History Education.

Wilke M, Depaepe F, Van Nieuwenhuyse K, Journal of Formative Design in Learning, 2023.

## Summary

A design study set in a curriculum reform that made historical thinking a central aim of secondary history. The authors start from the point that historical thinking needs both substantive and second-order knowledge and is hard to foster, and work through how research-based design guidelines can be turned into instructional practice in real classrooms.

## Why It Matters

Found while Priyanka and I were deciding how each enquiry should target one second-order concept ([[second-order-concepts]]). Its framing, that both kinds of knowledge are needed together, is the argument behind [[substantive-and-disciplinary-knowledge]] and our knowledge organisers.

## Caveats

A design study in another system's reform; it describes a design process rather than measuring outcomes. Read from the abstract and introduction only. Licence listed as unknown in Europe PMC.
