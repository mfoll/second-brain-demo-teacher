# Rubric (academic)

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on rubrics: scoring guides with criteria, descriptions of quality at each level and a scoring strategy, used both to mark work and to help pupils plan it. Distinguishes analytic from holistic rubrics.

## Why It Matters

Read while re-marking the 16-mark answers after the [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|December 2025 moderation]]. The Merrow levels mark scheme is a holistic rubric: one best-fit level for the whole answer. Our generosity came from marking it analytically, adding credit for knowledge rather than finding the level first. That distinction went into [[levels-based-mark-scheme]] and the first step of [[mark-and-moderate-year-11-mocks]].

## Caveats

General; says nothing about how exam boards write or apply their schemes.
