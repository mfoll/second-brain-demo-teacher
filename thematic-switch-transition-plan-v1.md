# Thematic Switch Transition Plan v1

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 13 May 2026 by the two-class pilot. Kept for the record of what the department originally planned. Current plan: [[thematic-study-switch]].

Written 2026-02-20 after the 5-1 vote of 4 February ([[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04]]).

## Plan as written

- All five Year 10 classes (10H1 to 10H5) to start Migration, Empire and People in September 2026.
- Five booklets, one per teacher to lead, about 15 hours each, all final by 10 July 2026.
- Specimen papers expected "spring 2026"; mark scheme work to start when they arrive.
- Medicine resources archived after the summer 2027 exams.
- Department training session on the new content on the July INSET day.

## Why it failed

Specimen papers came on 28 April; by 6 May only about 40% of the booklets were drafted and the estimate had to grow by 50 hours or more ([[migration-booklet-check-in-2026-05-06]]). Replaced by the pilot ([[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13]]).
