# Local Oral History

Volunteer work with the Corrandale Local History Society recording the memories of former Corrandale Ropeworks workers. This is outside Ewan's school job, though it feeds the Year 9 enquiry.

## Scope

- Interviewing, consent, the interview log, deposits at the Corrandale Archives Centre and any public use of the recordings.
- The society owns the project; Ewan leads the interviews and the consent paperwork because of his reporting background.
- Not here: lesson planning for the Year 9 enquiry, which sits with [[local-history-enquiry]] in the department.
- Interviewees are consenting adults. Notes record their role at the works, consent form version and what they agreed to, never health or family matters.

## Current State

- [[ropeworks-voices]] has 19 interviews recorded against a target of 25 by June 2027.
- 7 early interviews used the 2019 form, which has no education option. 12 used [[ropeworks-consent-form-v2]]; 11 of those allow education use.
- Re-consent letters went to the 7 earlier interviewees on 5 October 2026; 3 replies so far.
- [[society-listening-website]] is an idea waiting on a hosting decision and on web consent.
