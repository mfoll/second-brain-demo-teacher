# Exam Season 2026 Review

My review of 11H2's year, agreed with Ruth Calder on 27 August 2026.

## Results

72% at grade 4 or above against 66% predicted. Class-level figures in [[results-data-pack-2026]].

## What worked

- Fortnightly timed 16-mark essays from March, with [[whole-class-feedback]] plus one line each. The 16-mark question improved most.
- Starter quizzes from the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]] all year; the Elizabethan section, the most quizzed, was the strongest.
- Strict marking after the [[mock-moderation-2025-12-10|December moderation]]. Painful in January, honest by March.

## What did not

- The informal grades given in December before moderation. Families heard a grade that then dropped.
- Revision sessions faded by April ([[revision-session-attendance-2026]]).
- Medicine 16-mark answers still too often one-sided at the top end; matches the [[merrow-examiners-report-summer-2026]].

## Carried into 2026-27

1. Mark to the protocol from the first mock; report ranges ([[mock-grades-should-be-reported-as-ranges]]).
2. Start timed essays in November, not March.
3. A planned [[revision-programme-2027]] with a calendar.
4. Teach "turning point" judgements explicitly for the thematic study.
