# Teach History Classes

My own classroom: planning, teaching and marking for the classes on my timetable, getting exam groups through the Merrow Certificate in History, and the results that follow. It has no end date; each year brings a new exam class and a new set of mocks.

## Scope

- Lesson planning, marking and feedback for my 2026-27 classes: 7H3, 8H2, 9H4, 10H1 (the migration pilot), 11H1 and the Year 12 group I share with [[ruth-calder]]. The week-by-week picture is in [[teaching-timetable-2026-27]].
- Exam preparation for my Year 11 class, mocks, results analysis and the after-school revision offer.
- My own tools for teaching: the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]] in [[quillyard]], whole-class feedback sheets, booklets I write.
- Exams administration as it touches my classes: entries, access arrangements and mock timetables, worked out with [[sunil-varma]] and [[sian-pritchard]].

## Working Rhythm

The year has a fixed shape: September baseline quiz, November mocks, feedback lessons before Christmas, March mocks, Easter revision, exams from mid-May, results in late August. I plan backwards from the March mocks because they drive predicted grades and, from 2027, whether the department completes the [[thematic-study-switch]].

Marking load is the constraint I manage most. Whole-class feedback freed about an hour and a half per set of books (see [[whole-class-feedback-trial-findings]]), and I spend that on the 16-mark essays, where individual comment still matters.
