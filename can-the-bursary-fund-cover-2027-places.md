# Can The Bursary Fund Cover 2027 Places?

## Why It Matters

The governors' visits committee will approve the [[tarnholm-residential-2027]] on 9 December 2026 only if the bursary position is confirmed ([[elspeth-moran]]). Without bursary places, the trip goes back to being for families who can pay.

## The Numbers

- 2026: 11 funded places at 268 GBP = 2,948 GBP, from the Friends' 1,200 GBP and 1,748 GBP from the school fund.
- 2027 estimate: 289 GBP per pupil ([[tarnholm-2027-provisional-quote]]). 11 places = 3,179 GBP.
- School fund available for trips in 2026-27: 1,974 GBP, including the 226 GBP left over from 2026 ([[olumide-bankole]]).
- Gap: about 1,205 GBP.

## Options

1. Ask the Friends for 1,200 GBP again. On their 10 November agenda ([[chris-delahaye]]).
2. Reduce funded places to 7 and part-fund others. Not preferred; 2026 needed 11.
3. Lower the cost: one coach plus minibus instead of two-driver coach saves about 300 GBP but adds a staff driver.

## Current Answer

Open until 10 November.
