# Exploratory Reading

Where reading goes when it has no Project yet. Some of it turns into lessons or department proposals; most of it just sharpens how I think about the job. Nothing here is a commitment.

## Scope

- Reading clusters held as Topics under this Responsibility: [[public-memory-and-heritage]], [[comparative-judgement]] and [[ai-in-education]].
- Articles, papers and talks I want to keep but that do not yet serve a live Project.
- Not here: reading done for a Project (it belongs to that Project) or for the Middle Leadership Programme ([[professional-development]]).

## Current State

- Public memory and heritage is the busiest cluster, fed by the ropeworks interviews and by [[hollis-granger]]'s seminar at Esterbrook in March 2026. Part of it already moved into the [[local-history-enquiry]].
- Comparative judgement became practical in September 2026, when the AI feedback idea stalled and a comparative judgement trial became the interim plan for the November mocks.
- AI in education is reading only, pending the trust's data protection review.

## How I use it

- A cluster graduates when it gets an outcome I could tick off. Then its Topic moves under that Project and its notes follow.
- I try to write one line in each Source's "Why It Matters" saying what I would do differently because of it. If I cannot, the source probably did not need a note.
- About one evening a fortnight, more in the holidays. Realistically less in exam season.
