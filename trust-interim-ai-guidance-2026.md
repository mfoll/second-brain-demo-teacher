# Trust Interim AI Guidance 2026

The Wyvernmoor Learning Trust's interim guidance for staff on AI tools, issued by Paul Ingram in June 2026 pending the trust DPIA.

## Key Details

- Staff may use trust-approved AI tools for planning and resource drafting, with no pupil personal data.
- No pupil work, names, marks or other personal data in any AI tool until the DPIA concludes and a tool is approved.
- Pupils must not be asked to use AI tools for homework set through school systems.
- Any proposal for a pilot goes to the DPO with a description of data, tool and purpose.

## Why It Matters

The rule my request ran into. Paul's answer of 30 September applied this guidance directly: see [[dpo-ai-tools-decision-2026-09-30]].

## Caveats

"Interim" with no end date except "when the DPIA concludes", expected December 2026.
