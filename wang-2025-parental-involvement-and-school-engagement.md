# Parental Involvement and School Engagement Reduce Adolescent Aggressive Behaviors: A Three Wave Cross-Lagged Model

Wang Z, Chen Y, Su Y, Song Y, Tao Z. *Frontiers in Public Health*, 2025.

## Summary

1,835 early adolescents completed questionnaires at three time points. In a cross-lagged model, parental involvement predicted later school engagement, which in turn was linked to lower aggressive behaviour. The relationship ran both ways: aggressive behaviour also predicted later engagement and parental involvement.

## Why It Matters

Read in January 2026, between the two parents' evening trials, when [[adeel-chaudhry]] asked whether the effort of getting more families to book was worth it. The study is not about parents' evenings, but it supports treating family contact as part of keeping pupils engaged rather than as an administrative event. It informed the stance in [[online-parents-evenings-reach-more-families]] and the decision to keep an in-person block in the [[parents-evening-review]].

## Caveats

- Correlational, self-reported, younger pupils than Year 10, different school system.
- "Parental involvement" here is broad, not school meetings specifically.
