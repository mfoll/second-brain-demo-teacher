# Diverse Curriculum Audit Spreadsheet

The coding sheet for the audit of the 2019 Key Stage 3 schemes: one row per lesson (168), columns for each named individual or group, coded by role, gender, social class, origin and whether they act or are acted upon.

## Summary

- 71% of named individuals were rulers, generals or politicians.
- Women named in 9% of lessons.
- Working people mostly as crowds or casualties.
- 0 lessons with local content.
- Inter-coder agreement on the first 20 lessons about 85%.

## Why It Matters

The evidence behind the nine recommendations in [[diverse-curriculum-audit]]. Recommendation 8 is a re-audit after a year of each new year's units; the same frame will be reused.

## Caveats

Coding is a judgement; 'acts or is acted upon' was the least reliable column.
