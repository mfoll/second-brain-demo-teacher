# Black Death

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes the Black Death as the plague pandemic of 1346 to 1353, one of the deadliest in history, killing up to 50 million people and an estimated 30 to 60% of Europe's population, caused by the bacterium Yersinia pestis and spread mainly by flea bites.

## Why It Matters

Teacher background for Year 7 enquiry 3, 'How far did the Black Death change life for ordinary people?' ([[year-7-enquiry-units]]). The range in the mortality estimate is itself a good lesson starter: why do historians give a range rather than a number?

## Caveats

Pupils should not read it unaided; reading level is well above Year 7. Mortality figures are estimates with wide ranges.
