# Should Year 10 Tutor Groups Be Reshuffled?

## Why It Matters

Pupils at Corrandale keep their tutor group from Year 7 to Year 11. Some heads of year argue for mixing groups at the start of Year 10, when option classes already break up friendship groups, to break up entrenched dynamics in a few groups.

## Arguments

- For: two of the eight groups carried behaviour patterns from Year 9 into Year 10; a reshuffle is a clean start.
- Against: the settling-in survey shows tutor groups are the main source of belonging for many pupils, especially those whose friends chose different options ([[settling-in-survey-findings-2026]]). Changing tutor and group at once removes both anchors.

## Current Answer

Deferred. Not my decision alone; [[adeel-chaudhry]] wants all heads of year to discuss it in summer 2027. Revisit if the February 2027 repeat of the survey shows belonging falling.
