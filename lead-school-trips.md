# Lead School Trips

Leading the Year 10 residential to the [[tarnholm-field-and-heritage-centre]]: approval, risk assessment, costs, bursaries, staffing and parent letters. It repeats every year, so it is a Responsibility with one Project per trip.

## Scope

- The annual Tarnholm residential for Year 10, from first booking to evaluation.
- Not here: day visits run by departments, which go through [[tripplan]] with their own leaders, and the Year 9 local history walk, which belongs to the [[local-history-enquiry]].
- Pupil medical, dietary and consent details live in TripPlan and Ledgerwise. Counts only in this vault.

## Current State

- [[tarnholm-residential-2026]] ran 15 to 17 June 2026: 52 pupils, 6 staff, 268 GBP per pupil, evaluation written. Places were allocated by lottery with a [[behaviour-agreement]] after the attendance threshold was dropped at the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]].
- [[tarnholm-residential-2027]] is provisionally booked for 14 to 16 June 2027 and goes to the governors' visits committee on 9 December 2026 for approval with 50 places.
- Estimated 2027 cost is 289 GBP per pupil, up 21 GBP on 2026, mainly the coach.

## Standing Decisions

- Places are not gated on attendance. See [[should-trip-places-depend-on-attendance]] and [[trip-places-should-not-be-earned-by-attendance]].
- Oversubscribed places go by [[lottery-allocation|lottery]], with a signed behaviour agreement as the condition of a place.
- Staffing at one adult to ten pupils or better, with at least one first aider per activity group ([[supervision-ratio]]).
