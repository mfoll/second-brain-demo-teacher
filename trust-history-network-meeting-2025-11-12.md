# Trust History Network Meeting 2025-11-12

Termly meeting of history leads across the seven Wyvernmoor schools, chaired by [[aled-pryce]]. I went with Priyanka because of the Key Stage 3 assessment item.

## Key Stage 3 assessment

- Four of the seven schools report Key Stage 3 history as a 9 to 1 grade "flight path"; two use descriptors; one uses percentages.
- Aled said the trust would set a common Key Stage 3 data collection calendar, probably three points a year, and asked schools not to build new models until it is published. Timing: "during 2026".
- [[vikram-iyer]] described Kittow's descriptor model and offered to share it.

## Other items

- Moderation: Vikram confirmed the December date for our Year 11 mocks ([[mock-moderation-2025-12-10]]).
- Migration option: two other trust schools are considering it; nobody has taught it yet.

## Consequence for us

We slowed the [[ks3-assessment-model]] and paused it formally in March 2026 ([[faculty-meeting-2026-03-04]]).
