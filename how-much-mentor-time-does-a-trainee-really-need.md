# How Much Mentor Time Does a Trainee Really Need?

The timetable gives one protected period a week. How much time does good mentoring actually take, and where should the rest come from?

## What we know

- Autumn 2025 time log: 3.5 hours a week on mentoring ([[ewan-time-log-autumn-2025]]).
- During the spring 2026 support plan, nearer 5 hours a week for six weeks.
- Autumn 2026 with Yusuf: about 3 hours in September, falling to 2 by October because routines were front-loaded.
- Esterbrook's handbook "expects" one hour of protected time.

## Working answer

About three hours a week in the first half-term and two after, more if a support plan starts. One protected period covers the weekly meeting only; observations, co-planning and reports come from elsewhere.
