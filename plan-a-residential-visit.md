# Plan A Residential Visit

The sequence for planning the Year 10 residential, from first booking to evaluation. Written after the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]] and in use for [[tarnholm-residential-2027]]. Timings count back from a mid-June trip.

## Steps

1. **September to October: book.** Hold dates with the centre ([[tarnholm-field-and-heritage-centre]]). Get a written quote, coach quote and staff availability. Build the budget per pupil with a contingency line, using [[olumide-bankole]]'s template ([[tarnholm-2026-budget]]).
2. **October to November: bursary.** Work out the cost of 11 funded places and how it will be met: school fund, Friends of Corrandale School grant ([[can-the-bursary-fund-cover-2027-places]]).
3. **Late November: TripPlan outline.** Submit purpose, dates, staffing, budget and a draft risk assessment in [[tripplan]]. Allow four weeks for [[rebecca-tull]]'s review.
4. **December or January: governors.** Two-page summary plus risk assessment appendix to the governors' visits committee ([[elspeth-moran]]); deadlines in its [[governors-visits-committee-terms-of-reference|terms of reference]]. Do not tell families before approval.
5. **January: launch and applications.** Letter with cost, payment plan, bursary route, allocation rules. Three-week window, two reminders.
6. **Allocation.** If oversubscribed, [[lottery-allocation|lottery]] drawn with the EVC and an outside witness. Every place conditional on the [[behaviour-agreement]].
7. **February to May: details.** Full risk assessment with the centre's own assessments attached. Medical and dietary forms through TripPlan only. Kit list to families in March. DSL sign-off of safeguarding arrangements ([[fiona-mcrae]]).
8. **May: information evening.** Programme, mine and river controls, phones, contact during the trip.
9. **Trip.** Leader and deputy each carry a trip phone; DSL on call named in the plan; daily message to families through the school office.
10. **Within two weeks: debrief and evaluation.** Staff debrief, pupil survey, written evaluation with money reconciled; leftover contingency to the bursary fund.

## Guardrails

- No attendance threshold for places ([[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]]).
- Pupil medical, dietary and consent details stay in TripPlan and Ledgerwise; this vault holds counts.
- Staffing at one adult to ten or better ([[supervision-ratio]]).
