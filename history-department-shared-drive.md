# History Department Shared Drive

The department's shared drive: the working home of every scheme, booklet, mark scheme and minute. This note says what lives where so I stop searching.

## Summary

- `History/KS3/`: curriculum map (Priyanka's master), one folder per enquiry with booklet, quizzes export, assessed task and teacher overview.
- `History/KS4/`: specification, specimen papers, mark schemes, Medicine and Migration folders, mock papers by series.
- `History/Moderation/`: protocol, double-marking logs, Kittow reports.
- `History/Minutes/`: department minutes since 2019.
- `History/Archive/`: the 2019 schemes and the textbook comparison.

## Why It Matters

Notes in this vault point here rather than copying documents. Pupil data never goes on this drive; aggregates only. Marks for individual pupils stay in Ledgerwise.

## Caveats

Folder names drift when someone saves in a hurry. Priyanka tidies it each July.

## Source Location

School network, staff shared drive, `History/`. Access: history staff and the head of humanities.
