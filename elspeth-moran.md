# Elspeth Moran

Governor at Corrandale School and chair of the governors' visits committee, which approves every residential and overseas visit.

## Role

The committee meets termly and approves residentials after the educational visits coordinator, [[rebecca-tull]], has signed them off in [[tripplan]]. It looks at purpose, cost, access for bursary-eligible pupils and the risk assessment summary.

## How We Work Together

- The 2026 Tarnholm trip was approved at the committee on 21 January 2026. Her questions then were about the cost to families and how places would be allocated; she asked for the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] to be attached to the papers, and it was.
- She prefers a two-page summary with the risk assessment as an appendix rather than the full TripPlan export.
- The 2027 trip goes to the committee on 9 December 2026. She has asked that the papers state the bursary position as confirmed, not hoped for, which is why [[can-the-bursary-fund-cover-2027-places]] has to be settled first.
