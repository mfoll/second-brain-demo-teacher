# Esterbrook University Secondary Placement Handbook 2026-27

The 2026-27 version of the partner school handbook, received on 27 August 2026.

## Summary

Different pattern from last year for first-placement trainees: a 15-week first placement from 7 September to 18 December, then a second school from January. Interim report due at week 10 (13 November for Corrandale). Same placement standards. New section on "early conversations" encouraging mentors to raise concerns in writing by week 4.

The new early-conversations section matches what Ewan concluded after 2025-26. It also gives [[trainee-mentoring-2026-27]] its fixed dates.
