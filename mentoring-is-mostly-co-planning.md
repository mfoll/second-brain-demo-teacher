# Mentoring Is Mostly Co-Planning

## Stance

The most useful thing a subject mentor does is plan lessons with the trainee, not observe them. Observation finds problems; co-planning prevents them and shows how decisions are made.

## Scope

School-based mentoring of trainee teachers in their first and main placements. Less true for an early career teacher in year two, who needs challenge more than modelling.

## Why I Think This

- 2025-26: observations named the Year 9 problem for weeks without fixing it. Weekly co-planning fixed it in six.
- 2026-27: front-loading routines and co-planning one lesson a week from week one has meant no behaviour target so far.
- My own training year: the mentor who planned with me taught me more than the one who wrote long observation notes.

## What Would Change My Mind

A trainee who becomes dependent on co-planning and cannot plan alone by the end of the placement. Watch for it with Yusuf at the interim point.

## Implications

Protect the co-planning slot before the observation slot when time is short.
