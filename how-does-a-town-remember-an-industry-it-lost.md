# How Does A Town Remember An Industry It Lost?

## Question

Corrandale's public story of the ropeworks (proud works, killed by imports) is simpler than what ex-workers say in the interviews. How did that public story form, and is it worth a Year 9 lesson in its own right?

## What I have so far

- The Courier's 1986 coverage leaned on the company's explanation ([[courier-ropeworks-closure-1986]]). Later anniversary pieces in [[joanna-rees]]'s column turned it into a story of loss.
- The canal mural (2011) shows the ropewalk at its 1950s peak, not the closure.
- Interviewees often start with the public story and then complicate it.
