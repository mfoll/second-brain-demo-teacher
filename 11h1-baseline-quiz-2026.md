# 11H1 Baseline Quiz 2026

Results of the 40-question baseline quiz on Year 10 content, sat by 11H1 in the first week of September 2026. Class level only.

## Key Details

- Class average: 54%.
- By topic: Medicine 61%, Weimar 52%, Elizabethan 49%.
- Weakest areas: the Elizabethan religious settlement (38%) and the Weimar recovery years 1924-29 (41%).
- Strongest: Medicine in the 19th century (72%).

Sets the starting point for [[year-11-exam-preparation]] and shapes the target groups in [[11h1-target-groups-autumn-2026]]. Re-run planned for January.
