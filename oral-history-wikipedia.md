# Oral History

English Wikipedia article on oral history.

## Summary

Defines oral history as gathering and studying historical information through planned, recorded interviews with people who took part in or witnessed past events, kept as an aural record. Its value is the perspectives and everyday experience that written records often miss.

## Why It Matters

Ewan's starting point the week after the society launched [[ropeworks-voices]], before he had read any methods literature. Useful for the vocabulary (narrator, interviewer, deposit) and as a pointer to the professional associations' guidance. The pupil version of the method in the [[local-history-enquiry]] borrows its plain definition.
