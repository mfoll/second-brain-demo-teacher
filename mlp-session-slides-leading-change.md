# MLP Session 2 Slides: Leading Change

Slides from the second programme session, 26 November 2025, led by Catriona Dalgleish.

## Summary

Three ideas: start from the problem, not the solution; write down the chain from activity to outcome before starting; decide in advance what evidence would show it is not working. A worked example on homework completion in another trust school.

## Why It Matters

Ewan used the "chain" slide to sketch the attendance drive's logic in December: calls lead to earlier contact, earlier contact to fewer lost days. He then forgot it until Catriona pointed back to it in October 2026. It is now the frame for rewriting section 5 of [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]].
