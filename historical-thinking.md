# Historical Thinking

The disciplinary side of history teaching: causation, change and continuity, significance, evidence, interpretations and chronology. The things pupils do with knowledge, as opposed to the knowledge itself.

## Why it matters here

The [[ks3-curriculum-redesign]] is built on enquiries, each driven by an [[enquiry-question]] that targets one second-order concept. The department's long-running argument, mostly carried by [[desmond-hartigan]], is whether that leaves pupils knowing how to argue but not knowing when anything happened ([[chronological-framework]]).
