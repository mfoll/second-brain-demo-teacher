# Collective memory

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines collective memory as the shared memories and knowledge of a social group, tied to its identity. Traces the phrase to the late nineteenth century and credits Maurice Halbwachs's 1925 work with developing the idea that memory is framed socially.

## Why It Matters

Read two days after [[hollis-granger]]'s seminar ([[esterbrook-public-history-seminar-2026-03-18]]). It gave me words for the gap between the town's public story of the ropeworks and the interviews, the starting point of [[how-does-a-town-remember-an-industry-it-lost]].

## Caveats

Broad survey; the article's examples are national, not local.
