# Should The Whole Department Switch To Migration In 2027?

## Question

From September 2027, should all five Year 10 classes study Migration, Empire and People instead of Medicine Through Time?

## Current Answer

Not decided. The rule agreed on 13 May 2026 ([[history-department-meeting-2026-05-13]]): yes, if the pilot classes' (10H1 and 10H3) March 2027 mock results on the thematic study are at least as good as the Medicine classes'. Decision at the April 2027 department meeting.

## What would decide it

- The March 2027 comparison, done the way agreed beforehand ([[how-do-we-compare-pilot-and-medicine-mock-results]]).
- Whether booklets 3 to 5 are finished and usable by someone who did not write them.
- Desmond's view on whether the comparison was fair.

## Caveats

Two classes, one mock. Small numbers; a difference of a few percentage points means nothing. The decision will also rest on whether the department still wants the change for curriculum reasons ([[thematic-study]]).
