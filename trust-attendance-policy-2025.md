# Trust Attendance Policy 2025

Wyvernmoor Learning Trust attendance policy, effective September 2025. The frame for the [[year-10-attendance-drive]].

## Summary

Defines attendance codes, the 90% [[persistent-absence]] threshold, the school's 96% attendance aim, roles (tutor, head of year, attendance officer, deputy head), and a staged response: first-day contact, a stage 1 letter at 92%, a stage 2 letter and meeting at 90%, then a formal attendance plan.

## Key Details

- Schools may adapt the letter wording but not the stages' thresholds.
- Headteacher can authorise term-time leave only in exceptional circumstances.
- Data reported to the trust half-termly by year group.

## Why It Matters

The stage 2 letter is the one we stopped sending in Year 10 in February 2026 in favour of a meeting offer. [[adeel-chaudhry]] confirmed the policy allows a meeting to replace the letter as long as the meeting is offered in writing.
