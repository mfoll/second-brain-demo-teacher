# SLT Meeting 2025-11-19

Senior leadership meeting, Wednesday 19 November 2025. I attended for the Tarnholm item, about 15 minutes. Present for the item: [[gillian-mortimer]], [[adeel-chaudhry]], [[fiona-mcrae]], [[olumide-bankole]].

## What Happened

I reported that the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]] had 61 applicants for 52 places and asked how to allocate them. [[adeel-chaudhry]] proposed that only pupils with attendance above 95% should be eligible: the trip would be a visible reward in the middle of the Year 10 attendance push, and would stop places going to pupils who were not in school while others attended every day.

Gillian asked what that would do in practice. Nobody knew. I said I suspected it would land hardest on bursary-eligible families and asked for a week to model it with [[marek-nowicki]]. Agreed; item deferred to 3 December.

## Notes

- Adeel's argument was about fairness to pupils who attend, not about punishment. I wrote that down because it was easy to caricature later.
- Olumide noted that with fewer than about 45 pupils the coach cost per pupil would rise.
- Outcome: [[trip-places-equity-analysis]] (1 December), decided at the [[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]].
