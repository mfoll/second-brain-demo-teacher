# Theory of Change

A written chain from what you do to what you expect to change, with each link stated so that it can be checked: if we do A, then B should happen, which should lead to C.

## Attendance drive version

1. Tutors call home on the first day of absence.
2. Families hear from someone who knows the child, the same day.
3. Short absences are less likely to stretch into several days.
4. Fewer pupils drop below 90% attendance.
5. Year-group attendance rises.

## Why it matters for the write-up

Writing the chain shows where the evidence is strong (links 1 and 4: call logs and the persistently absent count) and where it is assumed (links 2 and 3). It also explains why the persistently absent figure moved more than the average: the drive targeted the link that matters for pupils at risk.
