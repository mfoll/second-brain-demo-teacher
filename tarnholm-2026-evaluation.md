# Tarnholm 2026 Evaluation

Evaluation of the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]], written on 26 June 2026 for [[rebecca-tull]], the governors' visits committee and the Friends of Corrandale School. Two pages in the original; this is the vault copy.

## Summary

> [!success]
> 52 pupils and 6 staff, 15 to 17 June 2026. Programme delivered, no significant incidents, 226 GBP under budget. 92% of pupils rated the trip good or very good. 11 places were bursary-funded, 20 places went to pupils who would have been excluded by the attendance threshold that was proposed and dropped.

## Aims and Whether We Met Them

1. **History in place.** Pupils use evidence from a real site to answer an enquiry question about power and work. Met: castle enquiry completed by all groups; follow-up lesson in history classes in the last week of June.
2. **Belonging.** Pupils who are less attached to school have a positive shared experience with the year group. Partly evidenced: in the survey, 41 of 49 agreed "I got to know people in my year I did not know before." No baseline.
3. **Access.** Cost not a barrier. Met: 11 places bursary-funded, deposit waived for bursary families, instalments for everyone.

## Pupil Survey

49 of 52 returned the survey in tutor time on 22 June ([[tarnholm-2026-pupil-survey]]).

- 92% rated the trip good or very good; none rated it poor.
- Highlights: the mine (38 mentions), the campfire before the rain (21), the castle (14).
- Least liked: the food on night 1 (12), the rain (10), the coach journey (8).
- 44 of 49 would recommend it to next year's Year 10.

## Staff View

From the [[tarnholm-debrief-2026-06-24]]: keep the lottery and behaviour agreement, the castle on day 1 and the mine controls. Change the kit list timing, add a quiet room, book the second driver earlier, write the wet-weather plan in advance.

## Caveats

The survey is self-report, taken a week after the trip, in tutor time where pupils could see each other's answers. Attendance figures for trip pupils after the trip are too few and too uncontrolled to say anything about effect ([[year-10-attendance-final-2026-07-17]]).
