# Donna Whitlock

Year 10 pastoral manager at Corrandale School: non-teaching, full-time, based in the Year 10 office. She stays with Year 10 as I do, so she is the continuity between cohorts.

## Role

First contact for Year 10 families during the day, behaviour follow-up, the reintegration room, and the practical side of attendance: home visits with [[marek-nowicki]], meetings with families, and keeping the huddle list current in [[ledgerwise]].

## How We Work Together

- Weekly attendance huddle, Tuesdays 08:10, with Marek ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]). She usually knows the reason behind a pattern before the data shows it.
- She reads every [[year-10-newsletter]] draft for anything that could identify a pupil. She caught a celebration item in Spring 1 2026 that named a club with three members.
- She came on the [[tarnholm-residential-2026]] as the sixth adult, which the families who knew her valued more than I expected.
- She had run Year 10 pastoral for four years before I arrived as head of year, and was candid that first-day calls had been tried before and lapsed. The difference this time was the huddle checking that the calls happened.

## Current Threads
