# Trip Places Should Not Be Earned By Attendance

## Stance

> [!info]
> A residential trip is part of the curriculum offer, not a reward. Places should not depend on attendance, because attendance mostly measures things a pupil cannot control.

## Scope

Residentials and curriculum trips. Rewards days and social events are a closer call; I would still argue against attendance gates there, but less firmly.

## Why I Think This

- The 2025 analysis: a 95% threshold would have excluded 14 of the 17 bursary-eligible applicants and mostly pupils whose absence was authorised illness ([[trip-places-equity-analysis]]).
- The pupils with the weakest attachment to school are the ones a residential might help most. Taking it away from them in the name of attendance works against the attendance goal.
- A lottery with a [[behaviour-agreement]] deals with the real risk (conduct on the trip) and is easy to explain.
- As a reporter I saw how often a rule that sounds neutral lands on the same families. It is worth checking who a rule actually hits before adopting it.

## What Would Change My Mind

Evidence that a threshold raised attendance among pupils just below it without excluding pupils with illness or caring responsibilities. I have not seen any.

## Implications

- I will model any proposed gate before it is adopted, as in December 2025.
- The attendance team uses the trip only as a positive conversation ([[slt-meeting-2025-12-03]]).
