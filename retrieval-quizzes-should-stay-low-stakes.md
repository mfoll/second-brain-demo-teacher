# Retrieval Quizzes Should Stay Low-Stakes

## Stance

Starter quiz results should never be reported to parents, used for predictions, setting or league tables in class. The moment they count, they stop being practice.

## Scope

Starter and homework quizzes from the [[retrieval-quiz-bank]]. Not end-of-unit assessments, which are meant to count.

## Why I Think This

- Under low stakes pupils guess honestly, which shows me what is not secure. In the one term when a colleague put quiz scores on a class leaderboard (spring 2025, Year 8), copying in Quillyard homework quizzes rose noticeably.
- The point of the quiz is the act of recall ([[testing-effect]]), not the score.
- Ruth's condition in February 2025 was the same, and it got the department to use the bank.

## What Would Change My Mind

A senior leadership request for quiz data in the data drop with a clear use that does not reach pupils. Even then I would offer class-level only.

## Implications

Keep pupil-level quiz exports out of Ledgerwise. Say no, politely, when asked to add quiz averages to reports.
