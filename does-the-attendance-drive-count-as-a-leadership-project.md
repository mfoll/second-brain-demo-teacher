# Does the Attendance Drive Count as a Leadership Project?

The MLP project must be a leadership project, not just operational work. The attendance drive was already part of the head of year job. Does it qualify?

## Answer

Yes, agreed by Catriona Dalgleish at the session of 21 January 2026, on the condition that the write-up is about leading the tutor team through a change of routine and judging its impact, not about the attendance tasks themselves.

## Why it was in doubt

The [[mlp-programme-handbook]] asks for a project that involves leading people outside one's line management and has evidence of impact. The drive has both: eight tutors, the pastoral manager and the attendance officer, and weekly figures. The risk was that the write-up would read as a job description.

## Consequence

Shapes section 5 of the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]].
