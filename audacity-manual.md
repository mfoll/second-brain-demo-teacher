# Audacity Manual

The online user manual for Audacity, the free, open-source audio editor, published by the Audacity Team.

## Summary

Reference and tutorials for importing, editing and exporting audio: selecting and cutting regions, fades, noise reduction, normalising levels, labels, and export formats including WAV and MP3.

## Why It Matters

Ewan edits clips for the Year 9 enquiry and any future [[society-listening-website]] in Audacity. He uses the label track to mark timestamps in the one-page summary, and exports access copies only; the WAV masters are never edited.

## Caveats

The manual documents the current release; menu names have changed between versions.
