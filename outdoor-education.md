# Outdoor education

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes outdoor education as structured learning outside, from school camps to multi-day expeditions with activities such as hiking, climbing, canoeing and ropes courses. It draws on experiential and environmental education.

## Why It Matters

Read while making the [[tarnholm-2027-options-comparison]]. The coastal centre on the trust list offers outdoor education in this sense: adventurous activities with learning about self and group. Tarnholm offers history in place with some outdoor elements. Keeping that difference clear is why we stayed with Tarnholm for [[tarnholm-residential-2027]].
