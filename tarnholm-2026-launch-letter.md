# Tarnholm 2026 Launch Letter

Letter to all Year 10 families, 20 October 2025, launching the residential.

## Key Details

- Dates 15 to 17 June 2026; 52 places; 268 GBP with a 50 GBP deposit and three instalments.
- Bursary route described in one paragraph, with the business manager's contact; no need to explain circumstances to the trip leader.
- Application by reply form, closing 14 November 2025.
- Allocation: "if oversubscribed, places will be allocated fairly; details to follow". This vagueness was deliberate, as allocation had not been agreed, and it led to the [[slt-meeting-2025-11-19]] discussion.
