# Year 10 Behaviour Points Summary 2025-26

End-of-year summary of Year 10 behaviour points from [[ledgerwise]], aggregated by half-term and tutor group, prepared with [[donna-whitlock]] on 20 July 2026.

## Key Details

- Positive points: 18,420 across the year, up 12% on the same cohort in Year 9.
- Negative points: 3,960, down 9%. Half of all negative points came from 22 pupils; those pupils were followed up through the Friday review, not here.
- Most common negative category: lateness to lessons after lunch.
- Spring 1 was the peak for negative points, matching the attendance plateau ([[why-did-year-10-attendance-stall-in-the-spring-term]]).
- Two tutor groups accounted for 38% of negative points; both have experienced tutors in 2026-27 ([[year-10-tutor-team-2026-27]]).

## Caveats

Points depend on how often staff award them; a rise in positives partly reflects a push to use them.
