# Tarnholm Behaviour Agreement Form

The one-page form sent to families with each place offer on 12 December 2025, returned signed with the deposit. Approved by [[adeel-chaudhry]].

## Key Details

- Five commitments in plain language (follow instructions, stay with your group, respect the centre, phones in at lights-out, look after each other).
- What happens if they are broken, on the trip and before departure.
- Signed by the pupil and a parent or carer.
- 52 of 52 returned by 16 January 2026.

## Why It Matters

The only condition of a place: [[behaviour-agreement]]. Reused for 2027 with the dates changed.
