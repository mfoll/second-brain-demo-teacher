# Co-Planning

Mentor and trainee plan a lesson together, at the same table, with the mentor thinking aloud about choices: what to cut, where pupils will go wrong, what the first five minutes look like.

## How it differs from feedback

Feedback comes after the lesson and is about what went wrong. Co-planning comes before and shows how an experienced teacher decides. Trainees learn more from watching the decisions than from hearing the verdict.

## How we do it

- One lesson a week, 30 minutes, for the class the trainee finds hardest.
- Start from the end: what should pupils be able to do at the bell?
- Plan the entry routine and the first question word for word.
- Mentor plans out loud; trainee writes the plan. Swap roles after about four weeks.

## Evidence here

In the 2025-26 support plan, weekly co-planning of the Year 9 lessons was the support that made the targets stick. See [[trainee-support-plan-2026-02-09]].
