# Quillyard Quiz Analytics Summer 2025

Class-level export of quiz results from Quillyard, 19 May to 18 July 2025, for the bank's first full half-term in use.

## Key Details

- About 9,400 question attempts across seven classes.
- Median percentage correct per question: 71%.
- 11 questions above 95% correct in every class: too easy, rewritten.
- 7 questions below 30%: four were badly worded, three were genuinely hard and stayed.
- Sequence questions had the widest spread between classes.

## Why It Matters

Our own evidence that a quiz everyone gets right does little. It pushed the rewrite toward [[desirable-difficulty]] and backs the five-question format in [[how-many-retrieval-questions-per-lesson]].

## Caveats

Attempts, not learning. No pupil-level data exported.
