# MLP Session 3: Project Proposals

Middle Leadership Programme session 3, 21 January 2026, trust training centre. 17 of 18 participants.

## What happened

- Each participant presented a one-page project proposal for five minutes in trios, then to Catriona Dalgleish.
- Ewan's proposal on the Year 10 attendance drive was approved: [[mlp-project-proposal-2026-01]].
- Catriona's challenge: the drive was already running, so what is the leadership project, as opposed to the operational work? Answer agreed: leading the tutor team through a change in routine, and the evidence of whether it worked.
- The earlier idea of tutor-time reading was formally dropped: [[mlp-project-idea-tutor-time]].

## Notes

Two other participants are also doing attendance projects in other trust schools. Agreed to share anonymised year-group figures in the spring.
