# Desmond Hartigan

Teacher of History, 31 years in the classroom, 19 of them at Corrandale. The department's most experienced teacher and its most consistent sceptic about enquiry-led Key Stage 3 and about the thematic study switch. Teaches 10H2 (Medicine Through Time) in 2026-27.

## Role

- Teaches across Key Stages 3 and 4; very strong Year 11 results on Medicine Through Time over many years.
- Was the one vote against the switch to Migration on 4 February 2026 ([[history-department-meeting-2026-02-04]]).
- Raised the chronology objection at the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25|curriculum review of 25 June 2025]]: stand-alone enquiries leave pupils without a sense of when things happened. The timeline lessons and the cumulative timeline in the back of every book are the compromise. He accepted it and still says it is not enough.

## How we work together

- He argues in meetings and then teaches the agreed thing properly. I trust his objections because they come from what he has seen in classrooms, not from theory.
- He prefers to talk things through in person after school rather than by email.
- He was right that the migration specimen materials would be late, and I said so in the May meeting.
- Least generous marker of the three of us in the December 2025 moderation, though still above the moderator ([[mock-moderation-2025-12-10]]).

## Current threads

- Whether the cumulative timeline works ([[is-a-cumulative-timeline-enough-for-chronology]]). He has offered to run a chronology quiz with his Year 8 in January 2027.
- His view on the pilot comparison: compare against the same teachers' Medicine results in previous years, not against other classes this year.
