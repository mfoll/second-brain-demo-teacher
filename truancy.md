# Truancy

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines truancy as intentional, unauthorised absence from compulsory education, by the pupil's own choice, as distinct from excused absences such as illness. It notes that schools usually define it in their own policies and that attending school but skipping lessons is sometimes called internal truancy.

## Why It Matters

I went back to the definition while drafting the [[mlp-attendance-project-write-up]] in October 2026, because "persistently absent" kept being read by colleagues as "truanting". Most of the 2025-26 Year 10 caseload was not truancy in this sense: absence was authorised, or the pupil found it hard to come in at all ([[school-refusal]]). The write-up now says so in its first section, and the [[persistent-absence]] note makes the same distinction.

## Caveats

An encyclopedia overview, with examples drawn from several countries' legal systems. Not a source for our policy, which is the [[trust-attendance-policy-2025]].
