# Parents' Evening Survey 2025

Family survey after the first online Year 10 parents' evening, sent 28 November 2025, closed 5 December. Built in Google Forms ([[how-to-use-google-forms]]); results exported as aggregates.

## Key Details

- 118 responses from 209 families.
- Preferred format: video 71%, in person 19%, no preference 10%.
- Slot length: 64% said 5 minutes was too short.
- Technical problems: 14% had at least one.
- Free-text themes (not quoted): no travel or childcare needed; hard to raise a worry in five minutes; want an option to meet in person; booking system confusing for a few families.

## Caveats

Families who did not book were less likely to respond, so this over-represents families who already engage.
