# Tarnholm Staffing Plan v1

> [!warning]
> Superseded in May 2026 by the final staffing recorded in [[tarnholm-residential-2026]]. Kept because it shows why the team grew from five to six.

## The First Plan (10 November 2025)

Five staff for up to 52 pupils, one to 10.4: me, [[jamal-osei]], [[luis-ortega]], [[hannah-greaves]] and one more teacher to be found. Male and female staff on each dormitory corridor, one outdoor first aider (Jamal).

## Why It Changed

- [[rebecca-tull]]'s review of the [[tarnholm-2026-risk-assessment]] asked for a named member of our staff with every underground group of ten, which needed five staff underground plus one on the surface with the alternative group.
- Five staff left nobody free at night if a pupil needed to be collected or taken to hospital.
- [[donna-whitlock]] offered to come; [[tomasz-zielinski]] took the fifth teaching place.

Final: six staff, one to 8.7 ([[supervision-ratio]]).
