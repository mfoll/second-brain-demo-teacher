# Backward design

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes backward design as planning a curriculum by setting the desired learning outcomes first, then deciding how they will be assessed, and only then choosing teaching activities. Typically presented in three stages: identify results, determine evidence, plan learning.

## Why It Matters

The method behind writing the Year 7 enquiries ([[year-7-enquiry-units]]): enquiry question and final assessed task first, lessons last. Now steps 1 to 4 of [[plan-an-enquiry-unit]].

## Caveats

The article is generic; in history the 'desired result' is an argument plus a body of knowledge, and the knowledge part is easy to under-plan if you only start from the task.
