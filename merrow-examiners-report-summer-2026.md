# Merrow Examiners' Report Summer 2026

The board's report on the summer 2026 Merrow Certificate in History papers, released to centres on 2 October 2026.

## Summary

- Medicine 16-mark question: the most common weakness was answers that described change in each period without a judgement on "how far". Strong answers chose a turning point and argued from it.
- Weimar interpretations question: many answers summarised both interpretations without explaining why they differ.
- Elizabethan question on the Armada: well answered; strong knowledge of causes.
- Spelling, punctuation and grammar: most candidates scored 3 or 4 of 4.

## Why It Matters

Confirms what 11H2's results showed in [[exam-season-2026-review]]: our pupils did best where they had practised the judgement step. I added a "turning point" sentence starter to the whole-class feedback sheet for 11H1.

## Caveats

National commentary across all centres; does not tell us how our pupils compared.
