# Corrandale Courier Coverage of the Ropeworks Closure, 1985-86

Seven Corrandale Courier reports from March 1985 to September 1986 on short-time working, the closure announcement and the last shift, read on microfilm at the Archives Centre.

## Summary

The Courier's front page of 23 January 1986 reported the closure announcement with the company's figure of 480 jobs; the union's 510 is in a later paragraph. The last-shift report of 26 September 1986 has a photograph of the ropewalk gates. Coverage is sympathetic to the workers but takes the company's explanation (falling demand, imports) mostly at face value.

## Why It Matters

Ewan worked at the Courier from 2009 to 2019 and knows how a local paper covers a closure: fast, close to official statements, little time for analysis. That makes it a good source for pupils to question alongside the interviews. [[joanna-rees]] has offered the paper's permission to reproduce headlines in school booklets.

## Caveats

Microfilm only; the paper's own archive was damaged in 2004. Some issues from mid-1985 are missing.

## Source Location

Corrandale Archives Centre, Courier microfilm reels for 1985 and 1986.
