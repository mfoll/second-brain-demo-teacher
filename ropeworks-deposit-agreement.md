# Ropeworks Voices Deposit Agreement With Corrandale Archives Centre

Deposit agreement between the Corrandale Local History Society and the Corrandale Archives Centre for the Ropeworks Voices recordings, signed by Maureen Castleford and Alistair Finnie on 17 November 2025.

## Summary

- The society deposits recordings, transcripts and signed consent forms in batches. The archive accessions them as one collection.
- Copyright, assigned to the society by each interviewee, is licensed to the archive for preservation and access copies.
- Access follows each interviewee's consent choices and any closure period. The archive does not publish online; that stays with the society.
- The society may withdraw a recording only if the interviewee withdraws consent.

## Why It Matters

Settled [[who-should-hold-copyright-in-ropeworks-recordings]]. It also makes the archive the keeper of the consent record, which is why re-consent forms must be logged there before the usable count changes.

## Key Details

Formats accepted: WAV at 48 kHz/24-bit masters, transcripts as PDF and DOCX. Archive makes its own access copies.

## Source Location

Signed copy in the society's paperwork folder; archive copy on the collection file.
