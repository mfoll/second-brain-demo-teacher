# Is A Cumulative Timeline Enough For Chronology?

## Question

The compromise from the [[ks3-curriculum-review-2025-06-25|June 2025 review]] gives each year two timeline lessons and a cumulative timeline in pupils' books. Does it give pupils a working [[chronological-framework]] by the end of Key Stage 3?

## Current Answer

Unknown. Desmond's informal July 2026 check says it works within a year and not across years ([[history-department-meeting-2026-07-08]]).

## How we will find out

January 2027: a 20-item ordering and placing test for all Year 8 classes (first cohort with two years of the new curriculum), written by Desmond and me. Half the items are taught events, half are events between enquiries. Compare with the same test given to one Year 10 class that had the old chronological scheme.

## Caveats

Old-scheme comparison group is older and a year further on; any difference in their favour understates the problem.
