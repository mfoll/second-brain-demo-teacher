# Year 12 Shared Group Plan 2026

How Ruth Calder and I split the Year 12 history group in 2026-27: 14 students, five lessons a fortnight.

## Key Details

- Ruth teaches the British period (three lessons a fortnight); I teach the coursework strand and the historiography skills (two).
- Coursework titles agreed by Christmas; I supervise all 14.
- Shared mark book in Ledgerwise; we swap one essay set per half-term to check marking.
- Handover notes in a shared document after each lesson.

## Why It Matters

My first sixth form teaching since 2023. The interviewing and source-checking work I do with [[ropeworks-voices]] feeds the historiography lessons.
