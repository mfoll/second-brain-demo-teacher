# Ropeworks Voices Project Plan 2025

The society's two-page plan for Ropeworks Voices, presented by Maureen Castleford at the launch meeting on 7 April 2025.

## Summary

Aim: record 25 former workers by June 2027, the society's 60th anniversary. Volunteers: Ewan to interview, Nisha Kapoor to transcribe, two committee members to recruit. Budget 640 GBP from society funds for a recorder, microphones, batteries, a backup drive and postage. Output: a deposited collection at the Corrandale Archives Centre and a talk at the anniversary meeting.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for the project's outcome and budget. School use was not in the plan at all; it arrived in the summer of 2025, which is why the consent form had to change.

## Caveats

The plan assumed three interviews a month. Real pace has been about one a month, with gaps in exam season and September.
