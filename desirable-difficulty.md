# Desirable Difficulty

A learning condition that makes practice feel harder and slower now but improves long-term retention: recalling instead of re-reading, spacing practice out, mixing topics. Not every difficulty qualifies; one that stops pupils succeeding at all is just difficulty.

## How I use it

- Starter quizzes mix last lesson, last week and last term, so at least two of five questions should feel hard.
- I rewrote the 11 quiz questions that more than 95% of pupils answered correctly every time ([[quillyard-quiz-analytics-summer-2025]]).
- Pupils are told in September that getting some wrong is the point. Otherwise they read a hard quiz as failure.

## Where it stops being desirable

- Questions failed by most of the class because the wording is poor.
- Recall of content never properly taught.
- Exam-condition essays before the knowledge is secure: that is a test, not practice.
