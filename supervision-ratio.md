# Supervision Ratio

The number of pupils per accompanying adult on a visit. The [[trust-educational-visits-policy]] sets a floor; the trip leader sets the actual ratio from the risk assessment.

## At Corrandale

- Trust floor for residentials with secondary pupils: one adult to fifteen.
- Our standard for Tarnholm: one adult to ten or better, with at least one first aider in every activity group and a mix of staff the pupils know.
- 2026: 6 staff for 52 pupils, one to 8.7. During the mine visit, each group of ten had a centre instructor and one of our staff, so the effective ratio underground was one to five.

## Why Not the Floor

At night in a bunkhouse, the ratio that matters is staff per dormitory corridor, not per pupil. Six staff let us put one adult on each corridor with two free. On the river and underground, the centre's instructors add to our numbers, but they do not know our pupils.

## Costs

Staff places are free at one per ten at Tarnholm; above that each extra adult costs the centre rate. Each extra teacher is also a day of cover, which [[rebecca-tull]] and the cover manager have to find.
