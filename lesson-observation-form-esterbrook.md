# Esterbrook Lesson Observation Form

The one-page observation form mentors use for formal observations: lesson context, a timed narrative column, strengths, one or two targets, and the standards evidenced.

## Summary

No grades. The form asks for "what the pupils were doing" at each timed point, which pushes the observer to watch the class rather than the trainee.

## Why It Matters

Ewan adds a pacing line to the narrative (planned time against actual time for each phase). That line is where Yusuf's pacing target came from: [[trainee-observation-2026-09-24]].
