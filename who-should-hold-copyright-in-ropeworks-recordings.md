# Who Should Hold Copyright in the Ropeworks Recordings?

Who owns the recordings: the interviewee, the interviewer, the society or the archive?

## Answer

Interviewees assign copyright in their words to the society on the consent form; the society licenses the archive to make preservation and access copies under the [[ropeworks-deposit-agreement]] of 17 November 2025. The society alone decides any online publication, within each interviewee's choices.

## Why

- One owner makes later decisions possible. If copyright stayed with each interviewee, every new use would need fresh permission from people who may no longer be able to give it.
- The archive does not want to publish online and said so.
- Ewan, as interviewer, assigns his own rights too. He does not want the project to depend on him.

## Answered on

17 November 2025, when the agreement was signed.
