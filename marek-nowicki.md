# Marek Nowicki

Attendance officer for Corrandale School, covering all year groups.

## Role

Runs the attendance codes and registers in [[ledgerwise]], issues letters under the [[trust-attendance-policy-2025]], makes home visits with pastoral staff, and produces the weekly attendance extract that feeds my [[year-10-attendance-dashboard]].

## How We Work Together

- Weekly Year 10 huddle with me and [[donna-whitlock]] ([[run-the-weekly-attendance-huddle]]). He brings the extract, cut to the pupils whose attendance moved by more than two points in a fortnight.
- He built the "dropping below 92% in four weeks" flag after we asked how to see [[persistent-absence]] coming earlier ([[how-do-we-spot-emerging-persistent-absence-earlier]]).
- Good on codes and careful about what the data can and cannot say: he pointed out that authorised illness made up most of the gap for 15 of the 23 applicants in the [[trip-places-equity-analysis]].
- He covers six year groups and can give Year 10 about an hour a week beyond the huddle; requests go through the huddle, not email.
